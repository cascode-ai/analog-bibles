The idea of the active current mirror can be applied to the output stage of a two-stage op amp as well, that is, the load current source can become a function of the signal. Transistors $M_7$ and $M_8$ operate as active current sources because their currents vary with the signal voltage at nodes Y and X, respectively ($M_9$ with $M_{11}$, and $M_{10}$ with $M_{12}$, form the current-mirror paths).
