The mobility of MOSFETs is in fact a function of the gate–source voltage, expressed as $\mu = \mu_0/[1 + \theta(V_{GS} - V_{TH})]$, where $\theta$ is an empirical factor. For the common-source stage shown in [Fig. 1], the gate voltage is $V_{GS} = V_{GS0} + V_m\cos\omega t$ and the transistor stays in saturation (square law with this mobility; neglect channel-length modulation). Assuming that $\theta(V_{GS}-V_{TH}) \ll 1$ and using the relationship $(1+\epsilon)^{-1} \approx 1 - \epsilon$ for $\epsilon \ll 1$, calculate the third harmonic of the output.
