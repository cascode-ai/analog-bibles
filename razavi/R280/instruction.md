For a MOSFET operating in the subthreshold region, the drain current is
$$I_D = \mu C_d\frac{W}{L}V_T^2\exp\!\left(\frac{V_{GS}-V_{TH}}{V_T}\right)\left[1-\exp\!\left(\frac{-V_{DS}}{V_T}\right)\right],$$
where $C_d$ is the depletion capacitance per unit area and $V_T = kT/q$. If the device is in the deep triode region, $V_{DS} \ll V_T$. Using $\exp(-\epsilon) \approx 1 - \epsilon$, determine the on-resistance.
