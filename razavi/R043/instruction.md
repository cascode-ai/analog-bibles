Assume that the minimum (lateral) dimension $E$ of the S/D areas is $1.5\ \mu$m and that the device is folded as shown in [Fig. 1] (two gate fingers of width $W/2$ share the central drain region; the two outer regions are sources). What is the $f_T$ if the drain current is 1 mA? ($f_T$ is the frequency at which the current gain, with source and drain at ac ground, falls to unity.)

Device data: $C_{ox} = 3.84\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$ ($t_{ox} = 9$ nm), $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$; gate–source and gate–drain overlap capacitance $0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$ each; S/D junction bottom capacitance $C_j = 0.56\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$ and sidewall capacitance $C_{jsw} = 0.35\times10^{-11}$ F/m, with all four sides of each S/D region counted as sidewall. Use the zero-bias junction capacitances.
