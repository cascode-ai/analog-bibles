Suppose the supply voltage of a switched-capacitor amplifier is reduced by a factor of two, and so is the maximum allowable output voltage swing. In order to maintain the dynamic range constant, the noise voltage must scale down by the same factor. The noise is only of $kT/C$ type, and the capacitors in the circuit are scaled accordingly. The amplifier uses a one-stage op amp of transconductance $G_m$, and its small-signal time constant is set by $C/G_m$. How should $G_m$ be scaled to maintain the same small-signal time constant?
