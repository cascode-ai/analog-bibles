# Golden Solution - R683

## Reference answer

Notation: corners FF, SS, FS (fast NMOS, slow PMOS) and SF (slow NMOS, fast PMOS); bias current I fixed.

Summary:
- Gain: set by the NMOS corner alone. Higher at FF and FS, lower at SS and SF; the PMOS corner hardly matters.
- Input-referred noise: lowest at FS, highest at SF; in between, FF below SS when the NMOS and PMOS shifts are comparable.

Gain:
- $A_v = -g_{m1}(r_{O1}\|r_{O2})$.
- At constant current, $r_{O1}$ and $r_{O2} = 1/(\lambda I)$ do not change (λ is not affected by $\mu C_{ox}$), while $g_{m1} = \sqrt{2\mu_nC_{ox}(W/L)_1I} \propto \sqrt{\mu_nC_{ox}}$. So $|A_v| \propto \sqrt{\mu_nC_{ox}}$.
- FF and FS: higher gain (by the square root of the NMOS $\mu C_{ox}$ ratio). SS and SF: lower gain.

Input-referred thermal noise:
- $\overline{v_{n,in}^2} = \dfrac{4kT\gamma}{g_{m1}}\left(1 + \dfrac{g_{m2}}{g_{m1}}\right)$, with $g_{m2} = \sqrt{2\mu_pC_{ox}(W/L)_2I}$ the transconductance of the current-source device.
- FF: $g_{m1}$ and $g_{m2}$ both rise by the same factor, so the noise power falls ∝ $1/\sqrt{\mu C_{ox}}$. SS: the noise rises.
- FS: lowest ($g_{m1}$ up, $g_{m2}/g_{m1}$ down). SF: highest.
- Ordering: FS is always the lowest and SF always the highest. If the NMOS and PMOS $\mu C_{ox}$ shifts are comparable (the same fractional change), FS < FF < nominal < SS < SF; otherwise the order of FF and SS depends on the relative shifts and on $g_{m2}/g_{m1}$. The PMOS corner affects the noise only through the $g_{m2}/g_{m1}$ term.

Note: to keep the current constant, $V_b$ must track the corner (e.g., generated by a current mirror).

## Grading notes

- +2: gain ∝ $\sqrt{\mu_nC_{ox}}$, with only the NMOS corner mattering (FF and FS higher, SS and SF lower) and $r_O$ unchanged at constant current; noise with FS lowest and SF highest and the PMOS contribution via $g_{m2}/g_{m1}$. For FF versus SS, either FF < SS (stated for comparable shifts) or a statement that their order depends on the relative shifts is accepted.
- +1: gain right, with a noise ranking that has one corner misplaced or omits the PMOS contribution.
- 0: only one of gain and noise right.
- -2 (asserted as fact): gain claimed to depend on the PMOS corner as a ratio (as for a diode load), $r_O$ claimed to change with $\mu C_{ox}$ at constant current, or FS and SF swapped in the noise.
