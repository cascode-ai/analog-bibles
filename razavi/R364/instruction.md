A common-gate stage incorporates an NMOS device with $W/L = 50/0.5$ biased at $I_D = 1$ mA and a load resistor of 1 kΩ. Calculate the input-referred thermal noise voltage and current. Use $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $T = 300$ K, channel thermal noise $4kT\gamma g_m$ with $\gamma = 2/3$; neglect channel-length modulation, body effect, the noise of the bias-current source and capacitances.
