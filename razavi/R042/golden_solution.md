# Golden Solution - R042

## Reference answer

$f_{T,sub} = \dfrac{g_m}{2\pi C_{in}}$ with $g_m = \dfrac{I_D}{\zeta V_T}$, i.e. $f_{T,sub} = \dfrac{I_D}{2\pi\zeta V_T C_{in}} \propto I_D$. It is exponential in $V_{GS}$, dropping tenfold for every $\zeta V_T\ln 10$ of gate voltage (≈ 90 mV for ζ = 1.5), and far below the strong-inversion value.
- Gate capacitance in weak inversion: there is no strong inversion layer to shield the bulk, so $C_{GS} \approx C_{GD} \approx WC_{ov}$ (overlap only) and the gate couples to the bulk through $C_{ox}$ in series with the depletion capacitance: $C_{GB} = \dfrac{WLC_{ox}C_{dep}}{C_{ox} + C_{dep}} = WLC_{ox}\dfrac{\zeta - 1}{\zeta}$ (using $\zeta \approx 1 + C_{dep}/C_{ox}$). With the bulk at ac ground, $C_{in} \approx WLC_{ox}(\zeta-1)/\zeta + 2WC_{ov}$, so $f_{T,sub} \approx \dfrac{I_D}{2\pi(\zeta - 1)V_TWLC_{ox}}$ when the overlap is neglected. (Simply using $C_{in} = C_{GS} + C_{GD}$ of order $WLC_{ox}$ gives the same conclusion.)
- Comparison, for the same device (same W and L) biased in the two regions: in strong inversion $f_T \propto V_{GS} - V_{TH} \propto \sqrt{I_D}$; in subthreshold $f_T \propto I_D$, and $I_D$ is exponentially small. At the weak/strong boundary ($V_{GS} - V_{TH} \approx 2\zeta V_T \approx 80$ mV) the two are of the same order, i.e. comparable to the strong-inversion $f_T$ at only ~80 mV of overdrive, several times below the $f_T$ at typical overdrives of 0.2–0.5 V. Below threshold, $f_T$ falls a decade per $\zeta V_T\ln 10$. Subthreshold operation gives high $g_m/I_D$ but low speed.

## Grading notes

A ratio $f_{T,sub}/f_{T,SI} = (V_{GS} - V_{TH})/(2\zeta V_T)$ stated explicitly for equal drain current and equal gate capacitance is algebraically correct and not a false claim, but it misses the intended same-device comparison: at most +1 unless the answer also concludes that the subthreshold $f_T$ of a given device is much lower.
