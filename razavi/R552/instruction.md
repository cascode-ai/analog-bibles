A differential pair with resistive loads $R_D$ is designed such that its voltage gain, $g_mR_D$, has a zero temperature coefficient at room temperature ($T = 300$ K). If only the temperature dependence of the mobility is considered ($\mu \propto T^{-3/2}$; $R_D$, $C_{ox}$ and $W/L$ temperature-independent; square-law devices), determine the required temperature behavior of the tail current. Design a circuit that roughly approximates this behavior.
