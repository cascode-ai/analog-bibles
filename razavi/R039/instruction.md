The transit frequency, $f_T$, of a MOSFET is defined as the frequency at which the small-signal current gain of the device drops to unity while the source and drain terminals are held at ac ground. Prove that
$$f_T = \frac{g_m}{2\pi(C_{GD} + C_{GS})}.$$
Note that $f_T$ does not include the effect of the S/D junction capacitance.
