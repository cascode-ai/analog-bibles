For power-conscious applications, a figure of merit is defined as the transconductance of devices normalized to their bias current, $g_m/I_D$. Determine this quantity for long-channel devices operating in strong inversion (square-law model) or in the subthreshold region. At what drain current are these two equal? Express the results in terms of $\mu C_{ox}$, $W/L$, the overdrive or the drain current, the thermal voltage $V_T = kT/q$, and the subthreshold slope factor $\zeta$ (in subthreshold, $I_D \propto \exp[V_{GS}/(\zeta V_T)]$).
