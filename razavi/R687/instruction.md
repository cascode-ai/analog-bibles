In a CMOS process, the threshold-adjust implant is performed after the gate oxide is grown. It is intended to place a thin sheet of p-type dopants just below the silicon surface, which shifts the thresholds of both the NMOS and the PMOS transistors in the positive direction: without the implant $V_{THN} \approx 0$ and $V_{THP} \approx -1$ V, and the targets are $V_{THN} = 0.7$ V and $V_{THP} = -0.8$ V. During this threshold-adjust implant, the wafer was not tilted, which led to severe channeling. Explain whether the resulting threshold voltages are higher or lower than the target values.
