A metal-4 wire with a length of 1,000 μm and a width of 2 μm is driven by a source impedance of 500 Ω. Metal 4 has a sheet resistance of 40 mΩ/□, a parallel-plate (bottom-plate) capacitance to the substrate of 7.0 aF/μm², and a fringe capacitance (both sides together) of 30 aF/μm. Calculate the delay through the wire and compare the result with the lumped time constant obtained by multiplying the source impedance by the total wire capacitance.
