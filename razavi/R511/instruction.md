It is possible to raise the voltage gain of a folded-cascode op amp by adding a secondary path. As shown in [Fig. 1] by the gray section, the input signal can also travel through a differential pair with current-source loads, $I_1$ and $I_2$, and drive the current sources $M_7$ and $M_8$ in the original op amp. Call the gray input transistors $M_A$ (gate tied to the gate of $M_1$, drain at X) and $M_B$ (gate tied to the gate of $M_2$, drain at Y); each has transconductance $g_{mA}$ and output resistance $r_{OA}$; channel-length modulation in $I_1$ and $I_2$ is neglected, and so is body effect. Considering only the capacitances at X, Y, P, Q, and the output nodes, compute the overall transfer function. Is it possible for the zero to cancel one of the poles?
