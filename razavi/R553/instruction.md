A differential pair with resistive loads $R_D$ has a small-signal voltage gain $g_mR_D$. Its tail current is constant (temperature-independent), but the load resistors exhibit a finite temperature coefficient. If only the temperature dependence of the mobility is considered ($\mu \propto T^{-3/2}$; square-law devices), what resistor temperature coefficient cancels the variation of the mobility, so that $g_mR_D$ has a zero TC at room temperature ($T = 300$ K)?
