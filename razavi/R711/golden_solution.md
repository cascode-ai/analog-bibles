# Golden Solution - R711

## Reference answer

$C$ = 84 aF/μm², $C_P$ = 30 aF/μm², $C_P/C$ ≈ 0.36.

- Interlayer capacitance between layers i and j: $1/(1/C_{sub,i} - 1/C_{sub,j})$ (heights $h = \epsilon/C_{sub}$).
- Metal 4–3: $1/(1/7 - 1/9) = 31.5$; metal 3–2: $1/(1/9 - 1/15) = 22.5$; metal 2–1: $1/(1/15 - 1/30) = 30$ aF/μm².
- $C = 31.5 + 22.5 + 30 = 84$ aF/μm².
- $C_P$ = metal 1 to substrate = 30 aF/μm².
- $C_P/C = 30/84 = 0.357$.
