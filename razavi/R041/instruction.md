The transit frequency of a MOSFET is $f_T = g_m/[2\pi(C_{GD} + C_{GS})]$ (the frequency at which its current gain, with source and drain at ac ground, falls to unity). For a given bias current, the minimum allowable drain–source voltage for operation in saturation can be reduced only by increasing the width and hence the capacitances of the transistor. Using square-law characteristics, prove that
$$f_T = \frac{\mu_n}{2\pi}\,\frac{V_{GS} - V_{TH}}{L^2}.$$
This relation indicates how the speed is limited as a device is designed to operate with lower supply voltages.
