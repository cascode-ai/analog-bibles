## Reference answer

With $W/L$ (β) fixed:
- $g_m = \beta^{1/3}(3I_D)^{2/3} \propto I_D^{2/3}$ starts at the origin with infinite slope and is concave. It grows faster than the square law's $\sqrt{I_D}$: doubling $I_D$ raises $g_m$ by 1.59 times instead of 1.41.
- $V_{GS}-V_{TH} = (3I_D/\beta)^{1/3} \propto I_D^{1/3}$ rises steeply at first and then flattens, more strongly than the square law's $\sqrt{I_D}$: 8 times the current doubles the overdrive, instead of 4 times.
