At a constant collector current, the temperature coefficient of the base–emitter voltage of a bipolar transistor is
$\dfrac{\partial V_{BE}}{\partial T} = \dfrac{V_{BE} - (4 + m)V_T - E_g/q}{T}$,
where $V_T = kT/q$, $E_g \approx 1.12$ eV is the bandgap energy of silicon and $m \approx -3/2$ is the temperature exponent of the minority-carrier mobility ($\mu \propto T^m$).
