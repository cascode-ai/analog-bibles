# Golden Solution - R581

## Reference answer

$M_2$ produces no net error. The error comes from $M_1$'s turn-off and builds up over the cycles to its full value:
$$\Delta V_{out} \to -\Delta V_1,\qquad \Delta V_1 \approx \frac{\alpha\,WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_{in} - V_{TH}) + WC_{ov}V_{DD}}{C},$$
where α is the fraction of $M_1$'s channel charge that goes to $C_1$ (worst case 1, or ½ for an equal split). The error is largest at the lowest input ($V_{in} = 0$, $V_{TH} = V_{TH0}$): $|\Delta V_{out}|_{max} \approx [WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_{TH0}) + WC_{ov}V_{DD}]/C = (2.3\ \mathrm{V}\cdot WLC_{ox} + 3\ \mathrm{V}\cdot WC_{ov})/C$ (all charge to $C_1$), or $(1.15\ \mathrm{V}\cdot WLC_{ox} + 3\ \mathrm{V}\cdot WC_{ov})/C$ with half.

- $M_1$ turning off (CK falling) dumps α of its channel charge $WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_{in} - V_{TH})$ onto $C_1$ and couples $-V_{DD}WC_{ov}/(C + WC_{ov})$ through its overlap. So $V_{C1} = V_{in} - \Delta V_1$: an input-dependent error (gain error, offset, and nonlinearity through body effect).
- $M_2$: when $\overline{CK}$ rises, $M_2$ forms its channel by drawing $q_2 = WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_x - V_{TH})$ from the merged $C_1$–$C_2$ node, and its rising gate couples charge through both overlaps. When it turns off at the same $V_x$, it returns $q_2$, half to each capacitor, and the falling gate couples the same overlap charge back. With $C_1 = C_2$, equal overlaps and an equal split, charge conservation gives $V_{1f} + V_{2f} = V_1 + V_2$ and $V_{1f} = V_{2f}$, so $M_2$ adds no net error.
- Charge sharing: $V_{out}[k] = (V_{in} - \Delta V_1 + V_{out}[k-1])/2$. The output error is $\Delta V_1/2$ after the first transfer and approaches $\Delta V_1$ after many cycles; it never exceeds $\Delta V_1$.
- If the charge $M_2$ absorbs at turn-on is ignored, its turn-off would appear to add a pedestal $-[WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_{out} - V_{TH})/2 + WC_{ov}V_{DD}]/C$ on $C_2$. That pedestal is cancelled by the turn-on.
- Phase dependence: the value above is the held output (after $M_2$ turns off), which is the largest error in the cycle. While $M_2$ is on, $V_{out}$ is higher by $[q_2/2 + WC_{ov}V_{DD}]/C$, i.e. $V_{out,on} \approx V_{in} + (\tfrac12 - \alpha)WLC_{ox}(V_{DD} - V_{in} - V_{TH})/C$; with α = ½ the on-phase output equals $V_{in}$ while the held value is still $V_{in} - \Delta V_1$.
- Second order: the two nodes do not carry equal parasitic loading ($C + 2WC_{ov}$ at the $C_1$ node, $C + WC_{ov}$ at $V_{out}$), so the cancellation of $M_2$ and the denominators are exact only for $WC_{ov} \ll C$. A lumped analysis that keeps them, e.g. $|\Delta V_{out}| = [q_1/2 + WC_{ov}V_{DD}]/(C + WC_{ov})$ for α = ½, is equivalent to first order.

## Grading notes

+2: the error attributed to $M_1$'s input-dependent charge injection plus its clock feedthrough, propagated to $V_{out}$ (full value in steady state, i.e. the held value); $M_2$'s net contribution recognized as zero (or correctly shown to cancel, including an analysis that finds $V_{out} = V_{in}$ while $M_2$ is on and the error $-(q_1/2 + WC_{ov}V_{DD})/C$ on the held value with α = ½: that is the reference result, not a claim that all errors cancel); an expression in $V_{in}$ equivalent to the reference, with α = 1 or α = ½ stated. The question asks for the result as a function of $V_{in}$, so placing the worst case at $V_{in} = 0$ is a welcome extra, not required for +2. Denominators $C + WC_{ov}$ (or similar parasitic loading) are acceptable. +1: $M_1$'s term right (any stated α), but $M_2$'s turn-off injection and feedthrough counted as a net pedestal on $C_2$ without accounting for the charge $M_2$ draws at turn-on (e.g. a steady-state error $3\times(q/2 + WC_{ov}V_{DD})/C$ from the recursion $V_{n+1} = (V_{in} - a + V_n)/2 - a$); this is +1, not -2. 0: only the first-transfer value ($\Delta V_1/2$) given as the maximum, or $M_1$'s injection without its feedthrough. -2 (asserted as fact): $M_1$'s error said to be independent of $V_{in}$, all errors claimed to cancel (zero total error), a positive pedestal from NMOS charge injection, or all of $M_2$'s channel charge put onto $C_2$ contrary to the stated equal split.
