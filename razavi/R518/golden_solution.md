# Golden Solution - R518

## Reference answer

Both are straight lines through the origin, the same shapes as for the square law. With $\beta = kW/L$: $g_m = k(W/L)(V_{GS}-V_{TH})^2 \propto W/L$ and $I_D = (k/3)(W/L)(V_{GS}-V_{TH})^3 \propto W/L$. Their ratio is fixed at $g_m/I_D = 3/(V_{GS}-V_{TH})$ (versus $2/(V_{GS}-V_{TH})$ for the square law). Linear scaling of both with $W$ at constant overdrive holds for any device law, because widening the device at a fixed overdrive is equivalent to putting identical devices in parallel.
