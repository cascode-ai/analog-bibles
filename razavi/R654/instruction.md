A VCO used in a type I PLL exhibits nonlinearity in its input-output characteristic, i.e., $K_{VCO}$ varies across the tuning range. The PLL consists of a phase detector with gain $K_{PD}$, a first-order low-pass filter $1/(1 + s/\omega_{LPF})$, and the VCO with gain $K_{VCO}$, in a unity-feedback loop. If the damping ratio must remain between 1 and 1.5, how much variation can be tolerated in $K_{VCO}$?
