[Fig. 1] and [Fig. 2] show two representations of the thermal noise of a MOSFET $M_1$ whose source is degenerated by an impedance $Z_S$ and whose drain is shorted to ac ground: in [Fig. 1] the noise is a current $I_n$ between drain and source and the gate is at ac ground; in [Fig. 2] the noise is a voltage $V_n$ in series with the gate. $I_{n,out1}$ and $I_{n,out2}$ are the resulting currents through the drain short. At low frequencies (no capacitances), $I_{n,out1} = I_n/[Z_S(g_m + g_{mb} + 1/r_O) + 1]$ and $I_{n,out2} = g_mV_n/[Z_S(g_m + g_{mb} + 1/r_O) + 1]$, so the two are equivalent for any $Z_S$ if $V_n = I_n/g_m$ (the "lemma"). Now include the gate–source capacitance $C_{GS}$ and a noiseless impedance $Z_1$ in series with the gate (between the gate and ac ground in [Fig. 1]; in series with $V_n$ in [Fig. 2]). Derive expressions for $I_{n,out1}$ and $I_{n,out2}$, including $g_m$, $g_{mb}$ and $r_O$. Does the lemma hold in this case?
