Determine the transfer function, for differential signals, of a differential pair with current-source loads: NMOS input transistors $M_1$, $M_2$ with an ideal tail current source, and PMOS current-source loads $M_3$, $M_4$ whose gates are held at a fixed bias. Each input is driven through a series resistance $R_S$; the output is taken differentially between the drains. Include channel-length modulation ($r_{O1} = r_{O2}$, $r_{O3} = r_{O4}$), the gate–source and gate–drain capacitances $C_{GS1}$, $C_{GD1}$ of the input transistors, and lump all capacitance from each output node to ground (drain junction capacitances of $M_1$ and $M_3$, gate–drain capacitance of $M_3$, and any load) into $C_L$. Neglect the body effect.
