[Fig. 1] depicts a transimpedance amplifier employing voltage–current feedback. Assume that $I_1$–$I_3$ are ideal, $I_1 = I_2 = 1$ mA, $I_3 = 10\ \mu$A, $(W/L)_{1,2} = 50/0.5$, and $(W/L)_3 = 5/0.5$; $V_{DD} = 3$ V. The output node is loaded by a 0.5-pF capacitor, and the circuit is compensated by a capacitor $C_C = 9$ pF connected between the gate and the drain of $M_1$ (loop analyzed by breaking it at the gate of $M_3$). What resistance must be placed in series with $C_C$ to position the zero introduced by the compensation network atop the first nondominant pole?
