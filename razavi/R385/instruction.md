Calculate the input-referred thermal and flicker noise voltage of the circuit shown in [Fig. 1], where the input is the differential voltage between the gates of $M_1$ and $M_2$ and the output of interest is the current difference $I_{D3} - I_{D4}$. The PMOS $M_3$ (source at $V_{DD}$, gate at $M_1$'s drain, drain at $M_1$'s source) and $M_4$ (likewise for $M_2$) close local feedback loops; $R_S$ connects the two source nodes. The four current sources are realized by MOSFETs: the upper ones by PMOS devices $M_5$ (feeding $M_1$'s drain) and $M_6$ (feeding $M_2$'s drain), the lower ones by NMOS devices $M_7$ (at $M_1$'s source) and $M_8$ (at $M_2$'s source), all saturated with fixed gate biases. Neglect channel-length modulation and body effect; the circuit is symmetric ($g_{m5} = g_{m6}$, $g_{m7} = g_{m8}$). Model channel thermal noise as $4kT\gamma g_m$, the noise of $R_S$ as $4kT/R_S$, and flicker noise as a gate voltage $K/(C_{ox}WL\,f)$ ($K_N$ for NMOS, $K_P$ for PMOS).
