A two-stage op amp contains a nondominant pole, $\omega_{p2}$, at its output and was compensated for a phase margin of 45° in a feedback loop with feedback factor β, so that $|\beta H|$ drops to unity at $\omega_{p2}$ ($H$ being the op amp's open-loop transfer function); the dominant pole is much lower than $\omega_{p2}$. The load capacitance seen at the output is then doubled. How should the compensation be modified to ensure that the phase margin is 45° again?
