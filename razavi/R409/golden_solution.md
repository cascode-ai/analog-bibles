# Golden Solution - R409

## Reference answer

$A_v \approx +1.55$ and $R_{out} \approx 0.17\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ (exact small-signal analysis with λ and body effect: 1.554, 166 Ω). Without body effect: ≈ 1.77, ≈ 0.17 kΩ.

- Bias: $V_S = V_{out} = 1.5$ V (no current in $R_F$), so $V_{SB1} = 1.5$ V. $g_{m1} = 3.66$ mS, $g_{mb1} = g_{m1}\gamma/(2\sqrt{0.9 + 1.5}) = 0.145g_{m1} = 0.53$ mS, $g_{m3} = 1.96$ mS, $r_{O1} = 20\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$, $r_{O2} = r_{O3} = 10\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$.
- Series–shunt feedback: $R_F$–$R_{S1}$ sense $V_{out}$ and return $\beta = 0.5$ of it to the source of $M_1$.
- Open loop (two-port loading): $M_1$ degenerated by $R_{S1}\|R_F = 1.5\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$: $G_{m1} = g_{m1}/[1 + (g_{m1}+g_{mb1})1.5\ \mathrm{k\Omega}] = 0.50$ mS; node X: $r_{O2}$ in parallel with ≈ 147 kΩ looking into the degenerated $M_1$ ≈ 9.4 kΩ → $A_1 \approx -4.7$; $M_3$ load $R_{D2}\|(R_F+R_{S1})\|r_{O3} = 1.67\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ → $A_2 = -3.27$; $A_{v,open} \approx 15.2$.
- The fed-back voltage acts on the source with weight $g_{m1}+g_{mb1}$, so the loop gain is $\beta(1+\eta)A_{v,open} \approx 0.5 \times 1.145 \times 15.2 \approx 8.7$ ($\eta = g_{mb1}/g_{m1}$), and $A_v \approx 15.2/9.7 \approx 1.57$ (exact 1.554; for infinite loop gain the limit would be $(1/\beta)/(1+\eta) = 1.75$, not 2). $R_{out} \approx 1.67\ \mathrm{k\Omega}/9.7 \approx 0.17\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ (two-port), 0.166 kΩ exact. A plain two-port $A/(1+\beta A)$ with $g_{mb1}$ only in the degeneration gives ≈ 1.77 and ≈ 0.19 kΩ, close to the no-body-effect values.
- λ of the current source $M_2$ is essential: with $r_{O2} = \infty$ the open-loop gain would be infinite.
