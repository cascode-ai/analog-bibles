The zero of a transfer function $V_{out}/V_{in}$ can be found as the complex frequency $s_z$ at which $V_{out}$ vanishes for a nonzero input: short the output node to ac ground and find the value of $s$ at which no current flows into that short. Apply this method to the circuit shown in [Fig. 1] to determine the zero of $V_{out}/V_{in}$.

The circuit is a small-signal model of a differential pair with an active current mirror: the diode-connected PMOS $M_3$ is represented by the resistance $1/g_{mP}$ from $V_{DD}$ to the mirror node E; $C_E$ is the total capacitance at E; $M_4$ (gate at E) has transconductance $g_{mP}$, its output resistance being included in $r_{OP}$; the input transistors and $V_{in}$ are replaced by the Thevenin equivalent $V_X = g_{mN}r_{ON}V_{in}$ with $R_X = 2r_{ON}$; $C_L$ and $r_{OP}$ load the output.
