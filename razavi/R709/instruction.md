A capacitor is built in a four-metal process from metal 4 (top plate) and metal 3 (bottom plate), with no other conductive layer underneath. The parallel-plate capacitance of each layer to the substrate (with nothing in between) is 7.0 aF/μm² for metal 4 and 9.0 aF/μm² for metal 3. Assume a single uniform dielectric, neglect the thickness of the conductive layers, and neglect fringe capacitances. Calculate, per unit area, the capacitance $C$ between the two terminals and the parasitic capacitance $C_P$ from the bottom plate to the substrate, and the ratio $C_P/C$.
