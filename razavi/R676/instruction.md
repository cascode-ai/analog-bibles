A compact model of a MOSFET in saturation that includes mobility degradation by both the vertical field (fitting parameter θ) and the lateral field (velocity saturation; saturated velocity $v_{sat}$, low-field mobility $\mu_0$) gives
$$I_D = \frac12\mu_0C_{ox}\frac{W}{L}\,\frac{(V_{GS}-V_{TH})^2}{1 + \left(\dfrac{\mu_0}{2v_{sat}L} + \theta\right)(V_{GS}-V_{TH})} .$$
Using this equation, calculate the transconductance of the MOSFET. Prove that
$$g_m = \frac{I_D}{V_{GS}-V_{TH}}\left[1 + \frac{1}{1 + \left(\dfrac{\mu_0}{2v_{sat}L} + \theta\right)(V_{GS}-V_{TH})}\right].$$
