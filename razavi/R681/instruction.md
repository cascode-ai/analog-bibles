In a MOSFET simulation model, the bandgap energy of silicon varies with temperature according to the empirical relation
$$E_g = 1.16 - \frac{7.02\times10^{-4}\,T^2}{T + 1108}$$
($E_g$ in eV, T in kelvin). Determine $\partial E_g/\partial T$ at room temperature (T = 300 K) and explain how it affects bandgap reference voltages.
