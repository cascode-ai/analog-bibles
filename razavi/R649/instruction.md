[Fig. 1] shows a reference clock $V_{CK}$ and the output $V_{out}$ (also called $V_{VCO}$) of a VCO controlled by $V_{cont}$. Before $t = t_1$ both waveforms have the same frequency, but the rising edges of $V_{out}$ lag those of $V_{CK}$ by a fixed skew. At $t = t_1$, $V_{cont}$ is stepped up, so the VCO runs faster and gradually catches up. At $t = t_2$ the phase error has dropped to zero and $V_{cont}$ returns to its original value; after that the two waveforms remain aligned.
