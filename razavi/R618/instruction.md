The input devices of a differential pair have an effective channel length of 0.5 μm. Assuming that their threshold mismatch is $\Delta V_{TH} = 0.1\,t_{ox}/\sqrt{WL}$, with $\Delta V_{TH}$ in millivolts, $t_{ox}$ in ångströms ($t_{ox}$ = 90 Å) and $W$, $L$ in micrometers (that is, $\Delta V_{TH} = 9\ \mathrm{mV\cdot\mu m}/\sqrt{WL}$), and neglecting other mismatches, determine the minimum width of the transistors such that $V_{OS} \le 2$ mV. Compare the result with the minimum width required for $V_{OS} \le 5$ mV.
