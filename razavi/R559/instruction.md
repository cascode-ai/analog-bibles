[Fig. 1] shows a "single-junction" bandgap circuit. $I_1$ and $I_2$ are current sources from $V_{DD}$; $Q_1$ is a pnp transistor with its emitter at $X$ and its base and collector grounded (saturation current $I_S$); the op amp is ideal; switches $S_1$ and $S_2$ are driven by complementary clocks. The circuit first settles with $S_1$ on and $S_2$ off; then $S_1$ turns off and $S_2$ turns on. The ratio $I_2/I_1$ is nominally $r$, but deviates from it by a small error $\epsilon$: $I_2/I_1 = r + \epsilon$ ($I_1$ is accurate). Calculate $V_{out}$ when $S_1$ is off. Neglect base current and switch charge injection; $V_T = kT/q$.
