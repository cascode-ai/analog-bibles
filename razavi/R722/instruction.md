In order to reduce the total bond-wire inductance, a package uses 4 supply pads and 4 ground pads (one bond wire each). The self-inductance of each wire is 4 nH and the mutual inductance between adjacent wires is 2 nH; neglect the mutual inductance between nonadjacent wires, and assume the current divides equally among the four wires of each connection. Calculate the equivalent inductance of the supply and ground connections if every supply wire is placed next to a ground wire (the eight wires lie in one row and alternate: supply, ground, supply, ground, …).
