Determine the input-referred thermal and $1/f$ noise voltages of the two degenerated differential pairs shown in [Fig. 1] and [Fig. 2], and compare the results. In [Fig. 1], the sources of $M_1$ and $M_2$ are joined through two resistors $R_S$ to a common node biased by a single tail transistor $M_3$; in [Fig. 2], each source has its own current-source transistor ($M_3$ for $M_1$, $M_4$ for $M_2$) and the sources are connected by a resistor $2R_S$. Both use load resistors $R_1$, differential input between the gates and differential output between the drains. Assume the circuits draw equal supply currents (so the input transistors are biased identically; $M_3$ in [Fig. 1] carries twice the current of each of $M_3$, $M_4$ in [Fig. 2]), perfect matching, and neglect channel-length modulation and body effect. Model channel thermal noise as $4kT\gamma g_m$, resistor noise as $4kT/R$, and flicker noise as a gate voltage $K_N/(C_{ox}WL\,f)$; denote the transconductance of each current-source transistor in [Fig. 2] by $g_{m3}$.
