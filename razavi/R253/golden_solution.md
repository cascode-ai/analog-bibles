# Golden Solution - R253

## Reference answer

$V_X$ stays at $V_{GS}(I_{REF}) = 0.7 + a$, $a = \sqrt{2I_{REF}/\beta}$, until $M_3$ turns on. $V_Y$ rises from 0: first with $M_1$ in triode (initial slope $1/(1 + \beta R_1a)$, rising to 1), then as $V_1 - R_1I_{REF}$ (slope 1) once $M_1$ saturates (at $V_1 = R_1I_{REF} + a$), and finally, once $V_Y$ reaches $V_X + V_{TH3}(V_X)$ (at $V_1 = V_X + V_{TH3} + R_1I_{REF}$), $M_3$ turns on. Its current is mirrored into $M_1$, the extra drop on $R_1$ holds $V_Y$ back (negative feedback, $V_Y \approx V_X + V_{GS3}$), so $V_Y$'s slope falls below 1 while $V_X$ starts to rise. How strong this clamp is depends on $\beta R_1(V_X - 0.7)$: strong for large $R_1$, weak (slope near 1) for small $R_1$.

- While $M_3$ is off, $M_2$ carries $I_{REF}$ and $M_1$ copies it if saturated: $V_Y = V_1 - R_1I_{REF}$. For small $V_1$ there is not enough voltage: $M_1$ is in triode, $(V_1 - V_Y)/R_1 = \beta(aV_Y - V_Y^2/2)$, and $V_Y$ is a fraction of $V_1$ (≈ 0.3 initially in the example).
- $M_3$ (drain at $V_1$, gate at Y, source at X) conducts when $V_Y - V_X > V_{TH3}(V_X) = 0.7 + 0.45(\sqrt{0.9 + V_X} - \sqrt{0.9})$ (≈ 0.89 V for $V_X \approx 0.97$ V). Its current adds to $M_2$'s, $M_1$ copies the larger current, the drop on $R_1$ grows and $V_Y$ is pulled back.
- In that region, with $u = V_X - 0.7$ and $w = \sqrt{u^2 - a^2}$ ($M_3$'s overdrive): $V_Y = V_X + V_{TH3}(V_X) + w$ and $V_1 = V_Y + \tfrac{\beta R_1}{2}u^2$. Hence $dV_X/dV_1 = 1/(D + \beta R_1u)$ and $dV_Y/dV_1 = D/(D + \beta R_1u)$ with $D = 1 + dV_{TH3}/dV_X + u/w$. At turn-on $w = 0$: $V_Y$'s slope is still 1 and $V_X$'s is 0 (smooth transition, $V_X - V_{GS}(I_{REF}) \approx (V_1 - V_{1,on})^2/(2a)$); further on both keep rising, $V_Y$ with a slope decreasing below 1.
- Branches within the sweep: if $R_1I_{REF} + a \ge 3$ V only the triode region appears; if $V_{GS}(I_{REF}) + V_{TH3} + R_1I_{REF} \ge 3$ V, $M_3$ never turns on.
- Example ($I_{REF}$ = 100 μA, W/L = 10/0.5 with $\mu_nC_{ox}$ = 134 μA/V², i.e. β = 2.68 mA/V², $R_1$ = 3 kΩ; square-law simulation): $V_X$ = 0.97 V until $V_1 \approx 2.16$ V; $V_Y$ ≈ 0.09 V at 0.25 V, 0.45 V at 0.75 V, then $V_1 - 0.3$ V up to ≈ 1.86 V, then 2.09, 2.33 V at $V_1$ = 2.5, 3 V (slope ≈ 0.5) with $V_X$ rising to 1.11 V. With $R_1$ = 300 Ω the post-turn-on slope of $V_Y$ is ≈ 0.9; with $R_1$ = 30 kΩ $M_1$ stays in triode over the whole sweep ($V_Y$ ≈ 0.2 V at 3 V).

## Grading notes

Answers may describe the curves symbolically, by parameter cases, or with their own example values; an exact square-law treatment must not be docked.
+2: constant $V_X = V_{GS}(I_{REF})$ initially; $V_Y$ low (M1 triode) then $V_1 - R_1I_{REF}$; $M_3$ turning on when $V_Y \approx V_X + V_{TH3}$ and holding $V_Y$ back (negative feedback through $M_2$–$M_1$), after which both rise with $V_Y$'s slope below 1. Calling this a clamp with $V_Y$ "rising slowly" is accepted; so is a weak clamp (slope near 1) for small $R_1$. Correctly stated branches where $M_3$ never turns on or $M_1$ never saturates within the sweep are correct, not omissions. A wrong secondary detail of the post-turn-on shape (e.g., $V_X$'s slope said to decrease monotonically from turn-on, though it starts at zero, or a wrong coefficient in the near-turn-on expansion) is a minor false claim: +1 if everything else is right. +1: clamp mechanism right but the triode region at low $V_1$ missing, or body effect of $M_3$ ignored. 0: $V_Y = V_1 - R_1I_{REF}$ all the way with $M_3$ never on, without a justification that $V_{GS}(I_{REF}) + V_{TH3} + R_1I_{REF} \ge 3$ V. -2 (asserted as fact): $M_3$ conducting from the start, $V_X$ following $V_1$, $V_Y$ decreasing, or $M_3$'s drain read as connected to $V_{DD}$.
