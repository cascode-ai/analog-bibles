An NMOS transistor with $W/L = 50/0.5$ is biased with $V_G = +1.2$ V and $V_S = 0$; the bulk voltage is zero. The drain voltage is varied from 0 to 3 V. Plot the intrinsic gain (the transconductance $g_m$ times the small-signal drain–source resistance) versus $V_{DS}$. Device data: $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = 0.7$ V, $\lambda = 0.1\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$. Dimensions are effective values in μm.
