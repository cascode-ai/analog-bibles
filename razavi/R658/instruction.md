In the charge-pump PLL shown in [Fig. 1], the PFD drives a charge pump whose UP and DOWN currents are $I_1 = I_2 = I_P$. The charge pump drives a loop filter consisting of $R_P$ in series with $C_P$, and the VCO control voltage $V_{cont}$ is taken at the charge-pump output. Prove that the transfer function of the PFD/charge-pump/loop-filter combination, from the input phase difference Δφ to $V_{cont}$, is $\dfrac{V_{cont}}{\Delta\phi}(s) = \dfrac{I_P}{2\pi}\left(R_P + \dfrac{1}{C_Ps}\right)$ (in the usual continuous-time approximation).
