A source follower can operate as a level shifter. Suppose the circuit shown in [Fig. 1] is designed to shift the voltage level by 1 V, i.e., $V_{in} - V_{out} = 1$ V, at $V_{in} = 2.5$ V, with $I_{D1} = I_{D2} = 0.5$ mA, $V_{GS2} - V_{GS1} = 0.5$ V, and $\lambda = 0$, but now with body effect: $\gamma = 0.45\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.9$ V (bulks at ground). Calculate the dimensions of $M_1$ and $M_2$. With these dimensions and bias, what is the minimum input voltage for which $M_2$ remains saturated? $V_{DD} = 3$ V. NMOS data: $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = 0.7$ V. Dimensions are effective values in μm.
