An LC oscillator is tuned by a pn-junction varactor whose capacitance is $C_{var} = \dfrac{C_0}{(1 + V_R/\phi_B)^m}$, where $C_0$ is the zero-bias value and $V_R$ the reverse-bias voltage, with $\phi_B = 0.7$ V and $m = 0.35$; $V_R$ can vary from 0 to 2 V. The inductor of the tank contributes a constant capacitance equal to $C_1$ in parallel with the varactor, and the tank has no other capacitance. How much tuning range can be achieved? Express the result in terms of $C_1/C_0$.
