# Golden Solution - R526

## Reference answer

$M_6$ is a common-source stage degenerated by $r_{O8}$, with no body effect (bulk tied to source), driving the resistance seen looking down into the drain of $M_4$, $R_D = [1 + (g_{m4}+g_{mb4})r_{O4}]r_{O2} + r_{O4}$. Its gate noise reaches $Y$ with gain
$$A_6 = \frac{g_{m6}r_{O6}R_D}{R_D + r_{O8} + r_{O6} + g_{m6}r_{O6}r_{O8}} \approx \frac{R_{out}}{r_{O8}},$$
whereas $M_8$'s gate noise sees $A_8 \approx g_{m8}R_{out}$. So $A_6/A_8 \approx 1/(g_{m8}r_{O8})$.

- Let $R_{up} = r_{O8} + r_{O6} + g_{m6}r_{O6}r_{O8}$ (the resistance looking up into the drain of $M_6$) and $R_{out} = R_{up} \parallel R_D$. Then $A_6 = (g_{m6}r_{O6}/R_{up})R_{out}$, which is ≈ $R_{out}/r_{O8}$ for $g_{m6}r_{O6} \gg 1$. In other words, the degeneration by $r_{O8}$ greatly reduces the noise current of $M_6$.
- For $M_8$, the short-circuit current at $Y$ is $g_{m8}V_{n8}\,r_{O8}(1+g_{m6}r_{O6})/R_{up} \approx g_{m8}V_{n8}$. Hence $A_8 = \dfrac{g_{m8}r_{O8}(1+g_{m6}r_{O6})R_D}{R_D + r_{O8} + r_{O6} + g_{m6}r_{O6}r_{O8}} \approx g_{m8}R_{out}$.
- The exact ratio is $A_6/A_8 = g_{m6}r_{O6}/[g_{m8}r_{O8}(1+g_{m6}r_{O6})] \approx 1/(g_{m8}r_{O8})$.
- With $\overline{V_n^2} = 4kT\gamma/g_m$, the ratio of output noise PSDs is $S_6/S_8 = (g_{m8}/g_{m6})(A_6/A_8)^2 \approx 1/(g_{m6}g_{m8}r_{O8}^2)$. This is ≈ $1/(g_{m8}r_{O8})^2$ for $g_{m6} \approx g_{m8}$ (equal sizes and currents).
- The cascode's noise is therefore suppressed by the intrinsic gain of the current source. With long-channel devices ($g_mr_O$ ~ 50–100) it is negligible. These short-channel PMOS devices have $g_mr_O$ of only about 5–10 ($r_{O8}$ is a few hundred ohms at 0.95 mA and $|V_{DS}| \approx 100$ mV). So $M_6$ contributes roughly 1–4% of $M_8$'s noise power: small, but no longer negligible.
