The bandgap reference shown in [Fig. 1] has $(W/L)_3 = (W/L)_4 = 50\ \mu\mathrm{m}/0.5\ \mu\mathrm{m}$, $|I_{D3}| = |I_{D4}| = 50\ \mu$A, $(W/L)_5 = 4.45\,(W/L)_4$ (so $|I_{D5}| \approx 222\ \mu$A), $R_1 = 1$ kΩ, $R_2 = 2$ kΩ and $n \approx 6.9$; $Q_3$ is identical to $Q_1$. The op amp is ideal and noiseless; neglect channel-length modulation and base currents. Neglecting the noise contribution of $Q_1$–$Q_3$ (but keeping their small-signal resistances $1/g_m = V_T/I_C$), calculate the output thermal noise voltage (spectral density at $V_{out}$) at $T = 300$ K. Use $4kT\gamma g_m$ with $\gamma = 2/3$ for the MOSFET drain thermal noise current and $4kTR$ for the resistors; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$.
