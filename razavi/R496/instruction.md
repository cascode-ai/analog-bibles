[Fig. 1] depicts a transimpedance amplifier employing voltage–current feedback. Assume that $I_1$–$I_3$ are ideal, $I_1 = I_2 = 1$ mA, $I_3 = 10\ \mu$A, $(W/L)_{1,2} = 50/0.5$, and $(W/L)_3 = 5/0.5$; $V_{DD} = 3$ V. The loop is analyzed by breaking it at the gate of $M_3$. If the circuit is compensated by adding a capacitor $C_C$ between the gate and the drain of $M_1$, what value of $C_C$ achieves a phase margin of 60°? Determine the poles after compensation.

Device data: NMOS $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH} = 0.7$ V, $\lambda = 0.1\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH} = -0.8$ V, $\lambda = 0.2\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$. For the device capacitances, consider only the gate–source and gate–drain capacitances of $M_1$–$M_3$: $C_{ox} = 3.84\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, overlap capacitance per unit width 0.4 fF/µm (NMOS) and 0.3 fF/µm (PMOS), effective $L = 0.5\ \mu$m. Neglect junction capacitances.
