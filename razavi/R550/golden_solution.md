# Golden Solution - R550

## Reference answer

$R_2 \approx 17$ kΩ (16.8 kΩ with the 17.2$V_T$ rule; 18 kΩ if the −1.5 mV/K slope is assigned to $Q_{1,2}$ instead of $Q_{3,4}$, see below); $(W/L)_1 = (W/L)_3 = 2(W/L)_2 = 2(W/L)_4$, e.g. $(W/L)_{2,4} \approx 29$ and $(W/L)_{1,3} \approx 58$ (overdrive ≈ 0.3 V; any $(W/L)_{2,4} \gtrsim 10$ keeps the overdrive below the ≈ 0.5 V available). The op amp can be a single-stage five-transistor OTA with an NMOS input pair and a PMOS current-mirror load.
- PTAT: $V_X = V_Y$ gives $I_{D2}R_1 = 2V_T\ln(2n) = 50$ mV, so $\ln(2n) = 0.967$, a current-density ratio $2n \approx 2.6$ and $n \approx 1.3$ (forced by $R_1$ and the currents).
- $V_{out} = |V_{EB3}| + |V_{EB4}| + I_{D2}(R_1 + R_2) = 2|V_{BE}| + (1 + R_2/R_1)(50\ \mathrm{mV})$. Zero TC: $(1 + R_2/R_1)(50\ \mathrm{mV}/300\ \mathrm{K}) = 3$ mV/K, so $1 + R_2/R_1 = 18$ and $R_2 = 17$ kΩ. $V_{out} \approx 1.6 + 0.9 = 2.5$ V.
- Equally valid: $V_{out} = V_X + I_{D2}R_2 = |V_{EB1}| + |V_{EB2}| + (R_2/R_1)(50\ \mathrm{mV})$, which with −1.5 mV/K applied to $Q_{1,2}$ gives $R_2/R_1 = 18$, $R_2 = 18$ kΩ. The two groups' slopes must differ by $50\ \mathrm{mV}/(2 \times 300\ \mathrm{K})$ ≈ 0.083 mV/K, so the given common slope cannot hold exactly for both; 17 vs 18 kΩ reflects only which pair it is applied to.
- Headroom: $M_2$'s drain is at $V_{out} \approx 2.5$ V, so $|V_{DS2}| \approx 0.5$ V and the overdrive must stay below that. With 0.3 V: $(W/L)_{2,4} = 2(50\ \mu\mathrm{A})/(38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2} \times 0.09\ \mathrm{V^2}) \approx 29$ and $(W/L)_{1,3} \approx 58$ (the 2:1 ratio is fixed by the currents). Long channels (e.g., 1–2 μm) reduce λ and mismatch. The gate voltage is ≈ 3 − 1.1 = 1.9 V; $M_1$ and $M_3$ (drains at ≈ 1.6 V and 0.8 V) are saturated.
- Op amp: its inputs sit at $V_X \approx V_Y \approx 1.6$ V, its output must be ≈ 1.9 V, and it drives only gates (no resistive load). A five-transistor OTA with NMOS inputs accepts input levels from ≈ $V_{GS,N} + V_{DS,tail}$ (≈ 1 V) to ≈ $V_{DD} - |V_{GS,P}| + V_{TH,N}$ (≈ 2.6 V), and its output rests near $V_{DD} - |V_{GS,P}|$, matching the required gate voltage. A single stage is easy to compensate. A PMOS-input pair would be marginal (input level ≤ ≈ 1.8 V). Polarity: inverting input at $X$, noninverting at $Y$.

## Grading notes

Three parts. (1) PTAT relation $I_{D2}R_1 = 2V_T\ln(2n)$ (giving $n \approx 1.3$) and $R_2 \approx 17$ kΩ (16–18 kΩ) from the zero-TC condition with two series junctions; 18 kΩ from $V_{out} = V_X + I_{D2}R_2$ with −1.5 mV/K applied to $Q_{1,2}$ is an equally correct derivation, not an omission of the $R_1$ drop, and earns full credit for this part. (2) PMOS sizes in a 2:1 ratio, with an overdrive compatible with the ≈ 0.5 V across $M_2$ (e.g., ≈ 29 and ≈ 58; other values accepted if saturation is verified). (3) A suitable op amp, e.g., a single-stage OTA with NMOS input pair, justified by the input level (≈ 1.6 V) and output level (≈ 1.9 V). +2: all three. +1: two of three, or all three with a minor slip. 0: one of three. -2 (asserted as fact): $R_2$ from a single-junction design (≈ 8 kΩ, missing the two series $V_{BE}$), equal sizes for all four PMOS despite the 2:1 currents, sizes that put $M_2$ in triode, or $n$ obtained from $V_T\ln n$ (ignoring the 2:1 current ratio and the two junctions).
