A negative-feedback system consists of a subtractor, a nonlinear feedforward amplifier and a linear feedback network of factor $\beta$: the amplifier input is $y_S(t) = x(t) - \beta y(t)$ and its output is $y(t) = \alpha_1y_S(t) + \alpha_3y_S^3(t)$, a mildly nonlinear characteristic. For the input $x(t) = V_m\cos\omega t$, estimate the magnitude of the third harmonic at the output of the overall system. Neglect phase shifts through the system.
