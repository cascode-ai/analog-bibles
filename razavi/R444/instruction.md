In the amplifier shown in [Fig. 1], $I_1 = 100\ \mu$A, $I_2 = 0.5$ mA, and $(W/L)_{1-3} = 100\ \mu\mathrm{m}/0.5\ \mu\mathrm{m}$. $I_1$ and $I_2$ are implemented with PMOS devices having $(W/L)_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{m}/0.5\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ (sources at $V_{DD}$), and $V_{in}$ is biased so that $M_1$ carries $I_2$. $V_{DD} = 3$ V; all transistors are saturated. Calculate the overall low-frequency voltage gain and the input-referred thermal noise voltage.
