Consider the amplifier shown in [Fig. 1]: the input $V_{in}$ is applied to the n-wells (bulks) of $M_1$ and $M_2$ (dotted connections), whose sources are at $V_{DD}$ and whose gates are held at the bias voltage $V_{b1}$; $M_3$ and $M_4$ are NMOS current sources biased by $V_{b2}$; $V_{out}$ is taken differentially between the two drains. Calculate the small-signal differential voltage gain. Express it in terms of the small-signal parameters and the input common-mode level $V_{in,CM}$; device sizes and bias voltages are not specified.

Device data: PMOS $V_{TH0} = -0.8$ V, $\gamma = 0.4\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.8$ V.
