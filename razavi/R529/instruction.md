A two-stage op amp contains a nondominant pole, $\omega_{p2}$, at its output and is compensated for a phase margin of 45° in a feedback loop with feedback factor β, so that $|\beta H|$ drops to unity at $\omega_{p2}$ ($H$ being the op amp's open-loop transfer function). Assume that the dominant pole is much lower than $\omega_{p2}$. Estimate the degradation in phase margin if the load capacitance seen at the output is doubled.
