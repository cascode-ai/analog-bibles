The circuit shown in [Fig. 1] is the small-signal model of a bandgap reference used for noise calculations. $M_1$ and $M_2$ are identical PMOS transistors, each with transconductance $g_{mP}$, driven at their gates by the op-amp output $P$; their small-signal drain currents are shown as $g_{mP}V_P$. Each bipolar transistor is represented by its small-signal resistance $1/g_{mN}$, the op amp has gain $A_0$, and the output is taken at node $B$. Calculate the output noise voltage at $B$ due to the thermal and flicker noise of $M_1$ and $M_2$ (set the op-amp noise source $V_{n,op}$ to zero). Model the drain noise current of each PMOS as $\overline{i_n^2} = 4kT\gamma g_{mP} + \dfrac{K_P}{C_{ox}WLf}g_{mP}^2$.
