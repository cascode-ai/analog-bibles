[Fig. 1] shows the root locus of the closed-loop poles of a type I PLL as $K_{PD}K_{VCO}$ increases from zero. The loop consists of a phase detector with gain $K_{PD}$, a first-order low-pass filter $1/(1 + s/\omega_{LPF})$, and a VCO with transfer function $K_{VCO}/s$, with unity feedback. Its closed-loop transfer function can be written $\omega_n^2/(s^2 + 2\zeta\omega_ns + \omega_n^2)$. Prove that, for complex poles, $\cos\varphi = \zeta$, where φ is the angle marked in [Fig. 1] between the negative real axis and the line from the origin to a pole.
