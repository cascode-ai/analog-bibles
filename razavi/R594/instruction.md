[Fig. 1] shows a differential pair $M_1$–$M_2$ (outputs at the drains of $M_1$ and $M_2$) with PMOS current-source loads $M_3$–$M_4$ biased by $V_b$ and tail transistor $M_5$, together with its switched-capacitor common-mode feedback (CMFB) network. Capacitors $C_1$, $C_2$ connect the two outputs to node X, the gate of $M_5$. NMOS source followers $M_6$ and $M_7$ have their gates on the two outputs, their drains at $V_{DD}$, and a common source biased by the current source $I_D$. During the CM-definition (reset) phase, switch $S_1$ connects the followers' common source to X, with zero differential input; $S_1$ then opens and $C_1$, $C_2$ hold the voltage. Assume $W/L = 50/0.5$ for all transistors, $I_{D5} = 1$ mA, and $I_{D6} = I_{D7} = 50\ \mu$A. Determine the allowable range of the input common-mode level (all transistors in saturation). $V_{DD} = 3$ V. Device data: NMOS $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = 0.7$ V, $\gamma = 0.45\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.9$ V; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$; NMOS bulks at ground, PMOS bulks at $V_{DD}$; neglect channel-length modulation.
