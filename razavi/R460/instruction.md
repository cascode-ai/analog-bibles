The two-stage op amp shown in [Fig. 1] has been designed with $V_{DD} = 3$ V as follows: $M_7$ carries 1 mA and has an overdrive $|V_{GS7} - V_{TH7}| = 0.4$ V; $M_1$ and $M_2$ (each 0.5 mA) have $(W/L)_{1,2} = 206$, i.e. $|V_{GS} - V_{TH}| = 0.356$ V; $M_3$ and $M_4$ have $V_{GS3} = V_{GS4} = 0.95$ V; $M_5$ carries 1 mA with $V_{GS5} = 0.95$ V (overdrive 0.25 V), and $M_6$ supplies 1 mA. The op amp is placed in unity-gain feedback: the output is connected to the gate of $M_1$ (the inverting input) and the input voltage is applied to the gate of $M_2$. What is the allowable input voltage range (all transistors saturated)? The n-wells of the PMOS devices are tied to $V_{DD}$; ignore channel-length modulation in the bias calculation.

Device data: NMOS $V_{TH0} = 0.7$ V; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = -0.8$ V, $\gamma = 0.4\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.8$ V.
