# Golden Solution - R200

## Reference answer

$A_v = -g_{m1}\left(r_{O1}\|r_{O3}\|R_1\right)$.
- The gates of $M_3$, $M_4$ connect to the midpoint of $R_1$–$R_2$, which does not move for differential signals (ac ground). Hence $M_3$, $M_4$ act as current sources ($r_{O3}$), and each output sees $R_1$ to ac ground.

## Grading notes

+2: the expression above (equivalent forms, magnitude accepted). +1: one $r_O$ omitted. 0: $R_1/2$ used instead of $R_1$. -2 (asserted as fact): $M_3$/$M_4$ treated as diode-connected ($1/g_{m3}$ in the load), or $R_1$ in series.
