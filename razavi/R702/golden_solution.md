# Golden Solution - R702

## Reference answer

$\rho_{poly}/\rho_{M1} = 250$.

- Resistivity = sheet resistance × thickness ($R_\square = \rho/t$); the widths do not enter.
- Poly: $\rho = 30\ \Omega \times 100\ \mathrm{nm} = 3\times10^{-6}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}$ (300 μΩ·cm).
- Metal 1: $\rho = 0.08\ \Omega \times 150\ \mathrm{nm} = 1.2\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}$ (1.2 μΩ·cm).
- Ratio: $3\times10^{-6}/1.2\times10^{-8} = 250$ (the sheet-resistance ratio 375 reduced by the thickness ratio 150/100).
