The common-gate circuit shown in [Fig. 1] — $M_1$ (NMOS, gate at $V_b$, source = input, drain = output) loaded by the PMOS current source $M_3$ and biased by the NMOS current source $M_2$ (both mirrors bypassed by capacitors) — is designed with $(W/L)_1 = 50/0.5$ and $I_{D1-3} = 0.5$ mA, with $V_{DD} = 3$ V. If an output swing of 2 V is required, estimate (by iteration if needed) the dimensions of $M_2$ and $M_3$ such that the input-referred thermal noise current is minimum. All transistors must stay in saturation and $V_b$ may be chosen freely. Use the square law with $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, channel noise $4kT\gamma g_m$ with the same γ for all devices; neglect body effect and channel-length modulation.
