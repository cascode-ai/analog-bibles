Consider the common-source stage shown in [Fig. 1], where the current source $I_1$ is realized by a PMOS transistor $M_2$ operating in saturation (source at $V_{DD}$, gate at a fixed bias voltage) with $(W/L)_2 = 162.8/0.5$ (an aspect ratio of about 325), chosen so that its overdrive is 0.4 V at 1 mA. Assume $(W/L)_1 = 50/0.5$ (effective dimensions in microns), $I_{D1} = 1$ mA, and $R_S = 1\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$. Determine the poles and the zero of the circuit, including the capacitances of $M_1$ ($C_{GS1}$, $C_{GD1}$, $C_{DB1}$) and those of $M_2$ that load the output.

Device data: NMOS $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0.1\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, overlap capacitance $0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$ (gate–source and gate–drain each), $C_j = 0.56\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{jsw} = 0.35\times10^{-11}$ F/m; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0.2\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, overlap $0.3\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$, $C_j = 0.94\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{jsw} = 0.32\times10^{-11}$ F/m; $C_{ox} = 3.84\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$ for both. Take the drain regions as $W \times E$ with $E = 1.5\ \mu$m (all four sides counted as sidewall) and use zero-bias junction capacitances.
