# Golden Solution - R516

## Reference answer

$I_D = \dfrac{\beta}{3}(V_{GS} - V_{TH})^3$.

Since $g_m = \partial I_D/\partial V_{GS} = \beta(V_{GS}-V_{TH})^2$, integrating with $I_D = 0$ at $V_{GS} = V_{TH}$ (no current at threshold in this model) gives $I_D = \beta(V_{GS}-V_{TH})^3/3$.
