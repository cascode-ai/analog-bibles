MOS devices operating in the subthreshold region exhibit an exponential characteristic: $I_D = I_0\exp[V_{GS}/(\zeta V_T)]$. Suppose the differential pair shown in [Fig. 1] (tail current $I_{SS} = 2I$, symmetric) operates in the subthreshold region. First prove that $I_{D1} - I_{D2} \propto \tanh[V_{in}/(2\zeta V_T)]$, and then, writing the Taylor expansion of the hyperbolic tangent, derive expressions for the harmonic amplitudes of $I_{D1} - I_{D2}$ for a differential input $V_{in} = V_m\cos\omega t$ with $V_m \ll \zeta V_T$.
