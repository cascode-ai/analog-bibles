The stage shown in [Fig. 1] is used in a three-stage ring oscillator built from identical stages. It is designed with $I_T = 1$ mA and $(W/L)_{1,2} = 50/0.5$; $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$. Neglect $I_H$, channel-length modulation and body effect. Determine the minimum value of $R_1 = R_2 = R$ that ensures oscillation of the three-stage ring when all of $I_T$ is steered to $M_1$–$M_2$ (the cross-coupled pair $M_3$–$M_4$ carrying no current).
