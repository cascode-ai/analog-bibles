The gate resistance of a wide MOSFET can be modeled by the distributed structure shown in [Fig. 1]: $n$ identical unit transistors share the drain D and the source S, and the gate signal applied at G travels through gate-resistance segments $R_{G1}, R_{G2}, \dots, R_{Gn}$ in series ($R_{G1} + R_{G2} + \dots + R_{Gn} = R_G$), the gate of the $j$-th unit transistor being tapped right after segment $R_{Gj}$. Ignoring the channel thermal noise, prove that, for gate noise calculations, the distributed gate resistance $R_G$ can be replaced by a lumped resistance equal to $R_G/3$ in series with the gate of the whole transistor. Assume low frequencies (no gate current) and equal segments. (Hint: model the noise of each $R_{Gj}$ by a series voltage source and calculate the total drain noise current. Watch for correlated sources of noise.)
