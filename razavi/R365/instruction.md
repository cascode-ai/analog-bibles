In the common-gate stage shown in [Fig. 1], $M_1$ (gate at $V_b$, source = input, load $R_D$) is biased by the NMOS current source $M_2$, which mirrors the diode-connected $M_0$ (fed by $I_1$); the capacitor $C_0$ shunts the noise of $M_0$ to ground, and $\overline{I_{n2}^2}$ is the noise current of $M_2$. The circuit is designed with $(W/L)_1 = 50/0.5$, $I_{D1} = I_{D2} = 0.5$ mA, and $R_D = 1$ kΩ. Determine $(W/L)_2$ such that the contribution of $M_2$ to the input-referred thermal noise current (not current squared) is one-fifth of that due to $R_D$. Use the square law with $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, channel thermal noise $4kT\gamma g_m$ with $\gamma = 2/3$, and neglect channel-length modulation and body effect.
