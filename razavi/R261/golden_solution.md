# Golden Solution - R261

## Reference answer

The body effect lowers $V_{DS2}$ below the edge of saturation, so $M_2$ enters the triode region and the mismatch becomes considerably larger than the γ = 0 value (≈ −λ$V_{TH}$): typically 10–15% low.

- $V_{DS2} = V_{GS1} + V_{GS0} - V_{GS4} - V_{GS3}$. Now the threshold of $M_0$ (source at X, $V_{SB} = V_X \approx V_{GS1}$) exceeds that of $M_3$ (source at $V_B = V_{DS2}$), which helps, but $M_4$'s threshold rises much more (its source is at the gate of $M_3$, ≈ 1 V): $V_{GS4} \approx V_{TH}(V_{SB} \approx 1\ \mathrm{V}) \approx 0.9$ V.
- Net ($V_{TH0}$ = 0.7 V, the zero-bias threshold): $V_{DS2} \approx (V_{GS1} - V_{TH0}) - (V_{GS4} - V_{TH4}) - \gamma\{[\sqrt{2|\phi_F|+V_{G3}} - \sqrt{2|\phi_F|+V_X}] + [\sqrt{2|\phi_F|+V_B} - \sqrt{2|\phi_F|}]\}$; the bracket is ≈ 0.12 V$^{1/2}$ for typical values, i.e., $V_{DS2}$ is ≈ 50–60 mV below $V_{GS1} - V_{TH}$. This takes $V_{ov0} = V_{ov3}$; the exact relation adds $+(V_{ov0} - V_{ov3})$, which is small (λ-dependent, set by the self-consistent currents and the output voltage) and does not reverse the conclusion for any reasonable output voltage.
- $M_2$ in triode carries less than $M_1$ for the same $V_{GS}$: $I_{out}/I_{REF} \approx 2(V_{DS2}/V_{ov}) - (V_{DS2}/V_{ov})^2$ (times the λ factor), so the error grows rapidly as $V_{DS2}$ falls below $V_{ov}$; the high output impedance of the cascode is also degraded.
- Example (same as for γ = 0: $I_{REF}$ = 100 μA, $M_0$–$M_3$ = 20/0.5, $M_4$ = 200/0.5 at 5 μA, $\mu_nC_{ox}$ = 134 μA/V², $V_{DD}$ = 3 V, output at 2 V; square-law simulation): $V_{GS4}$ = 0.91 V, $V_{DS2}$ = 0.136 V < $V_{ov}$ = 0.185 V, $I_{out}$ ≈ 86.6 μA (−13%, versus −6.4% with γ = 0); for an output between 1.5 and 3 V, $I_{out}$ = 85.9–87.7 μA. The percentage is an example, not a universal value.
- A general exact form, valid while $M_0$ and $M_3$ are saturated: $I_{out}/I_{REF} = (V_{ov3}/V_{ov0})^2[1+\lambda V_{DS3}]/[1+\lambda V_{DS0}]$, with the overdrives taken from the node voltages and the body-effect thresholds; equivalently, the $M_1$/$M_2$ ratio $\{[2V_{ov}V_{DS2} - V_{DS2}^2]/V_{ov}^2\}(1+\lambda V_{DS2})/(1+\lambda V_{GS1})$ once $M_2$ is in triode.

## Grading notes

+2: identifies that $M_4$'s threshold is raised by the body effect (source near 1 V), so the level shift exceeds the overdrive and $M_2$ is pushed into triode (partly offset by $M_0$'s threshold exceeding $M_3$'s), giving a larger mismatch than the γ = 0 case, with a reasonable estimate (≈ 10–15% low, or an equivalent expression in the node voltages). Small imprecisions in side arguments (e.g. a loose proof that $V_{DS2} > 0$, or the overdrives of $M_0$ and $M_3$ taken as equal) are not docked. +1: correct direction (larger error, $M_2$ toward triode) with a rough or missing estimate. 0: body-effect terms listed but no conclusion about $M_2$'s region or the error direction. -2 (asserted as fact): body effect claimed to reduce the mismatch to zero or to raise $V_{DS2}$ well into saturation, or $I_{out}$ claimed larger than $I_{REF}$ by tens of percent. The phrase "so as to bias $M_2$ at the edge of saturation" describes the γ = 0 design; with γ ≠ 0 the wide, low-current $M_4$ still has $V_{GS4} \approx V_{TH4}(V_{SB})$, which the body effect raises, so the premise no longer holds and checking it is the point of the question. Taking $M_2$ as still at the edge of saturation (or still saturated) with γ ≠ 0 and giving the γ = 0 mismatch (≈ $-\lambda V_{TH}/(1+\lambda V_{GS1})$, 6–7%) as the answer is -2 when asserted as fact, and -1 when offered only as one case (e.g., "if $M_4$ and $I_1$ are re-tuned to keep the edge") with the actual body-effect case left unsolved or set up with $M_2$'s saturation equation. It is 0 when the edge case is explicitly conditional and the answer also gives a correct general expression for the actual circuit (e.g. the $M_0$/$M_3$ current ratio in terms of the body-effect node voltages) and says that the body effect may move $M_2$ off the edge, without concluding which way. An answer that treats both readings and concludes that, as built, $M_2$ is pushed into triode with a larger error is +2.
