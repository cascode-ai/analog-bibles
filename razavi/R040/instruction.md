The transit frequency, $f_T$, of a MOSFET is the frequency at which its small-signal current gain drops to unity while the source and drain are held at ac ground; for a device with negligible gate resistance, $f_T = g_m/[2\pi(C_{GD} + C_{GS})]$. Suppose the gate resistance, $R_G$, is significant and the device is modeled as a distributed set of $n$ transistors, each with a gate resistance equal to $R_G/n$. Prove that the $f_T$ of the device is independent of $R_G$ and still equal to $g_m/[2\pi(C_{GD} + C_{GS})]$.
