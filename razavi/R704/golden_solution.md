# Golden Solution - R704

## Reference answer

Key features: (1) each device is split into N parallel gate fingers, with N set by the 10-Ω limit; (2) the input device $M_1$ and the cascode $M_2$ are interleaved so that each drain of $M_1$ is merged with a source of $M_2$ into one uncontacted, minimum-width diffusion; (3) N is even and the fingers are mirrored, so that every output drain (drain of $M_2$) is shared by two fingers and the outer diffusions are sources of $M_1$ (ground).

Finger count. A single 100-μm finger has an end-to-end gate resistance of $5 \times 100/0.5 = 1$ kΩ, far too high. With N fingers each $W/N$ wide, contacted at one end and connected in parallel:
- Distributed model (effective resistance one third of the end-to-end value): $R_G = R_\square W/(3LN^2) = 333/N^2\ \Omega \le 10\ \Omega$ gives $N \ge 5.8$, so N = 6 fingers of ≈ 16.7 μm ($R_G$ ≈ 9.3 Ω).
- Lumped end-to-end model: $1000/N^2 \le 10$ gives N = 10 fingers of 10 μm.
- Contacting each finger at both ends lowers the requirement (e.g., N = 4 with the distributed model, ≈ 5.2 Ω).

Layout (one active strip, gate fingers vertical, x = merged $M_1$-drain/$M_2$-source node, D = output drain, S = source of $M_1$):
S | G1 | x | G2 | D | G2 | x | G1 | S | G1 | x | G2 | D | G2 | x | G1 | S …
- Each x region lies between a $G_1$ and a $G_2$ finger. It connects to nothing else, so it needs no contact and is drawn at minimum poly-to-poly spacing. The capacitance at the cascode node shrinks to roughly W × (minimum spacing) of junction area. Leaving the x regions unconnected turns the structure into N identical cascode branches in parallel; since the branches are matched, their x nodes sit at the same potential and the circuit is equivalent to one cascode (the internal node is not used elsewhere). Contacting the x regions and tying them together in metal is electrically valid, but the contacts force wider diffusions and add capacitance at the cascode node, so it is a less optimized layout, not an error.
- The N/2 output drain regions are each shared by two $M_2$ fingers, which halves the output drain area and keeps their perimeter interior (no field-edge sidewall along the width). The N/2 + 1 source regions of $M_1$ (including both ends of the strip) go to ground, where capacitance is harmless.
- The $G_1$ fingers are tied together by poly (strapped in metal, preferably contacted at both ends) to the input; the $G_2$ fingers likewise to the cascode bias. For N = 6 the strip has 12 gate fingers; it may be folded into two rows if the aspect ratio is awkward.

## Grading notes

Three features, roughly equal weight: (i) multiple fingers with the count justified by the 10-Ω limit under a stated gate-resistance model (≈ 6 fingers with the distributed 1/3 factor, 10 with lumped end-to-end resistance, fewer if contacted at both ends: any consistent model is fine); (ii) $M_1$'s drain merged with $M_2$'s source in a shared, uncontacted, minimum-size diffusion (merged but contacted and strapped together in metal earns half of this feature: legal, but not minimum capacitance); (iii) the output drain regions shared between adjacent $M_2$ fingers (even number of fingers, sources on the outside). +2: all three. +1: two of the three with the third merely missing, or all three with a slip in the finger count, or the over-conservative reading that each finger alone must be ≤ 10 Ω (≈ 100 fingers) with (ii) and (iii) both right. 0: the over-conservative ≈ 100-finger count with only one of (ii) and (iii); or only the finger count right, with no attention to the drain/cascode-node capacitance. -2 (asserted as fact): a single 100-μm finger claimed acceptable, the finger count derived from $R = R_\square L/W$ or with resistance growing as fingers are added, or $M_1$'s drain and $M_2$'s source drawn as separate diffusions joined by metal (or the merged diffusion claimed not to conduct without a metal connection). Contacting merged x regions only to tie the parallel branches' internal nodes together is not a -2 error; it is the partial credit for (ii) above. Example: one-end distributed model with 6 fingers, all output drains shared, x merged but contacted and strapped: +1; 3 fingers (odd, one output drain at the strip edge) with x contacted and strapped: 0.
