# Golden Solution - R077

## Reference answer

$g_mr_O = \dfrac{1}{\lambda}\sqrt{\dfrac{2\mu C_{ox}(W/L)}{I_D}} \propto \sqrt{W/L}$: a square-root curve rising with $W/L$ (doubling for every fourfold increase of $W/L$).

- $g_m = \sqrt{2\mu C_{ox}(W/L)I_D}$ grows as $\sqrt{W/L}$; $r_O = 1/(\lambda I_D)$ is fixed; the overdrive falls as $1/\sqrt{W/L}$.
- (Optional: at very large $W/L$ the device enters weak inversion, $g_m$ saturates near $I_D/(\zeta V_T)$, and the real curve flattens.)

## Grading notes

+2: $\propto \sqrt{W/L}$ increasing curve with the reason ($g_m \propto \sqrt{W/L}$, $r_O$ constant). +1: increasing trend with the right reason but the law not stated, or the law without reason. 0: increasing but with a wrong law (e.g., linear) hedged. -2 (asserted as fact): gain constant, decreasing, or linear in $W/L$.
