# Golden Solution - R077

## Reference answer

Let $V_{ov}=V_{GS}-V_{TH}$. The plotted points have $V_{ov}>0$ and assume $V_{DS}\geq V_{ov}$.

With $I_D$ and $L$ fixed, $r_O=1/(\lambda I_D)$ is constant and $g_m=\sqrt{2\mu C_{ox}(W/L)I_D}$.

$$g_mr_O=\frac{1}{\lambda}\sqrt{\frac{2\mu C_{ox}(W/L)}{I_D}}.$$

The gain rises as $\sqrt{W/L}$ and doubles when $W/L$ is quadrupled.
