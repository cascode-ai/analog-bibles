In the circuit shown in [Fig. 1] (NMOS $M_1$ with gate = input, loaded by the PMOS current source $M_2$ with gate at fixed bias $V_b$ and source degeneration $R_S$ to $V_{DD}$), the bias current $I_D$ and the output voltage swing are given; the swing fixes the total voltage available for the load, $V_H = I_DR_S + |V_{GS2} - V_{TH2}|$. What value of $R_S$ minimizes the input-referred thermal noise? Use the square law, neglect channel-length modulation and body effect, and model the channel thermal noise as $4kT\gamma g_m$ with the long-channel value $\gamma = 2/3$; the noise of $R_S$ is $4kTR_S$.
