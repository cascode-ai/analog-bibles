# Golden Solution - R189

## Reference answer

Each load is a PMOS whose gate is taken from the bottom of $R_1$, so $M_3$ carries $I_{D1}$ and the left output is $V_{A} = V_{DD} - |V_{THP}| - \sqrt{2I_{D1}/\beta_p} + I_{D1}R_1$ (while $M_3$ is saturated, i.e. $I_{D1}R_1 < |V_{THP}|$); likewise on the right. Hence $V_{out} = f(I_{D1}) - f(I_{D2})$ with $f(I) = IR_1 - \sqrt{2I/\beta_p}$.
- Typical case, $R_1 < 1/g_{m3}$ ($g_{m3} = \sqrt{\beta_pI_{SS}}$ at balance): an inverting S-curve like that of a diode-loaded pair but with lower gain and swing. Central slope $dV_{out}/d(V_{in1} - V_{in2}) = -g_{m1}(1/g_{m3} - R_1)$; flat ends (full steering) at $V_{out} = \pm[\sqrt{2I_{SS}/\beta_p} - I_{SS}R_1]$, positive for low $V_{in1}$. The off side sits at $V_{DD} - |V_{THP}| = 2.2$ V.
- $R_1 = 1/g_{m3}$: zero small-signal gain at balance (the curve is flat at the center but still has nonzero ends, since $f$ is convex). For $1/g_{m3} < R_1 < \sqrt2/g_{m3}$ the slope at the center is positive while the ends keep the inverting signs (a non-monotonic curve); for larger $R_1$ the end levels change sign and the curve becomes non-inverting at the center, but it remains non-monotonic: near the edges of the steering region the slope tends to $-\sqrt{\beta_n/\beta_p}$ for any $R_1$ (the side whose current goes to zero has $1/g_m \to \infty$), so the curve first moves beyond the end levels and then crosses zero with positive slope.
- If $I_{SS}R_1 > |V_{THP}|$, the PMOS carrying the large current enters triode at full steering. In triode $I = \tfrac12\beta_p[V_{ov}^2 - (IR_1 - |V_{THP}|)^2]$ with $V_{ov} = |V_{GS}| - |V_{THP}|$, so the end level for low $V_{in1}$ becomes $+\big[\sqrt{2I_{SS}/\beta_p + (I_{SS}R_1 - |V_{THP}|)^2} - I_{SS}R_1\big]$ (the negative of it for high $V_{in1}$); at the low-$V_{in1}$ end this is algebraically larger than the saturated formula extrapolated, not smaller.
- These curves assume a constant tail current and an input pair saturated in the steering region (normal bias of $V_b$); at the extremes ($V_{in1} \to 3$ V) the conducting input device enters triode, which leaves the full-steering plateau unchanged as long as $M_5$ keeps carrying $I_{SS}$.
