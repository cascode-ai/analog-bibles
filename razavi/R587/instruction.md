The circuit shown in [Fig. 1] is the equivalent circuit of a switched-capacitor amplifier during amplification ($C_1$ driven by the input step, $C_2$ the feedback capacitor, $C_{in} = 0.2$ pF the op-amp input capacitance). The op amp is a single-stage transconductance amplifier with $G_m = 1/(100\ \Omega)$ and an output resistance $R_0$ such that its open-loop gain is $A_{v1} = G_mR_0 = 1000$; there is no load capacitance at the output. The gain error must not exceed 1%, and the circuit must achieve a time constant of 2 ns (at most) in the amplification mode. What is the maximum nominal gain $C_1/C_2$? Calculate $C_1$ and $C_2$.
