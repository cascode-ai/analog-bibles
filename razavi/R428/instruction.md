Design the folded-cascode op amp shown in [Fig. 1] for the following requirements: maximum differential output swing = 2.4 V (peak-to-peak), total power dissipation = 6 mW, $V_{DD} = 3$ V. If all of the transistors have a channel length of 0.5 μm, what is the overall voltage gain? Can the input common-mode level be as low as zero? Bulks of NMOS devices are at ground and of PMOS devices at $V_{DD}$; all transistors are to operate in saturation.

Device data: NMOS $\mu_nC_{ox} = 134\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = 0.7$ V, $\lambda = 0.1\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\gamma = 0.45\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.9$ V; PMOS $\mu_pC_{ox} = 38.4\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TH0} = -0.8$ V, $\lambda = 0.2\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\gamma = 0.4\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2|\phi_F| = 0.8$ V (λ values for L = 0.5 μm).
