The 4-bit DAC shown in [Fig. 1] has $M = 2$ and $K = 2$: an ideal 2-bit MSB subDAC converts $b_0 b_1$ ($b_0$ is the MSB), an ideal 2-bit LSB subDAC converts $b_2 b_3$ ($b_3$ is the LSB), and the output of the LSB subDAC is divided by the divisor $2^M = 4$ (which is correct) before it is added to the output of the MSB subDAC. Ideally both subDACs use the reference $V_{REF}$, but here the reference of the MSB subDAC is $0.75V_{REF}$ and the reference of the LSB subDAC is $1.25V_{REF}$. Express the $\pm INL$ and the $\pm DNL$ in terms of LSBs of the ideal 4-bit DAC (1 LSB $= V_{REF}/16$) and determine whether or not the DAC is monotonic.

Take the INL at each code as the deviation of the actual output from its ideal value, and the DNL at each transition between adjacent codes as the deviation of the actual step from 1 LSB; give the most positive and most negative values of each.
