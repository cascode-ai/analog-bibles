For a step pn junction the depletion region extends from $x_p$ ($x_p < 0$, in the p side, acceptor concentration $N_A$) to $x_n$ ($x_n > 0$, in the n side, donor concentration $N_D$), and its width is $x_d = x_n - x_p$. The edges are

$x_n = \left[\dfrac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)N_A}{qN_D(N_A+N_D)}\right]^{1/2}$, $\qquad x_p = -\left[\dfrac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)N_D}{qN_A(N_A+N_D)}\right]^{1/2}$,

where $\phi_0$ is the barrier potential and $v_D$ the applied voltage. Using these relations, develop the expression for the depletion-region width $x_d$ as a function of $N_A$, $N_D$ and $(\phi_0 - v_D)$, in its simplest form.
