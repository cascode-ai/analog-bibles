$x_d = \left[\dfrac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(N_A+N_D)}{qN_AN_D}\right]^{1/2}(\phi_0 - v_D)^{1/2}$。

推导：令 $k = \left[\frac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)}{q(N_A+N_D)}\right]^{1/2}$，则 $x_n = k\sqrt{N_A/N_D}$，$x_p = -k\sqrt{N_D/N_A}$，因此
$x_d = x_n - x_p = k\left(\sqrt{\frac{N_A}{N_D}} + \sqrt{\frac{N_D}{N_A}}\right) = k\,\frac{N_A + N_D}{\sqrt{N_AN_D}}$，
从而 $x_d^2 = \frac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)}{q(N_A+N_D)}\cdot\frac{(N_A+N_D)^2}{N_AN_D} = \frac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(N_A+N_D)(\phi_0 - v_D)}{qN_AN_D}$。对于单边结（one-sided junction，$N_D \gg N_A$），它简化为 $\sqrt{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)/(qN_A)} \approx |x_p|$。
