Capacitor top plates are to be laid out as squares, and the capacitor value is set by the top plate. The edge variation of the top plate is 0.05 µm: the side length of a square top plate may deviate from its drawn value by up to ±0.05 µm, independently for each capacitor. It is desired to match two equal capacitors to an accuracy of 0.1%. Assume that there is no variation in oxide thickness (and neglect fringing). How large would the capacitors have to be to achieve this matching accuracy?
