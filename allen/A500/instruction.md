Assume that the step response of a sample-and-hold circuit is
$$v_{OUT}(t) = V_I\left(1 - e^{-t\cdot BW}\right)$$
where $V_I$ is the magnitude of the input step and $BW$ is the bandwidth of the sample-and-hold circuit in rad/s, equal to $2\pi$ Mrad/s. Assume a worst-case analysis (a full-scale step, $V_I = V_{REF}$), that the whole sampling period is available for settling, and that the output must settle to within ±½ LSB of its final value. Find the maximum number of bits this sample-and-hold circuit can resolve if the sampling frequency is 1 MHz.
