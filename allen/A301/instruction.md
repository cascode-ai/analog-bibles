Design a folded-cascode op amp having the topology of [Fig. 1] but with a p-channel differential input pair: every n-channel transistor of [Fig. 1] becomes p-channel and every p-channel transistor becomes n-channel, and the power supplies are reversed. Thus M3 (tail current source) and the input pair M1, M2 are p-channel and connected to the upper rail, M4 and M5 are n-channel current sinks to the lower rail, M6 and M7 are n-channel cascodes, M8 and M9 p-channel cascodes, and M10, M11 a p-channel cascode-mirror pair connected to the upper rail. The circuit operates from $V_{DD} = 0$ (ground) and $V_{SS} = -2.5$ V. Specifications: slew rate 10 V/µs with a 10-pF load capacitor, output-voltage range −2 V to −0.5 V, $GB = 10$ MHz, input common-mode range −2.5 V to −1 V, differential voltage gain greater than 3000 V/V, power dissipation less than 5 mW. Use $K'_N = 120\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 25\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = |V_{TP}| = 0.5$ V, $\lambda_N = 0.06\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\lambda_P = 0.08\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$ and $L = 0.5\ \mu$m for all devices. Give the W/L of every transistor and verify the specifications, including the gain and the power dissipation.
