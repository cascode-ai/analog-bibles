In the cascode amplifier shown in [Fig. 1], a dc current source $I_{D4}$ is added from $V_{DD}$ to the drain of M1 (the source of M2), so that M1 carries $I_{D1} = I_{D2} + I_{D4}$ while M2 and M3 carry $I_{D2}$. Show how this increases the small-signal voltage gain. Without the added source the gain is approximately $A_v \approx -g_{m1}/g_{ds3} = -\sqrt{2K'_1W_1/(L_1I_D\lambda_3^2)}$; derive the corresponding expression in terms of $I_{D1}$ and $I_{D4}$. If $I_{D2}$ = 10 µA, what value for this current source would increase the voltage gain by a factor of 10? How is the output resistance affected?
