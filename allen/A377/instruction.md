For the two-stage open-loop comparator shown in [Fig. 1] ($V_{DD} = 2.5$ V, $V_{SS} = -2.5$ V, tail current $I_{SS} = 30\ \mu$A, M7 current 234 µA), find the initial (steady-state) values of the first-stage output $v_{o1}$ and of the comparator output $v_{out}$ for each of the following input conditions, before any transient begins. In conditions (1)–(4) the gate of M2 is held at a dc voltage $V_{G2} = 0$ V and $v_{G1}$ is varied (instead of grounding $v_{G1}$ as drawn in [Fig. 1]); in conditions (5)–(8) the gate of M1 is held at $V_{G1} = 0$ V (as drawn in [Fig. 1]) and $v_{G2}$ is varied. Give values or ranges.
(1) $v_{G1}$ slightly above $V_{G2}$, both input transistors conducting ($i_1 < I_{SS}$, $i_2 > 0$);
(2) $v_{G1} \gg V_{G2}$ ($i_1 = I_{SS}$, $i_2 = 0$);
(3) $v_{G1}$ slightly below $V_{G2}$ ($i_1 > 0$, $i_2 < I_{SS}$);
(4) $v_{G1} \ll V_{G2}$ (M1 cut off, e.g. $v_{G1} = V_{SS}$);
(5) $v_{G2}$ slightly above $V_{G1}$ ($i_1 > 0$, $i_2 < I_{SS}$);
(6) $v_{G2} \gg V_{G1}$ (e.g. $v_{G2} = +2.5$ V);
(7) $v_{G2}$ slightly below $V_{G1}$ ($i_1 < I_{SS}$, $i_2 > 0$);
(8) $v_{G2} \ll V_{G1}$ ($i_1 = I_{SS}$, $i_2 = 0$).

Device data: $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = 0.7$ V, $V_{TP} = -0.7$ V; neglect channel-length modulation and the body effect.
