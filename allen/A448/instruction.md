[Fig. 1] shows an 8-bit binary-weighted charge scaling DAC: capacitors $C$, $C/2$, …, $C/128$ for bits $b_0$ (MSB) … $b_7$ (LSB) plus a terminating capacitor $C/128$, with the top plates driving a unity-gain buffer. During φ1 all capacitors are discharged; during φ2 the capacitor of a bit that is 1 is switched to $V_{REF}$ and the others stay at ground, so ideally $v_{OUT} = (b_0/2 + b_1/4 + \cdots + b_7/256)V_{REF}$. If the tolerance of the capacitors in this array is ±0.5 %, what would be the worst-case DNL in units of LSBs and at what transition does it occur?
