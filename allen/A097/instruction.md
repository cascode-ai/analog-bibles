[Fig. 1] shows a voltage reference made by active voltage division: a diode-connected p-channel transistor M2 (source and bulk at $V_{DD}$) in series with a diode-connected n-channel transistor M1 (source and bulk at ground); $V_{REF}$ is taken at their common drain. Derive the sensitivity of $V_{REF}$ to $V_{DD}$, $S_{V_{DD}}^{V_{REF}} = \dfrac{V_{DD}}{V_{REF}}\dfrac{\partial V_{REF}}{\partial V_{DD}}$, and show that it is unity when M1 and M2 have equal transconductance parameters ($\beta_N = K_N'(W/L)_1 = \beta_P = K_P'(W/L)_2$) and equal threshold magnitudes ($V_{TN} = |V_{TP}|$). Use the square-law model and ignore channel-length modulation.
