# Golden Solution - A015

## Reference answer

A circular disc of metal in the center (the size of the transparent circle), still capped by the hardened resist until it is stripped; everywhere else the metal is etched away down to the substrate.

- Negative resist cross-links where UV reaches it, i.e. only inside the transparent circle.
- Developing removes the unexposed resist, leaving a resist disc in the center.
- The etch removes the metal wherever it is not protected, so only the metal under the resist disc remains (after resist removal: a bare metal dot on the substrate).
