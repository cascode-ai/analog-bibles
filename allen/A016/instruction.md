Illustrate the impact on the source and drain diffusions of an ion implant that arrives at an angle of 7° off perpendicular (tilted in the direction along the channel, i.e. perpendicular to the gate stripe). The source/drain implant is self-aligned to the polysilicon gate; assume that the thickness of the polysilicon is 8000 Å and that the outdiffusion (lateral spread of the dopant) from the point of ion impact is 0.07 µm.
