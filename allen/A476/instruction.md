[Fig. 1] shows an 8-bit DAC made of two 4-bit binary-weighted charge-amplifier subDACs: the LSB array (bits $b_4$–$b_7$, capacitors $C$, $C/2$, $C/4$, $C/8$) drives op amp A1 with feedback capacitor $2C$; its output $v_{O1}$ is coupled through $C/8$ into the summing node of op amp A2, which also receives the MSB array (bits $b_0$–$b_3$, capacitors $C$, $C/2$, $C/4$, $C/8$) and has feedback capacitor $2C$. The feedback capacitors are reset during $\phi_1$. If the op amps have a dc gain of $10^4$ and a dominant pole at 100 Hz, at what clock frequency will the effective number of bits (ENOB) equal 7 bits, assuming that the capacitors and switches are ideal? Use a worst-case approach and assume that the time responses of the LSB and MSB stages add to give the overall conversion time, which is one clock period.
