All W/L ratios, $C_c$ and $C_L$ are fixed, and all transistors stay in strong-inversion saturation. On a log–log plot with the vertical axis having a range of $10^{-3}$ to $10^{+3}$ and the horizontal axis having a range of 10 to 1000 µA, plot the low-frequency gain $A_v(0)$, the unity-gain bandwidth GB, the power dissipation $P_{diss}$, the slew rate SR, the output resistance $R_{out}$, the magnitude of the dominant pole $|p_1|$, and the magnitude of the RHP zero $z$, all normalized to their respective values at $I_B = 10$ µA, as a function of $I_B$ from 10 to 1000 µA.
