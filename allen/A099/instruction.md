[Fig. 1] illustrates a current reference: a resistor $R$ from the 5-V supply feeds the diode-connected n-channel transistor M1, and M2 (gate tied to M1's gate, source grounded) delivers $I_{REF}$. The $W/L$ of M1 and M2 is 100/1. The resistor is made from n-well and its nominal value is 500 kΩ at 25 °C. Using an n-well sheet resistivity of 1 kΩ/□ ± 40% and a resistor temperature coefficient of 8000 ppm/°C, calculate the total variation of the output current seen over process, a temperature range of 0–70 °C, and a supply voltage variation of ±10%. Assume that the temperature coefficient of the threshold voltage is −2.3 mV/°C.

Device data (n-channel, at 25 °C): $V_{T0}$ = 0.7 V ± 0.15 V, $K'$ = 110 µA/V² ± 10%; in saturation $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS} - V_T)^2$. Ignore channel-length modulation and the temperature dependence of $K'$.
