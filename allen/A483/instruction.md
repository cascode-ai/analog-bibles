[Fig. 1] shows a serial charge-redistribution DAC with $C_1 = C_2$. $S_1$ places $C_1$ in parallel with $C_2$ (charge redistribution), $S_2$ charges $C_1$ to $V_{REF}$, $S_3$ discharges $C_1$ to zero, and $S_4$ discharges $C_2$; only one switch is closed at a time and each closure lasts long enough for the voltages to settle. What is $v_{C1}$ after the following sequence of switch closures: $S_4$, $S_3$, $S_1$, $S_2$, $S_1$, $S_3$, $S_1$, $S_2$, and $S_1$?
