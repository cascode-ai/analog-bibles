The specification for an oversampled ADC is 16 bits with a bandwidth of 100 kHz and a sampling frequency of 10 MHz. If a single-loop delta-sigma modulator with noise transfer function $(1 - z^{-1})^2$ (two loops) is used, what is the minimum number of bits $B$ for its quantizer to meet the specification? Take the quantizer step as $\Delta = V_{REF}/2^B$ ($\Delta = V_{REF}/2$ for a 1-bit quantizer). Use the linearized model: the quantization error is white with power $\Delta^2/12$ spread over 0 to $f_s/2$, and the decimation filter is an ideal low-pass at the signal bandwidth. Require the in-band quantization noise to be no larger than that of an ideal 16-bit Nyquist-rate quantizer spanning $V_{REF}$, i.e. rms $V_{REF}/(2^{16}\sqrt{12})$.
