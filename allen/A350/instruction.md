Calculate the low-frequency voltage gain, the gain–bandwidth GB, the slew rate SR and the power dissipation $P_{diss}$ of the folded-cascode op amp shown in [Fig. 1] when it is operated in weak inversion. The supplies are $V_{DD} = 1.5$ V and $V_{SS} = -1.5$ V (the ground symbol in the figure is the negative supply $V_{SS}$); $V_{PB1}$, $V_{PB2}$, $V_{NB1}$ and $V_{NB2}$ are ideal bias voltages. The current in each transistor of the differential pair (M1, M2) is 50 nA, and the current in each of the current sources M4 and M5 is 150 nA. All transistors are 10 µm/1 µm, and the load capacitor is $C_L = 2$ pF.

Weak-inversion model: in saturation $i_D = (W/L)\,I_{DO}\exp[v_{GS}/(nV_t)]$, so $g_m = I_D/(nV_t)$, with slope factors $n_p = 1.5$ (p-channel) and $n_n = 2.5$ (n-channel) and $V_t = kT/q = 26$ mV (27 °C). Output resistance $r_{ds} = 1/(\lambda I_D)$ with $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$ and $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$. Neglect the body effect.
