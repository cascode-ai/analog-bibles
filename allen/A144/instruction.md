Device data: n-channel $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; p-channel $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; in saturation $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS}-V_T)^2(1+\lambda v_{DS})$.
