For the CMOS differential amplifier with a current-mirror load shown in [Fig. 1], assume that the current mirror M3–M4 has an output current that is 5 % larger than its input current (take the small-signal current gain of the mirror as 1.05, $i_{d4} = 1.05\,i_{d3}$). Find the small-signal common-mode voltage gain $v_{out}/v_{ic}$ (both gates driven by $v_{ic}$, output unloaded), assuming that $I_{SS}$ is 100 µA and the W/L ratios are 2 µm/1 µm for M1, M2 and M5 and 1 µm/1 µm for M3 and M4. All transistors are saturated; neglect the body effect.

Device data: n-channel $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; p-channel $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; in saturation $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS}-V_T)^2(1+\lambda v_{DS})$.
