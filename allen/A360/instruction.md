For the n-channel differential input stage shown in [Fig. 1] (input pair M1, M2 with gates tied together and driven by the common-mode input voltage $v_{icm}$ at the open terminal; p-channel current-source loads M3, M4; n-channel tail sink M5; the bottom rail is ground and the top rail is $V_{DD}$), the W and L of all transistors are 100 µm and 1 µm, respectively, and the dc current in M5 is 100 µA (50 µA in each of M1, M2, M3 and M4). Find the lowest supply voltage that gives a zero value of ICMR within the power-supply rails, that is, the lowest $V_{DD}$ for which there is (just) one value of $v_{icm}$ with $0 \le v_{icm} \le V_{DD}$ that keeps all five transistors in saturation.

Use $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = 0.7$ V, $V_{TP} = -0.7$ V; neglect the body effect and channel-length modulation.
