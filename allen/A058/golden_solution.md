# Golden Solution - A058

## Reference answer

$C_{GD}$ = CGDO·W = $220\times10^{-12}$ F/m × 22 µm ≈ 4.84 fF.

In cutoff only the gate–drain overlap contributes (no channel), and the width read from the layout is W = 22 µm.

## Grading notes

+2: 4.84 fF (4.8–4.9 fF) using W = 22 µm. +1: correct method with an arithmetic slip. 0: correct expression CGDO·W with W misread by one or two grid squares. −2 (asserted as fact): the overlap multiplied by L (0.44 fF), a share of the channel capacitance added in cutoff, or W misread by a large factor.
