The voltage amplifier using current feedback shown in [Fig. 1] has been designed as follows (all L = 1 µm): M1, M3, M5 and M7 have W = 200 µm and carry 1 mA; M2, M4, M6 and M8 have W = 20 µm and carry 100 µA, so the current amplifier formed by M1–M4 and $R_3$ has a current gain $A_o = 0.1$; $R_1$ = 3.2 kΩ, $R_2$ = 352 kΩ, $R_3$ = 300 Ω; M12 has W/L = 20/1. The capacitance at the output of the current amplifier (drains of M4 and M6, gate of M12) is $C_o$ = 105 fF. (The design target was a gain of −10 with a −3 dB frequency of 50 MHz, i.e., GB = 500 MHz.) Calculate the value of the input pole of the current amplifier, at the node where $R_1$, $R_2$ and $R_3$ meet, and compare it with the magnitude of the output pole.
