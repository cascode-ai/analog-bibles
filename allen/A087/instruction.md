Use Blackman's formula to calculate the small-signal output resistance $R_{out}$ of the stacked MOSFET configuration of [Fig. 1], whose two transistors have identical drain–source drops. M1 (bottom) has its gate driven by $v_{IN}$ (set to zero for this calculation) and its source grounded; M2 is stacked on M1, and its gate is connected through the dc source $V_{GS2}$ to the midpoint of two equal resistors $R$ in series from the output node to ground. Express your answer in terms of all the pertinent small-signal parameters, and then simplify your answer if $g_m > g_{ds} > 1/R$. Assume the MOSFETs are identical and ignore body effects.

Blackman's formula: $R_x = R_x(k = 0)\,\dfrac{1 + RR(\text{port shorted})}{1 + RR(\text{port open})}$, where $RR$ is the return ratio of the controlled source with parameter $k$.
