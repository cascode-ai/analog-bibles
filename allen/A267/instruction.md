Design a two-stage CMOS op amp similar to the one shown in [Fig. 1] but with a p-channel input pair: p-channel M1–M2 with a p-channel tail current source M5 (to $V_{DD}$), an n-channel current-mirror load M3–M4, an n-channel common-source output transistor M6 with a p-channel current-source load M7, and a Miller capacitor $C_c$. Use the following specifications, with a phase margin of 60° (obtained by placing the RHP zero at ≥ 10GB and the output pole at ≥ 2.2GB) and all channel lengths 1 µm:

$A_v > 5000$ V/V, $V_{DD} = 2.5$ V, $V_{SS} = -2.5$ V, GB = 5 MHz, $C_L = 10$ pF, SR > 10 V/µs, $V_{out}$ range = ±2 V, ICMR = −1 V to +2 V, $P_{diss} \le 2$ mW.

Choose the same currents as in the corresponding n-channel-input design: $I_5 = 30$ µA in the first stage and $I_6 = I_7 = 95$ µA in the second stage. Device data: $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{T0} = 0.7 \pm 0.15$ V (n), $-0.7 \pm 0.15$ V (p) (use the worst-case values for the ICMR), $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $C_{ox} = 2.47\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$; ignore the body effect. Give the W/L of every transistor and $C_c$, and check the specifications.
