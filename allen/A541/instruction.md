A first-order delta-sigma modulator is shown in [Fig. 1]. The quantization noise added at the output has the rms value $\Delta/\sqrt{12}$ and is white, its power spread uniformly over 0 to $f_s/2$ (one-sided spectral density $(\Delta/\sqrt{12})\sqrt{2/f_s}$). Find the magnitude of the output spectral noise with $V_{in}(z) = 0$, and determine the bandwidth of a 10-bit ADC if the sampling frequency $f_s$ is 10 MHz and $k = 1$. Repeat for $k = 0.5$. Take the bandwidth of a 10-bit ADC as the largest $f_B$ for which the rms output quantization noise in the band 0 to $f_B$ (ideal low-pass decimation filter) does not exceed that of an ideal 10-bit quantizer whose full-scale range equals the quantizer step $\Delta$, i.e. $\Delta/(2^{10}\sqrt{12})$.
