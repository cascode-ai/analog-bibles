The threshold voltage of an n-channel MOS transistor with source–bulk voltage $v_{SB}$ is

$V_T = V_{T0} + \gamma\left(\sqrt{2|\phi_F| + v_{SB}} - \sqrt{2|\phi_F|}\right)$, with $\gamma = \sqrt{2q\varepsilon_{Si}N_A}/C_{ox}$,

where $\phi_F$ is the Fermi potential of the p-type substrate (doping $N_A$) and $C_{ox}$ the gate-oxide capacitance per unit area (for a p-channel device the same holds with all polarities reversed). Explain in your own words why the magnitude of the threshold voltage increases as the magnitude of the source–bulk voltage increases. (The source–bulk pn diode remains reverse biased.)
