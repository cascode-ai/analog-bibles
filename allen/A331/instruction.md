Five differential-output op amps are shown: [Fig. 1] a two-stage Miller op amp with current-source loads; [Fig. 2] a two-stage Miller op amp with push–pull output stages; [Fig. 3] a folded-cascode (class A) op amp; [Fig. 4] a class B, folded-cascode, push–pull op amp; [Fig. 5] a class AB op amp with a cross-coupled differential input stage and cascoded outputs. Compare them from the viewpoint of output common-mode range: give the maximum and minimum output voltage $V_o(\max)$ and $V_o(\min)$ of each in terms of $V_{DD}$, $V_{SS}$ (the lower supply, drawn as ground in [Fig. 3] and [Fig. 4]) and the saturation voltage $V_{ON}$ (the same for all transistors), assuming that all input differential currents are identical.
