A binary-weighted DAC using a charge amplifier is shown in [Fig. 1]: capacitors $C$, $2C$, …, $2^{N-1}C$ drive the inverting input of the op amp, $C_X = 2^NC$ is the feedback capacitor and the switch across it is the reset switch. At the beginning of the conversion all capacitors are discharged; then, if a bit is 1, its capacitor is connected to $V_{REF}$ and if the bit is 0, to ground. The output magnitude is to be $(b_0/2 + b_1/4 + \cdots + b_{N-1}/2^N)V_{REF}$. Identify the switches by $b_i$, where $i = 0$ is the MSB and $i = N-1$ is the LSB.
