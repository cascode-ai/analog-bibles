# Golden Solution - A057

## Reference answer

$W$ = 22 µm and $L$ = 2 µm.

The channel is where the polysilicon gate crosses the n⁺ active area: the poly strip is 2 µm wide in the direction of current flow from drain to source (L), and the active area it crosses is 22 µm tall (W). The poly extension beyond the active area and the gate-contact pad do not count.

## Grading notes

+2: W = 22 µm and L = 2 µm. +1: one dimension off by 1 µm from a grid miscount, with the right identification. 0: one of W and L correct and the other missing or wrong by more than 1 µm, or both off by about 1 µm with the right identification. −2 (asserted as fact): both W and L wrong by more than 1 µm, whatever the cause (grid miscounts with the right identification of the directions included), W and L interchanged, W taken as the full length of the poly line including its extensions, or L taken as the 9 µm diffusion length or the 20 µm active-area width.
