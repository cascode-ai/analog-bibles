# Golden Solution - A436

## Reference answer

SR ≥ 0.5 V/µs.

The largest output change is the full-scale step 0 → 255/256 V ≈ 1 V; slewing it in 2 µs needs SR ≥ 0.996 V/2 µs ≈ 0.5 V/µs.

## Grading notes

+2: ≈ 0.5 V/µs (0.49–0.51). +1: correct reasoning with a minor slip. −2 (asserted as fact): a value off by a factor of 2 or more (e.g. 0.25 V/µs from a half-scale step, 1 V/µs), or a value derived from GB instead of the step size.
