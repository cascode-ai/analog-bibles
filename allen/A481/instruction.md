The circuit shown in [Fig. 1] is a double-decoder DAC used with a comparator. The resistor string consists of four equal resistors $R$ between $V_{REF}$ and ground; a switch labelled with a variable ($A$, $B$, $C$, $D$) is closed when that variable is 1 and a switch labelled with its complement is closed when it is 0; $\phi_1$ and $\phi_2$ are nonoverlapping clock phases. The op amp is ideal; during $\phi_1$ its output is connected to its inverting input, and during $\phi_2$ it operates open loop as a comparator. Find an expression for $v_X$ in terms of $V_{Analog}$, $V_1$, $V_2$ and $V_{REF}$ when the $\phi_2$ switches are closed (following a $\phi_1$ phase). If $A = 1$, $B = 0$, $C = 1$ and $D = 1$, will the comparator output be high or low if $V_{Analog} = 0.8V_{REF}$?
