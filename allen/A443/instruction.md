[Fig. 1] shows an N-bit charge scaling DAC. During φ1 the top plates (through the φ1 switch) and the bottom plates of all capacitors are grounded, so all capacitors are discharged. During φ2 the φ1 switch is open and switch $S_i$ connects the bottom plate of its capacitor to $V_{REF}$ if $b_i = 1$ or to ground if $b_i = 0$; the terminating capacitor $C/2^{N-1}$ stays grounded. The top-plate node drives a unity-gain buffer whose output is $v_{OUT}$. Investigate the influence of a load capacitor $C_L$ connected in parallel with the terminating capacitor (from the top-plate node to ground; it is also discharged during φ1). Find an expression for $v_{OUT}$ during φ2 as a function of $C$, $C_L$, the digital bits $b_i$, and $V_{REF}$.
