[Fig. 1] illustrates a reference circuit that provides an interesting reference voltage output. The current $I$ flows through the diode-connected M3 ($W/L$ = 4) stacked on the diode-connected M1 ($W/L$ = 4). M4 ($W/L$ = 1) has its gate tied to M3's gate and its drain at 5 V; M2 ($W/L$ = 4) has its gate tied to M1's gate and its source grounded; the source of M4 and the drain of M2 form the output $V_{REF}$. All are n-channel transistors with the same $K'$ and $V_T$. Derive an expression for $V_{REF}$. Ignore the body effect and channel-length modulation, and assume all transistors are saturated.
