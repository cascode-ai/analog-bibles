The bias battery $V_{BIAS}$ is connected between the gates of M5/M7 and the negative supply rail. Find $v_{out}/v_{ground}$ and identify its low-frequency gain and its roots.

Use the two-node small-signal model of the two-stage op amp: first-stage transconductance $g_{mI} = g_{m1} = g_{m2}$ and output conductance $G_I = g_{ds2}+g_{ds4}$; second-stage transconductance $g_{mII} = g_{m6}$ and output conductance $G_{II} = g_{ds6}+g_{ds7}$; neglect the capacitance at the first-stage output, and take the output-node capacitance as $C_L$.
