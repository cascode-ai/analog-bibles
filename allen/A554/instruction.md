Two first-order ΔΣ modulators are multiplexed as shown in [Fig. 1]: both sample the same input $v_{in}$, each has its own 1-bit quantizer and its own feedback loop, $\Delta\Sigma_1$ provides its quantizer output during clock phase $\phi_1$ and $\Delta\Sigma_2$ during clock phase $\phi_2$, where $\phi_1$ and $\phi_2$ are the nonoverlapping phases of the same clock, and the two outputs are combined into the PCM output. The rms noise in the band 0 to $f_B$ of a general $L$-loop ΔΣ modulator sampling at $f_S$ is
$n_o = \dfrac{\Delta}{\sqrt{12}}\,\dfrac{\pi^L}{\sqrt{2L+1}}\left(\dfrac{2f_B}{f_S}\right)^{L+0.5}$.
Assume that the quantization level of each quantizer is $\Delta = 0.5V_{REF}$ and find the dynamic range in decibels, defined as $20\log_{10}(V_{REF}/n_{o,total})$ with $n_{o,total}$ the rms in-band quantization noise of the combined output referred to the input, if the clock frequency is 100 MHz (each modulator is clocked at 100 MHz) and the bandwidth of the resulting ADC is 1 MHz.
