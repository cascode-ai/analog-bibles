# Detailed solution - A093

First identify the mechanism of the layout error: a mismatch in the source resistances of the two transistors. Then remove it by making both source paths equal and short.

### Step 1 — Cause of the error

The gate draws no current, and with both devices saturated and $V_{DS1}=V_{DS2}$ the drain-side resistances do not change the currents. The four GRD contacts carry the current of both devices and shift both sources equally. What differs is the n⁺ path from each source to the contacts at the lower right: M1's source current flows down, around a corner and along the bottom strip (about 2.2 squares, ≈ 77 Ω at 35 Ω/sq), while M2's source sits right above the contacts (about 0.7 square, ≈ 25 Ω). M1 is degenerated by about 50 Ω more, so for the same gate voltage M2 gets more gate drive. The current error is

$$
\frac{\Delta I}{I}\approx g_m\,\Delta R=\frac{2I}{V_{GS}-V_T}\,\Delta R\approx(332\ \mu\mathrm S)(52\ \Omega)\approx1.7\%,
$$

with $I_{out}$ about 1.7% high.

### Step 2 — Layout remedies

The cure is to make the source resistances of the two transistors equal and small, so that both see the same source potential:

- Place a row of GRD contacts along the source edge of each transistor, with the same number of contacts at the same distance from the gate, and strap them with metal to GRD. Source current then flows only a short, identical distance through diffusion.
- Lay the two devices out identically and symmetrically about the GRD connection, for example as mirror images on either side of a central ground contact, or interdigitated/common-centroid fingers that share the same source environment.
- Avoid carrying source current through long diffusion runs: use short, wide diffusion, more contacts, and metal over the diffusion.

### Step 3 — Circuit-level measures

Since the error scales as $g_m\Delta R=2I\Delta R/(V_{GS}-V_T)$, a larger overdrive (smaller $g_m/I$) reduces the sensitivity to any residual mismatch. Alternatively, deliberately equal and larger degeneration resistors in both sources swamp the small layout difference. These help, but equalizing the source paths in the layout removes the systematic error at its origin.
