[Fig. 1] illustrates a current reference circuit. M3 and M4 are p-channel transistors (M3 diode-connected) forming a mirror and are identical in size. M1, M2 and M5 are n-channel transistors with grounded sources; the sizes of M1 and M2 are different. M4's drain connects to the gate of M1 and to the top of $R$; the bottom of $R$ connects to the drain of M1 and to the gates of M2 and M5; M2's drain feeds M3. Derive an expression for the output current $I_{out}$ (the drain current of M5). Assume all transistors are saturated and ignore channel-length modulation and the body effect.
