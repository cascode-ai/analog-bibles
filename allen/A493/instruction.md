The iterative algorithmic DAC shown in [Fig. 1] is to convert the digital word 11001 (MSB to LSB). The output is zeroed before the conversion; the bits are applied serially, LSB first, a bit 1 adding $+V_{REF}$ (switch $A$) and a bit 0 adding $-V_{REF}$ (switch $B$) to the output of the feedback amplifier, and the sum is held by the sample-and-hold. If the gain of the 0.5 amplifier is 0.7, at which bit conversion does an error occur? Compare the output after each bit conversion with its ideal value; an error occurs when the deviation exceeds $\pm\frac12$ LSB of the bits converted so far, 1 LSB being the spacing between adjacent possible output levels after that many conversions.
