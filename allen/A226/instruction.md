Prove that a Class B push–pull amplifier has a maximum efficiency of 78.5% for a sinusoidal signal. Take complementary output transistors between $V_{DD}$ and $V_{SS} = -V_{DD}$, each conducting for one half of the period, driving a load $R_L$ connected to ground with an output $v_{OUT} = V_p\sin\omega t$; the efficiency is the power delivered to $R_L$ divided by the power drawn from the supplies.
