[Fig. 1] is the transistor-level implementation of a curvature-corrected, low-TC voltage reference. Its generators deliver: $I_{PTAT}' = V_{PTAT}/R_1$ with $V_{PTAT} = V_t\ln10$ (from D1 ×1 and D2 ×10); $I_{CTAT}' = V_{D3}/R_2$, where D3 is biased by a PTAT current (α = 1); and $I_{\alpha=0}' = V_{D4}/R_4$, where D4 is biased by a temperature-independent current (α = 0). The reference generator forms $V_{REF} = R_5(I_{24} + I_{25} - I_{22})$, where M24, M25 and M23 copy the $I_{PTAT}'$, $I_{CTAT}'$ and $I_{\alpha=0}'$ bus currents (bus devices M3, M8 and M18) in proportion to their $W/L$, and the M21–M22 mirror subtracts M23's current. Diode voltages follow $V_D(T) = V_{G0} - \frac{T}{T_0}\left[V_{G0} - V_D(T_0)\right] - (\gamma - \alpha)V_t\ln\frac{T}{T_0}$ with $V_{G0}$ = 1.205 V, γ = 3.2, $V_{D3}(T_0) = V_{D4}(T_0)$ = 0.7 V and $V_t$ = 0.026 V at room temperature $T_0$.

The original design used 1:1 copies and resistor ratios to obtain zero temperature coefficient at room temperature, cancellation of the nonlinear $T\ln T$ terms, and $V_{REF}$ = 1.0 V (with $R_4$ = 10 kΩ).
