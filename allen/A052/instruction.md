Calculate the values of $C_{GB}$, $C_{GS}$ and $C_{GD}$ for an MOS device that has a $W$ of 5 µm and an $L$ of 1 µm, for all three regions of operation (off, saturation and nonsaturation).

Data: gate-oxide capacitance $C_{ox} = 24.7\times10^{-4}$ F/m² ($t_{ox}$ = 140 Å); gate–source and gate–drain overlap capacitances CGSO = CGDO = $220\times10^{-12}$ F/m (per unit channel width); gate–bulk overlap capacitance CGBO = $700\times10^{-12}$ F/m (per unit channel length, both edges together). Neglect lateral diffusion and width reduction, i.e. take $W_{eff} = W$ and $L_{eff} = L$.
