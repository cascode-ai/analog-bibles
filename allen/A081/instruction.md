The four MOS diodes shown in [Fig. 1] form a switch connecting $v_{in}$ to a 1-kΩ load resistor when $I_{control}$ = 200 µA (ON) and disconnecting it when $I_{control}$ = 0 (OFF). M1 and M2 are n-channel devices with $W/L$ = 100/1, M3 and M4 are p-channel devices with $W/L$ = 200/1; each is diode-connected. $v_{in}$ has no dc component. Find the small-signal value of the on-resistance of this switch configuration when $I_{control}$ = 200 µA. What is the switch loss in dB when the switch is on? If this switch (the four MOS diodes and the controlling current) were inserted in place of the MOSFET switch in [Fig. 2] (a single n-channel MOSFET, gate driven by the clock $V_\phi$, that connects $v_{in}$ to the capacitor $C$), compare the performance of the two switch realizations.

Device data: n-channel $V_T = 0.7$ V, $K' = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$; p-channel $V_T = -0.7$ V, $K' = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$. Ignore channel-length modulation and the body effect.
