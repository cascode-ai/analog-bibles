An NMOS latch [Fig. 1] uses transistors with $W/L = 10\ \mu\mathrm{m}/1\ \mu$m, each carrying a dc latch current of 10 µA. Before the latch is enabled, the difference between its output voltages is $\Delta V_i$. Find the time from enabling the latch until the output difference $\Delta V_{out}$ reaches $0.5(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$, for $\Delta V_i = 0.01(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$ and for $\Delta V_i = 0.1(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$. Assume the capacitance at each latch node is dominated by the gate–source capacitance $C \approx \tfrac23WLC_{ox}$ and that the transistor's output resistance is large ($g_mR \gg 1$).

Device data: $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $C_{ox} = 24.7\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{F/m^2}$.
