In the circuit of [Fig. 1] the auxiliary op amp, with the two 100-kΩ resistors, servoes the output of the shaded op amp under test (its inverting input sums the currents from $v_{OUT}$ and from the upper-right source $v_{SET}$, whose negative terminal is at the resistor), and the auxiliary output $v_{OS}$ drives the noninverting input of the op amp under test through the 10-kΩ/10-Ω divider. The other two sources $v_{SET}$ are in series with $V_{DD}$ and $V_{SS}$, with their common node grounded. (a) All three $v_{SET}$ sources are first set to +1 V and the voltage $v_{OS}$ is recorded as $V_{OS1}$; then all three are set to −1 V and $v_{OS}$ is recorded as $V_{OS2}$. Verify that the common-mode rejection ratio of the op amp under test is $\mathrm{CMRR} = 2000\ \mathrm{V}/|V_{OS1}-V_{OS2}|$. (b) If instead the three $v_{SET}$ sources are equal small-signal voltages $v_{icm}$, verify that $\mathrm{CMRR} = 1000\,v_{icm}/v_{os}$, where $v_{os}$ is the resulting small-signal voltage at $v_{OS}$.
