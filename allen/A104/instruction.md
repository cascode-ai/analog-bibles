Further assume that the area ratio for the bipolar transistors is 10:1: Q1a and Q1b (the stack in the branch with $R_1$) each have 10 times the emitter area of Q2a, Q2b and Q3. Design the components to achieve an output reference voltage of 1.262 V (with zero temperature coefficient at room temperature, $T_0$ = 300 K). What advantage, if any, is there in stacking the bipolar transistors?

The bias current $I$ (= $I_1$ = $I_2$) and the emitter–base voltage $V_{EB3}$ of Q3 are not specified: give $R_1$ in terms of $I$ and $R_2/R_1$ in terms of $V_{EB3}$, and evaluate them for $V_{EB3}$ = 0.6 V and $I$ = 10 µA.
