For an abrupt (step) pn junction, the depletion-region edges are $x_n = \left[\frac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)N_A}{qN_D(N_A+N_D)}\right]^{1/2}$ and $x_p = -\left[\frac{2\varepsilon_{Si}(\phi_0 - v_D)N_D}{qN_A(N_A+N_D)}\right]^{1/2}$, with $x$ measured from the metallurgical junction (positive into the n side), $\phi_0$ the barrier potential and $v_D$ the applied voltage. Redevelop these two expressions for a junction whose impurity concentration is the diffused profile shown in [Fig. 1] instead of a step. Assume that the depletion region lies entirely within the linearly graded part of the profile, where the net impurity concentration is $N_D(x) - N_A(x) = a\,x$ ($a$ is the grading constant, in cm⁻⁴), and that the depletion region is free of mobile carriers.
