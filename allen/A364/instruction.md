Two n-channel source-coupled pairs, one using regular (enhancement) transistors and the other with depletion transistors having $V_T = -1$ V, are connected with their gates common (the two inputs are shared) and the sources taken to individual current sinks. The transistors are modeled by $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = 0.7$ V (regular devices), γ = 0.4 V^1/2, $2|\phi_F| = 0.7$ V, λ = 0.04 V⁻¹ (L = 1 µm), except that the threshold is −1 V for the depletion transistors; p-channel transistors, if needed, have $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TP} = -0.7$ V. Design the combined source-coupled pairs to achieve a rail-to-rail input common-mode range for a 0–2 V power supply. Try to keep the equivalent input transconductance constant over the ICMR. Show how to recombine the drain currents from each source-coupled pair in order to drive a single-ended second stage.
