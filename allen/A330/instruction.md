Compare them from the viewpoint of input common-mode range: give $V_{ic}(\max)$ and $V_{ic}(\min)$ of each in terms of $V_{DD}$, $V_{SS}$ (the lower supply, drawn as ground in [Fig. 3] and [Fig. 4]), the threshold $V_T$ (take $|V_{TP}| = V_{TN} = V_T$) and the saturation voltage $V_{ON}$ (the same for all transistors), assuming that all input differential currents are identical.
