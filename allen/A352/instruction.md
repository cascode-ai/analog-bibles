[Fig. 1] shows a dynamically biased differential input stage operating in weak inversion. M18–M21 are identical to M3 and M4, M22–M27 are all identical, and M28 and M29 are scaled from M26 and M27: $W_{28}/L_{28} = A\,(W_{26}/L_{26})$ and $W_{29}/L_{29} = A\,(W_{27}/L_{27})$. With $v_{IN} = v_{i1} - v_{i2} > 0$ (so $i_2 > i_1$), M28 adds $A(i_2 - i_1)$ to the tail current $I_5$ and M29 carries no current; for $v_{IN} < 0$ M29 adds $A(i_1 - i_2)$. In weak inversion the input transistors obey $i_2/i_1 = \exp[v_{IN}/(nV_t)]$. The output current of the op amp is $i_{OUT} = b\,(i_2 - i_1)$, where b is the current-mirror gain to the output.

Derive the expression for $i_{OUT}$ as a function of $v_{IN}/(nV_t)$, A, b and $I_5$ (for $v_{IN} > 0$). Then, for A = 2 and b = 1, at what value of $v_{IN}/(nV_t)$ will $i_{OUT} = 5I_5$?
