A comparator consists of an amplifier cascaded with a latch as shown in [Fig. 1]. The amplifier has a voltage gain of 10 V/V and $f_{-3dB} = 100$ MHz (single pole), and the latch has a time constant of 10 ns. The maximum and minimum voltage swings of the amplifier and latch are $V_{OH}$ and $V_{OL}$. When should the latch be enabled after the application of a step input of $0.05(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$ to the amplifier to get the minimum overall propagation delay? What is the value of the minimum propagation delay? The propagation delay of the latch is $t_p = \tau_L\ln\left(\dfrac{V_{OH}-V_{OL}}{2v_{il}}\right)$, where $v_{il}$ is the latch input (the amplifier output at the moment the latch is enabled).
