The two-stage op amp with a Class A output buffer shown in [Fig. 1] has been designed with the following values (all channel lengths 1 µm): $V_{DD} = 2.5$ V, $V_{SS} = -2.5$ V, $C_L = 100$ pF, $R_L = 500\ \Omega$. In the output buffer, M11 ($W_{11} = 1467$ µm) sinks 1 mA through Q10; M8 (p-channel, $W_8 = 146$ µm) supplies 100 µA to the p-channel source follower M9 ($W_9/L_9 = 10$ µm/1 µm, bulk at $V_{DD}$), whose source drives the base of the npn emitter follower Q10 (collector at $V_{DD}$; $\beta_F = 100$, $I_S = 10^{-14}$ A, $C_\pi = 10$ pF, $C_\mu = 1$ pF). The unity-gain bandwidth of the op amp is GB = 5 MHz. Find the dominant roots of the MOS follower and of the BJT follower, and compare these root locations with GB.
