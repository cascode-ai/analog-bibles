For the two-stage op amp shown in [Fig. 1] (supplies +1.5 V and −1.5 V, $C_c = 5$ pF, load 20 pF; W/L values in µm/µm as marked, all $L = 1\ \mu$m), find the low-frequency PSRR and all roots (poles and zeros) of the positive and negative power-supply rejection ratios. All transistors operate in saturation. The bias current $I_8$ is set by the diode-connected M9 and M8 across the full supply; for this analysis treat the resulting gate–source bias voltage of M5 and M7 as independent of the supply ripple (i.e., as a fixed bias voltage referenced to the negative supply). Neglect the capacitance at the first-stage output; the output-node capacitance is the 20-pF load; the gate–drain capacitance of M7 is its overlap capacitance $C_{GDO}W_7$ with $C_{GDO} = 220$ pF/m.

Device data: $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = 0.7$ V, $V_{TP} = -0.7$ V, $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$; in saturation $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS}-V_T)^2(1+\lambda v_{DS})$. Ignore the body effect.
