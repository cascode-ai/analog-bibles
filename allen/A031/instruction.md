The simple first-order RC low-pass filter shown in [Fig. 1] is to be built with a polysilicon resistor and an MOS capacitor. The polysilicon resistor has a sheet resistance of 50 Ω/□ ± 30% and is 5 µm wide. The MOS capacitor is 2 fF/µm² ± 10%. The −3 dB frequency of the low-pass filter is 1 MHz. Choose the size of the resistor (the number of squares, N) to minimize the total area of the filter, including both the resistor and the capacitor (ignore contacts and spacing). Find the area of the resistor and of the capacitor in µm², and their values. Use nominal values.
