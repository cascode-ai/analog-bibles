A two-stage, Miller-compensated CMOS op amp with GB = 1 MHz has a dominant pole due to the Miller compensation, a second (output) pole $p_2$ with $|p_2| = 2GB$, another pole at $-3GB$, and an RHP zero at 20GB. A nulling resistor is now used in series with the compensation capacitor so that the zero is moved into the left half-plane and cancels $p_2$. What is the new phase margin (feedback factor 1, large dc gain; evaluate the phase at $\omega = GB$; neglect any additional pole introduced by the nulling resistor)?
