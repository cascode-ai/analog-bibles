A two-stage charge scaling DAC is shown in [Fig. 1]. All capacitors are discharged before the conversion; then the switch designated by $b_i$ is connected to $V_{REF}$ if the $i$th bit is 1 and to ground if the $i$th bit is 0 ($b_0$ is the MSB, $b_5$ the LSB). The amplifier is an ideal unity-gain buffer. The coupling capacitor is designed for an ideal 6-bit DAC, nominal value $C_x = 8C/7$, but it is in error by $\Delta C_x$ (actual value $C_x + \Delta C_x$). Find an expression for $v_{OUT}$ in terms of $C_x$, $\Delta C_x$, the bits $b_{i-1}$ ($i = 1 \ldots 6$) and $V_{REF}$.
