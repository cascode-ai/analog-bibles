The circuit shown in [Fig. 1] contains a voltage-controlled voltage source of value $A_v(v_{in} - v_1)$, with finite gain $A_v$, connected in series between the top of $R_1$ and the output node, its + terminal on the $R_1$ side. The upper input terminal is not connected to the rest of the circuit: $v_{in}$ acts only as the controlling voltage of the source and the input draws no current. Use the circuit-reduction technique (a voltage-controlled voltage source in series between two parts of a circuit, controlled by a voltage of one of them, is replaced by a short circuit while the resistances of one part are scaled by the factor $1 + A_v$) to solve for $v_{out}/v_{in}$.
