In the voltage amplifier of [Fig. 1], the current mirror M1–M2 acts as a current amplifier: the input current enters the drain of the diode-connected M1, and the output is the drain of M2; M5 and M6 are p-channel current sources mirrored from M4, which carries $I_{BIAS}$. Design the currents in M1, M2, M5 and M6 and the W/L ratios to give an output resistance (at the drains of M2 and M6) of at least 1 MΩ and an input resistance (at the drain of M1) of less than 1 kΩ. (This would allow a voltage gain of −10 to be achieved using $R_1$ = 10 kΩ and $R_2$ = 1 MΩ.) The values of $I_{BIAS}$ and $W_4/L_4$ are yours to choose. Use $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_N = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\lambda_P = 0.05\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$ (L = 1 µm).
