[Fig. 1] shows an 8-bit charge scaling DAC built from two 4-bit binary-weighted capacitor arrays: an LSB array (with a terminating capacitor) and an MSB array, joined by a series scaling capacitor $C_s$, with the top node of the MSB array buffered by a unity-gain amplifier. During $\phi_1$ all capacitors are discharged; during $\phi_2$ each capacitor's bottom plate goes to $V_{REF}$ if its bit is 1 and to ground if it is 0. Design a 10-bit two-stage charge scaling DAC similar to this one using two 5-bit sections with a capacitive attenuator between the stages. Give all capacitances in terms of $C$, which is the smallest capacitor of the design, and show that the design gives a 10-bit binary output.
