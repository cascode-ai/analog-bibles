[Fig. 1] and [Fig. 2] show transistor-level realizations of the two gain-enhancement amplifiers used in an enhanced-gain folded-cascode op amp. In [Fig. 1] (upper amplifier) the n-channel pair M1, M2 with tail current sink M3 has M2's gate held at the fixed voltage $V_{DD} - V_{SD}(\mathrm{sat})$ and M2's drain at $V_{DD}$; M1's drain is folded into the p-channel cascode M5 (biased by the p-channel current source M4), and the output node is loaded by the n-channel current source M6. In [Fig. 2] (lower amplifier) the p-channel pair M1, M2 with tail source M3 has M2's gate held at $V_{DS}(\mathrm{sat})$ and M2's drain grounded; M1's drain is folded into the n-channel cascode M5 (biased by the n-channel current sink M4), and the output is loaded by the p-channel current source M6. Use small-signal analysis to check the validity of the gain estimates $A_v(\mathrm{upper}) \approx -0.5g_{m1}r_{ds6} = -0.5g_{mN}r_{dsN}$ and $A_v(\mathrm{lower}) \approx -0.5g_{m1}r_{ds6} = -0.5g_{mP}r_{dsP}$, where $g_{mN}, r_{dsN}$ ($g_{mP}, r_{dsP}$) are the parameters of the n-channel (p-channel) transistors.
