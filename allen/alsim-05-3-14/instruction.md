Design a cascode CMOS amplifier using the circuit and the design relationships shown in [Fig. 1] (n-channel input M1, n-channel cascode M2 whose gate voltage $V_{GG2}$ is generated by the diode-connected M5 fed by $I_{Bias}$, p-channel current-source load M3 mirrored from the diode-connected M4 fed by $I_{Bias}$) for the following specifications: $V_{DD}$ = 5 V, $P_{diss} \le$ 0.5 mW, $|A_v| \ge$ 100 V/V, $v_{OUT}(\max)$ = 3.5 V, $v_{OUT}(\min)$ = 1.5 V (the output range over which all transistors stay saturated must cover 1.5 V to 3.5 V), and a slew rate greater than 5 V/µs for a 5-pF capacitor load. Verify your design by simulation.

Use SPICE level-1 models: n-channel VTO = 0.7 V, KP = 110 µA/V², GAMMA = 0.4 V$^{1/2}$, PHI = 0.7 V, LAMBDA = 0.04 V⁻¹; p-channel VTO = −0.7 V, KP = 50 µA/V², GAMMA = 0.57 V$^{1/2}$, PHI = 0.8 V, LAMBDA = 0.05 V⁻¹. These λ values apply to L = 1 µm; for devices with L ≥ 2 µm use λ = 0.01 V⁻¹. Bulks of n-channel devices at ground, of p-channel devices at $V_{DD}$.
