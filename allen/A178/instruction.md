Six versions of a cascode amplifier are shown in [Fig. 1] (Circuits 1–6). Assume that $K'_N = 2K'_P$, $\lambda_P = 2\lambda_N$, all W/L ratios of all devices are equal, and all bias currents in each device are equal: every current-source and cascode transistor carries the same current $I$ (by KCL the input transistor of Circuits 5 and 6 then carries $2I$). Use the square-law model with $r_{ds} = 1/(\lambda I_D)$, neglect the body effect, and assume $g_mr_{ds} \gg 1$. Here $v_o(\max)$ and $v_o(\min)$ are the highest and lowest output voltages for which all transistors remain in saturation (input at its quiescent bias). Identify which circuit or circuits have the lowest $v_o(\max)$.
