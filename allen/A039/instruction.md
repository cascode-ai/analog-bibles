A forward-biased silicon pn diode follows $i_D \approx I_s\exp(v_D/V_t)$, where $V_t = kT/q$ (25.9 mV at $T = 300$ K) and the saturation current depends on temperature as $I_s = KT^3\exp(-V_{G0}/V_t)$, with $V_{G0} = 1.205$ V (the bandgap voltage of silicon) and $K$ independent of temperature. At room temperature ($T = 300$ K), assume $v_D = 0.7$ V and find the fractional temperature coefficients $\frac{1}{X}\frac{dX}{dT}$ of $I_s$ and of $v_D$ (for $v_D$, with the diode current held constant).
