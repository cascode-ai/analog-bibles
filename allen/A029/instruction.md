Find the zero-bias threshold voltage $V_{T0}$ and the body factor $\gamma$ of an n-channel MOS transistor with an n⁺ polysilicon gate of doping $N_D = 1\times10^{19}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, gate-oxide thickness $t_{ox} = 200$ Å, p-type substrate doping $N_A = 7\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, and a positive charge at the oxide–silicon interface of $10^{10}$ charges/cm².

Use $V_{T0} = \phi_{MS} - 2\phi_F - Q_{b0}/C_{ox} - Q_{ss}/C_{ox}$ with $Q_{b0} = -\sqrt{2q\varepsilon_{Si}N_A\,2|\phi_F|}$, $\gamma = \sqrt{2q\varepsilon_{Si}N_A}/C_{ox}$, $\phi_F = \phi_F(\text{substrate}) = -\frac{kT}{q}\ln(N_A/n_i)$, $\phi_F(\text{gate}) = \frac{kT}{q}\ln(N_D/n_i)$, $\phi_{MS} = \phi_F(\text{substrate}) - \phi_F(\text{gate})$, $Q_{ss} = q\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{C/cm^2}$. Constants: $kT/q = 0.0259$ V, $n_i = 1.45\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, $\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-14}$ F/cm, $\varepsilon_{Si} = 11.7\varepsilon_0$, $\varepsilon_{ox} = 3.9\varepsilon_0$.
