[Fig. 1] shows a current amplifier with two resistors. The current amplifier has zero input resistance at both inputs (both input terminals sit at 0 V) and infinite output resistance, and its output current is $i_o = A_i(i_p - i_n)$, where $i_p$ and $i_n$ are the currents flowing into its p and n inputs (directions as drawn) and $i_o$ is the current delivered by the amplifier output into the $v_o$ node (for this relation take $i_o$ positive out of the amplifier, opposite to the arrow drawn in the figure). The output terminal $v_o$ is otherwise unloaded. Find $v_o/v_s$ and compare it with the result $v_{out} = \dfrac{R_2}{R_1}\left(\dfrac{A_i}{1+A_i}\right)v_{in}$ obtained when the source drives the p input through $R_1$ and $R_2$ is connected from the output to the n input.
