A folded-cascode op amp has a low-frequency differential gain $A_{vd}(0) = 3678$ V/V, an output resistance $R_{out} = 7.44$ MΩ, and a dominant pole at the output set by the load capacitance, $|p_d| = 1/(R_{out}C_L)$; with $C_L = 10$ pF its GB is modest. Its nondominant poles, which do not depend on $C_L$, are $p_A = p_B = -0.702\times10^9$ rad/s, $p_6 = -1.652\times10^9$ rad/s, $p_9 = -4.176\times10^9$ rad/s and $p_8 = -555\times10^9$ rad/s. Extend the GB of this op amp as much as possible while maintaining a 60° phase margin. What is the maximum GB, and what is the minimum value of $C_L$ for this maximum GB?
