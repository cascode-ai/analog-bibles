[Fig. 1] shows an N-bit charge scaling DAC. During φ1 all capacitors are discharged (top plates through the φ1 switch and all bottom plates grounded). During φ2 switch $S_i$ connects the bottom plate of its capacitor to $V_{REF}$ if $b_i = 1$ or to ground if $b_i = 0$; the terminating capacitor $C/2^{N-1}$ stays grounded; the top-plate node drives a unity-gain buffer. A load capacitor $C_L$ is connected in parallel with the terminating capacitor (from the top-plate node to ground; also discharged during φ1). What is the largest value of $C_L/C$ possible before an error (more than ±0.5 LSB) is introduced in this DAC?
