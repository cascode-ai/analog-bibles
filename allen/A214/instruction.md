An output amplifier uses an n-channel source follower: n-channel M1 (W/L = 100 µm/1 µm) has its drain at +2.5 V and its gate driven by $v_{IN}$; its source is the output $v_{OUT}$, which is connected to an ideal 200-µA dc current sink returning to −2.5 V, a 50-pF capacitor to ground and a 10-kΩ resistor to ground. Assume that $v_{IN}$ can vary from −2.5 V to +2.5 V. Ignore bulk effects. Find the negative slew rate, $SR^-$, when $v_{OUT}$ = 0 V, in volts per microsecond.
