A comparator consists of two identical amplifiers cascaded with a latch. Each amplifier has a gain of 5 V/V and a single-pole −3 dB bandwidth of $1/\tau_L$ (rad/s), where $\tau_L$ is the latch time constant. The latch, once enabled with an input difference $\Delta V_i$, regenerates as $\Delta V_i e^{t/\tau_L}$, so its delay to half the output swing is $\tau_L\ln[(V_{OH}-V_{OL})/(2\Delta V_i)]$. What is the normalized propagation delay time $t_p/\tau_L$ if the applied input step is $0.05(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$ and the voltage applied to the latch is $\Delta V_i = 0.1(V_{OH}-V_{OL})$?
