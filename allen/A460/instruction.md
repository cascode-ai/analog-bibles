The 4-bit DAC shown in [Fig. 1] has $M = 2$ and $K = 2$: an ideal 2-bit MSB subDAC converts $b_0 b_1$ ($b_0$ is the MSB), an ideal 2-bit LSB subDAC converts $b_2 b_3$ ($b_3$ is the LSB), both subDACs use the reference $V_{REF}$, and the output of the LSB subDAC is divided by a divisor (ideally $2^M = 4$) before it is added to the output of the MSB subDAC. If the divisor has an incorrect value of 2, express the $\pm INL$ and the $\pm DNL$ in terms of LSBs of the 4-bit DAC and determine whether or not the DAC is monotonic. Repeat if the divisor is 6.

Take the INL at each code as the deviation of the actual output from its ideal value, and the DNL at each transition between adjacent codes as the deviation of the actual step from 1 LSB; give the most positive and most negative values of each.
