$A_v = \dfrac{g_{m1}}{g_{m1}+g_{mbs1}+g_{ds1}+g_{ds2}} = \dfrac{1}{1+\eta_1+(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)\sqrt{I_DL_1/(2K'_NW_1)}}$——基本上与 $I_D$ 无关（≈ $1/(1+\eta_1)$），仅随 $I_D$ 增大而略有下降；$R_{out} \approx \dfrac{1}{g_{m1}+g_{mbs1}} \propto I_D^{-1/2}$；$|p| \approx \dfrac{g_{m1}+g_{mbs1}}{C_1+C_2} \propto I_D^{1/2}$。
