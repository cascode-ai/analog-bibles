Find expressions for the maximum and minimum output-voltage swing of the push–pull source follower shown in [Fig. 1] (n-channel M1 with bulk at $V_{SS}$, p-channel M2 with bulk at $V_{DD}$, two floating batteries $V_{BIAS}$, load $R_L$ from the output to ground). Assume that $v_{IN}$ can be driven over the full range $V_{SS} \le v_{IN} \le V_{DD}$ (so the gate of M1 can reach $V_{DD}+V_{BIAS}$ and the gate of M2 can reach $V_{SS}-V_{BIAS}$), that at each extreme the conducting transistor is saturated while the other is off, and that $V_{BIAS}$ does not exceed the thresholds. Let $\beta_1 = K'_N(W/L)_1$ and $\beta_2 = K'_P(W/L)_2$; include the body effect through the threshold voltages $V_{T1}$ and $|V_{T2}|$.
