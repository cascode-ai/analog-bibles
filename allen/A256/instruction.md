A two-stage, Miller-compensated CMOS op amp with GB = 1 MHz has a dominant pole due to the Miller compensation, a second (output) pole $p_2$ with $|p_2| = 2GB$ for the original load capacitance $C_L$, another pole at $-3GB$, and an RHP zero at 20GB. A nulling resistor in series with the compensation capacitor has been designed so that the zero is moved into the left half-plane and cancels $p_2$ for the original $C_L$. What is the phase margin if $C_L$ is increased by a factor of 4 (feedback factor 1, large dc gain; evaluate the phase at $\omega = GB$; neglect any additional pole introduced by the nulling resistor)?
