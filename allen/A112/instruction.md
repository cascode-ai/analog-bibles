An n-channel transistor M1 ($W_1/L_1 = 2\ \mu$m$/1\ \mu$m, source and bulk grounded, input $v_{IN}$ applied to its gate) has a 10 kΩ load resistor connected from its drain, which is the output $v_{OUT}$, to $V_{DD} = 5$ V. Using a graphical (load-line) construction on the $i_D$–$v_{DS}$ output characteristics of M1, obtain the voltage-transfer function $v_{OUT}$ versus $v_{IN}$ of M1 with the 10 kΩ load resistor. What are the maximum and minimum output voltages if the input is taken from 0 to 5 V?

Device data for M1: $V_T = 0.7$ V, $K' = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0.04\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$. In saturation $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS}-V_T)^2(1+\lambda v_{DS})$; in the nonsaturation (triode) region $i_D = \frac{K'W}{L}\left[(v_{GS}-V_T)v_{DS} - \frac{v_{DS}^2}{2}\right]$.
