A pipeline algorithmic ADC is shown in [Fig. 1], with $V_{REF}$ = 5 V and a sampled input of 2.00 V. Each stage compares its input $V_{i-1}$ with zero: the stage bit is 1 ($b_{i-1} = +1$) if $V_{i-1} > 0$ and 0 ($b_{i-1} = -1$) otherwise, and passes $V_i = A V_{i-1} - b_{i-1}V_{REF}$ to the next stage, where the gain $A$ should be 2 but the gain-of-two amplifiers of all stages actually have a gain of 2.1. The stage bits form the output word MSB first. Find the 8-bit digital output word and its equivalent analog voltage $V_{analog} = V_{REF}\left[b_0 2^{-1} + b_1 2^{-2} + \cdots + b_7 2^{-8}\right]$ ($b_i = \pm1$), and compare them with the result for an ideal gain of 2.
