[Fig. 1] shows the differential input stage of a high-gain comparator with internal hysteresis: M3 and M4 are diode-connected p-channel loads, the cross-coupled p-channel transistors M6 (gate at $v_{o1}$, drain at $v_{o2}$) and M7 (gate at $v_{o2}$, drain at $v_{o1}$) provide positive feedback, and M8–M5 mirror $I_{BIAS}$ into the tail. All channel lengths are 1 µm; $W_1 = W_2 = 10\ \mu$m, $W_6 = W_7 = 10\ \mu$m, $W_3 = W_4 = 2\ \mu$m, and M5 and M8 are identical so that the tail current is $i_5 = I_{BIAS} = 50\ \mu$A. $V_{DD} = 2.5$ V, $V_{SS} = -2.5$ V. The gate of M1 is tied to ground and the input $v_{in}$ is applied to the gate of M2; the output is $v_{o2}$. Calculate the positive and negative trip points ($V_{TRP}^+$ for increasing $v_{in}$, $V_{TRP}^-$ for decreasing $v_{in}$), neglecting channel-length modulation. Confirm using a simulator (e.g., a slow triangular $v_{in}$ between −0.5 V and +0.5 V, or forward and backward dc sweeps).

Hand-analysis data: $K'_N = 110\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $K'_P = 50\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{TN} = 0.7$ V, $V_{TP} = -0.7$ V.
