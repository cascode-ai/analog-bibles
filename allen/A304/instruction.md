The enhanced-gain folded-cascode op amp shown in [Fig. 1] has a p-channel input pair M1, M2 (tail M3), n-channel current sinks M4 and M5, n-channel cascodes M6 and M7 whose gates are driven by the "lower" gain-enhancement amplifiers, p-channel cascodes M8 and M9 whose gates are driven by the "upper" gain-enhancement amplifier, and the p-channel current sources M10 and M11 (the shaded blocks are the enhancement amplifiers). Take the gain magnitude of the upper enhancement amplifier as $0.5g_{mN}r_{dsN}$ and that of each lower enhancement amplifier as $0.5g_{mP}r_{dsP}$. Find the small-signal differential voltage gain, the output resistance, the dominant pole, and the GB of the op amp.
