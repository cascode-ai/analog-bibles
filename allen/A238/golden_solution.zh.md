$A_v = \dfrac{g_{m1}+g_{m2}}{g_{m1}+g_{m2}+1/R_L}$ = 0.5 V/V（带 500-Ω 负载）；$R_{out} = 1/(g_{m1}+g_{m2})$ = 500 Ω。
