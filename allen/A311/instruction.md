The shaded part of the op amp shown in [Fig. 1] is its output buffer. Assume $V_{DD} = -V_{SS} = 2.5$ V and that the currents $I_{17}$ and $I_{20}$ are both 100 µA. At the quiescent point the output $v_{out}$ and the common gate node of M18 and M19 (the drains of M10 and M15) are both at 0 V. Design $W_{18}/L_{18}$ and $W_{19}/L_{19}$ to get $V_{SG18} = V_{GS19} = 1.5$ V. Design $W_{21}/L_{21}$ and $W_{22}/L_{22}$ so that the quiescent current in M21 and M22 is also 100 µA. The bulk connections are as drawn: n-channel bulks to $V_{SS}$, p-channel bulks to $V_{DD}$.
