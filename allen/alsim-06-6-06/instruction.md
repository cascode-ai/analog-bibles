Using two identical op amps, show how to use SPICE to obtain a voltage that is proportional to the CMRR of the op amp, rather than the inverse relationship given by the usual direct configuration (in which the op amp is connected as a unity-gain follower with identical common-mode sources $V_{cm}$ in series with both inputs, so that $V_{out}/V_{cm} \approx |A_c|/A_v = 1/\mathrm{CMRR}$).
