The iterative algorithmic ADC shown in [Fig. 1] converts as follows. In the first iteration switch S1 applies the sampled input $V_{in}^*$ to the sample-and-hold; in each iteration the held voltage is multiplied by the gain-of-2 amplifier to give $V_a$. If $V_a > V_{REF}$ the bit is 1 and $V_b = V_a - V_{REF}$; otherwise the bit is 0 and $V_b = V_a$. $V_b$ is then fed back through S1 to the sample-and-hold for the next iteration, and the bits come out MSB first; $T$ is the time for one iteration. The analog input is $0.8V_{REF}$ and five iterations are performed. The gain-of-2 amplifier actually has a gain of 2.1. Give $V_a$ and $V_b$ in each of the five iterations (sketch them versus $t/T$), the 5-bit digital word and its equivalent analog voltage, and state which bit, if any, is in error compared with an ideal gain of 2.
