A two-stage charge scaling DAC is shown in [Fig. 1]. All capacitors are discharged before the conversion; then the switch designated by $b_i$ is connected to $V_{REF}$ if the $i$th bit is 1 and to ground if the $i$th bit is 0 ($b_0$ is the MSB, $b_5$ the LSB). The amplifier is an ideal unity-gain buffer. Design $C_x$ in terms of $C$, the unit capacitor, to achieve a 6-bit two-stage charge scaling DAC.
