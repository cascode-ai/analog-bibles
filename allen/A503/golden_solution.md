# Golden Solution - A503

## Reference answer

A parasitic-insensitive noninverting (positive) SC integrator whose input capacitor samples either $v_{in}^*$ or $-V_{REF}$ on $\phi_1$:

- Op amp with its noninverting input grounded and an integrating capacitor $C_I$ from the inverting input to the output ($v_{int}$); a reset switch across $C_I$ (or a pre-integration of a positive voltage) initializes $v_{int}$ to $V_{th}$.
- Input capacitor $C_1$: on $\phi_1$ its left plate is connected to the source bus and its right plate to ground; on $\phi_2$ its left plate goes to ground and its right plate to the op amp's inverting input.
- Switch 1 (bus to $v_{in}^*$) and switch 2 (bus to $-V_{REF}$) are merged with the $\phi_1$ left-plate switch: during the run-up the left plate is connected to $v_{in}^*$ on $\phi_1$ (switch driven by $\phi_1\cdot$RUNUP), during the run-down to $-V_{REF}$ on $\phi_1$ (driven by $\phi_1\cdot\overline{\text{RUNUP}}$); RUNUP is cleared by the counter carry.
- Charge conservation at the inverting input over one clock period gives $v_{int}(n) = v_{int}(n-1) + \dfrac{C_1}{C_I}v_s(n-1)$, i.e. $H(z) = \dfrac{C_1}{C_I}\dfrac{z^{-1}}{1 - z^{-1}}$ (positive, delayed integrator), where $v_s$ is the selected source. So $v_{int}$ rises by $(C_1/C_I)v_{in}^*$ per period during the $N_{REF}$-period run-up and falls by $(C_1/C_I)V_{REF}$ per period during the run-down; the comparator compares $v_{int}$ with $V_{th}$ at the end of each $\phi_2$. The SC integrator corresponds to $K = C_1/(C_IT)$, and $N_{out} = N_{REF}v_{in}^*/V_{REF}$ still does not depend on $C_1/C_I$.
- Equivalent alternative: keep a positive reference and use an inverting branch for it (left plate to ground on $\phi_1$ and to $+V_{REF}$ on $\phi_2$, right plate grounded on $\phi_1$ and connected to the inverting input on $\phi_2$), which gives $v_{int}(n) = v_{int}(n-1) - (C_1/C_I)V_{REF}$ without a negative reference.

## Grading notes

+2: a correct SC integrator (op amp with feedback capacitor, switched input capacitor) whose phasing gives a noninverting integration of the selected source (or an equivalent correct scheme, e.g. the phase-swapped inverting branch for $+V_{REF}$), with switches 1 and 2 connecting $v_{in}^*$ and $-V_{REF}$ to the input capacitor in the sampling phase of a two-phase clock, and the per-period change $(C_1/C_I)v_s$ (or $H(z)$) stated. +1: correct circuit and phasing but the difference equation missing or with a minor sign or delay slip. Initializing $v_{int}$ (by a reset switch across $C_I$ or by stating $v_{int}(0) = V_{th}$) is good practice but not required for +2: the question does not ask for it, and the run-down returns $v_{int}$ to $V_{th}$ at the end of each conversion. 0: a recognizable SC integrator whose phasing makes it inverting for $v_{in}^*$ while $-V_{REF}$ is still used (so the two slopes have the same sign), with the rest sound. −2 (asserted as fact): a circuit that cannot integrate (no feedback capacitor, or the input capacitor permanently connected), phases that connect the source directly to the virtual-ground node without a capacitor, or a claim that the conversion result depends on $C_1/C_I$.
