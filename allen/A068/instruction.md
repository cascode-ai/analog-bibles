Another way to approximate the transition from strong inversion to weak inversion is to find the current at which the weak-inversion transconductance and the strong-inversion transconductance are equal. Using this method, with the weak-inversion approximation $i_D \approx \frac{W}{L}I_{D0}\exp\!\left(\frac{v_{GS}}{n(kT/q)}\right)$ (n the subthreshold slope factor) and the strong-inversion saturation model $i_D = \frac{K'W}{2L}(v_{GS} - V_T)^2$, derive an expression for the drain current at the transition between strong and weak inversion.
