A ΔΣ modulator has the noise transfer function $NTF_Q(z) = (1 - z^{-1})^2$ and a 1-bit quantizer with step $\Delta$; the quantization error is white with power $\Delta^2/12$ spread uniformly over 0 to $f_s/2$, and the full-scale input range of the converter equals $\Delta$. If the sampling frequency $f_s$ is 10 MHz, find the maximum signal bandwidth $f_B$ in hertz if the ΔΣ modulator is used in a 16-bit oversampled analog-to-digital converter. Take 16-bit performance to mean that the rms quantization noise in the band 0 to $f_B$ (ideal low-pass decimation filter) does not exceed that of an ideal 16-bit quantizer with full-scale range $\Delta$, i.e. $\Delta/(2^{16}\sqrt{12})$.
