An 8-bit current DAC is shown in [Fig. 1]: bit $b_i$ connects its current sink ($I$, $I/2$, …, $I/128$ for $b_0$ … $b_7$) to the op amp's inverting input when $b_i = 1$ and to ground when $b_i = 0$; $R$ = 1 kΩ and $I$ = 0.5 mA, so the full-scale range is 1 V. Assume that all aspects of the DAC are ideal except for the op amp. If the differential voltage gain of the op amp has a single-pole frequency response with a dc gain of $10^5$, find the unity-gain bandwidth GB, in hertz, that gives a worst-case conversion time of 2 µs (output settled to within ±0.5 LSB of its final value).
