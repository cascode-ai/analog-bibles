Draw the first-order (single-pole) time response of an inverting comparator with a 20 µs propagation delay time, where the propagation delay is measured from the 50 % point of the input transition to the 50 % point of the output transition. The input is
$v_{in} = 0$ for $t < 5\ \mu$s,
$v_{in} = 5\,(t - 5\ \mu\mathrm{s})$ V (with $t$ in µs, i.e. a 5 V/µs ramp) for $5\ \mu\mathrm{s} < t < 7\ \mu$s,
$v_{in} = 10$ V for $t > 7\ \mu$s.
Express the output in terms of the output levels $V_{OH}$ and $V_{OL}$.
