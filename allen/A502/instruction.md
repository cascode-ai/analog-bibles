The dual-slope ADC shown in [Fig. 1] works as follows. After the positive integrator has been reset so that its output $v_{int}$ equals the comparator threshold $V_{th}$, switch 1 connects the sampled input $v_{in}^*$ (positive) to the integrator for a fixed number $N_{REF}$ of clock periods (until the counter produces a carry). Switch 2 then connects $-V_{REF}$ to the integrator, and the counter counts clock periods until $v_{int}$ has fallen back to $V_{th}$; that count $N_{out}$ is the converter output.
