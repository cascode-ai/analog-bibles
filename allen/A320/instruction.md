A two-stage op amp uses Miller compensation with a nulling resistor. Besides its dominant pole it has a second pole at $-10\times10^6$ rad/s and two higher poles, both at $-100\times10^6$ rad/s. The zero of the compensation network (a right-half-plane zero without the nulling resistor) is used to cancel the second pole, and the load capacitance stays constant. Find the GB that gives a 60° phase margin. If the input transconductance is 500 µA/V, what is the value of $C_c$?
