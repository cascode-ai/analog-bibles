[Fig. 1] shows an N-bit charge scaling DAC. During φ1 the top plates (through the φ1 switch) and the bottom plates of all capacitors are grounded, so all capacitors are discharged. During φ2 the φ1 switch is open and switch $S_i$ connects the bottom plate of its capacitor to $V_{REF}$ if $b_i = 1$ or to ground if $b_i = 0$; the terminating capacitor $C/2^{N-1}$ stays grounded; the top-plate node drives a unity-gain buffer. For a given digital word, develop an equivalent circuit of the capacitor array during φ2 that contains only $V_{REF}$ and two capacitors, give the two capacitances in terms of $C$ and the bits, and use it to obtain $v_{OUT}$.
