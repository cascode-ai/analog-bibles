Determine the worst-case input offset voltage of the circuit of [Fig. 1] (a differential pair with current-mirror load, tail current 100 µA), with the bipolar transistors replaced by MOS transistors: $Q_1$, $Q_2$ by n-channel $M_1$, $M_2$ and $Q_3$, $Q_4$ by p-channel $M_3$ ($M_3$ diode-connected) and $M_4$. Assume the worst-case $W/L$ mismatches in the transistors are ±5 percent and the worst-case $V_t$ mismatches are ±10 mV (in each pair). Assume the dc output voltage to ground is $V_{SUP} - |V_{GS3}|$. Also assume that $(W/L)_1 + (W/L)_2 = 20$ and $(W/L)_3 + (W/L)_4 = 60$. Use $k'_n = \mu_nC_{ox} = 194\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$ and $k'_p = \mu_pC_{ox} = 64.7\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$; neglect channel-length modulation in computing the overdrives. (Mismatch = difference divided by average.)
