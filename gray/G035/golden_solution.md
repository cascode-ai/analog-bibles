# Golden Solution - G035

## Reference answer

Estimates: $r_b \approx 5\ \Omega$ (extrinsic part), $r_c \approx 165\ \Omega$, $C_{je0} \approx 3$ pF, $C_{\mu0} \approx 1.1$ pF, $C_{cs0} \approx 8$ pF (≈ 10 pF if the per-area capacitances are computed from the step-junction formula rather than read as round numbers).

- Per-area zero-bias capacitances (one-sided step junction, $C_j/A = \sqrt{q\epsilon N/(2\psi_0)}$): emitter-base ($N = 10^{17}$, 0.7 V) ≈ 1.0–1.1×10⁻³ pF/μm²; collector-base and epi-substrate/isolation-sidewall (epi $10^{15}$ is the light side) ≈ 1.0–1.25×10⁻⁴ pF/μm²; buried layer–substrate (substrate $10^{16}$ is the light side, 0.52 V) ≈ 3.3–4×10⁻⁴ pF/μm².
- $r_b$: each of the four 42-μm emitter edges faces a base contact: $R = 100\ \Omega/\square\times(\text{spacing}/42\ \mu\mathrm{m})$ = 19, 25, 22, 21 Ω for spacings 7.9, 10.7, 9.1, 8.9 μm; four paths in parallel ≈ 5.4 Ω. (The intrinsic, pinched base under the emitters is not covered by the data: with contacts on both sides it is $R_{\square,\text{pinch}}W/(12L)$ per emitter, about 50–250 Ω for the two emitters in parallel at a typical 2–10 kΩ/□, so it would dominate at low currents; it is shorted out by current crowding at the high currents this device is designed for, so the extrinsic value is the one used here.)
- $r_c = r_{c1} + r_{c2} + r_{c3}$, each modeled as a truncated pyramid with vertical flow, $R = \rho T\ln(a/b)/[WL(a-b)]$ ($a$, $b$ = bottom-to-top ratios of width and length; bottom edges = top edges + 2T, or the buried-layer edge if closer):
  - $r_{c1}$ (epi under each emitter): $T$ = 6 μm − ≈1 μm zero-bias depletion = 5 μm, top 28 × 46 μm (emitter + side diffusion), bottom 38 × 56 μm (top + 2T), $a$ = 1.36, $b$ = 1.22 → ≈ 150 Ω per emitter; two in parallel ≈ 75 Ω.
  - $r_{c2}$ (buried layer): $20\ \Omega/\square\times 55/131 \approx 8\ \Omega$.
  - $r_{c3}$ (epi under the collector contact): $T$ = 6.5 μm, top 141 × 21 μm, bottom ≈ 147 × 37 μm (limited by the buried-layer edges except 2T toward the base) → ≈ 80 Ω.
  - Total ≈ 165 Ω in the forward-active region (≈ 90 Ω, i.e. $r_{c2}+r_{c3}$, in saturation, where the epi under the emitter is conductivity-modulated).
- $C_{je0}$: bottom $2\times24\times42 = 2016\ \mu$m²; sidewall (quarter-cylinder) $2\times2(24+42)\times2.5\times\pi/2 = 1037\ \mu$m²; total ≈ 3050 μm² × 1×10⁻³ ≈ 3.0 pF (3.3 pF with 1.09×10⁻³).
- $C_{\mu0}$: bottom 137 × 64 ≈ 8800 μm²; sidewall $2(137+64)\times3\times\pi/2 \approx 1900\ \mu$m²; ≈ 10,700 μm² × 10⁻⁴ ≈ 1.1 pF (1.3 pF with 1.23×10⁻⁴).
- $C_{cs0}$: isolation sidewall $17\times2(183+147)\times\pi/2 \approx 17{,}600\ \mu$m² → 1.8 pF; buried layer incl. side diffusion $(131+16)\times(89+16) \approx 15{,}400\ \mu$m² × 3.3×10⁻⁴ → 5.1 pF; remaining epi-substrate bottom $183\times147 - 15{,}400 \approx 11{,}500\ \mu$m² → 1.1 pF; total ≈ 8 pF.

## Grading notes

Five quantities, each about one fifth of the credit. Accept, for hand estimates of this kind: $r_b$ 3–12 Ω for the extrinsic (contact-to-emitter-edge) resistance with the four parallel paths recognized (an answer that also lists a separately identified intrinsic component for low currents is fine; since the question gives no pinched-base sheet resistance, an answer that adds the intrinsic part to $r_b$ with an openly stated assumed value and a sound formula, such as $R_{\square,\text{pinch}}W/(12L)$ per emitter, is also right for this part provided the extrinsic part is computed as above); $r_c$ 80–300 Ω with the three-part decomposition (vertical epi under the emitters, buried layer, epi under the contact); $C_{je0}$ 2.2–4.2 pF; $C_{\mu0}$ 0.8–1.5 pF; $C_{cs0}$ 5.5–11 pF including the buried-layer–substrate, epi-substrate and isolation-sidewall parts. +2: all five within these ranges with sound geometry. +1: four of five, or all five with one component of a capacitance omitted (e.g. sidewalls) that keeps it just outside range. 0: about half right. −2 (asserted as fact): only one emitter counted, the two emitters' $r_{c1}$ placed in series, $r_b$ of hundreds of ohms from a single path with wrong geometry, collector-substrate capacitance computed with the buried layer ignored and the epi treated as heavily doped, or per-area capacitances off by an order of magnitude.
