Estimate the series base resistance $r_b$, the series collector resistance $r_c$, the base-emitter capacitance, the base-collector capacitance, and the collector-substrate capacitance (zero-bias values) of the high-current $npn$ transistor structure whose layout is shown in [Fig. 1] (distance scale in μm; mask layers coded as in the legend). This structure is typical of those used as the output transistor in operational amplifiers that must supply up to about 20 mA.

Approximate mask dimensions read from the layout: isolated epi pocket 183 μm × 147 μm; base diffusion 137 μm × 64 μm; two emitters, each 24 μm × 42 μm, with three base-contact stripes (about 11 μm × 42 μm) beside and between them, the 42-μm emitter edges lying 8–11 μm (about 9 μm on average) from the facing base-contact edges; $n^+$ collector-contact diffusion 137 μm × 17 μm, 14 μm from the base edge, its center about 55 μm from the emitter centers; $n^+$ buried layer about 131 μm × 89 μm on the mask, extending from under the base to under the collector contact.

Vertical doping profile: $n$-type epitaxial layer 17 μm thick, 5 Ω-cm ($N_D \approx 10^{15}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$); $p$ base diffusion 3 μm deep with a sheet resistance of 100 Ω/□; $n^+$ emitter diffusion (also used for the collector contact) 2.5 μm deep, the base doping at the emitter-base junction being about $10^{17}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$; the buried layer (sheet resistance 20 Ω/□) begins 9 μm below the surface, leaving 6 μm of epi between the base-collector junction and the buried layer and 6.5 μm between the bottom of the collector-contact diffusion and the buried layer; $p$ substrate $10^{16}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$. Lateral (side) diffusion beyond the mask edges: about 2 μm for the emitter-diffused regions and about 8 μm for the buried layer. Built-in potentials: 0.7 V (emitter-base), 0.55 V (collector-base), 0.52 V (collector-substrate). Use $\epsilon_{Si} = 1.04\times10^{-12}$ F/cm and $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
