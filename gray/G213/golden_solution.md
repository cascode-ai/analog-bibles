# Golden Solution - G213

## Reference answer

$v_o/i_i = -\beta(\beta + 1)R_L = -100\times101\times3\ \mathrm{k\Omega} = -30.3$ MΩ.

$i_{b2} = (\beta+1)i_i$ and only $Q_2$'s collector current $\beta(\beta+1)i_i$ flows in $R_L$.

## Grading notes

+2: −30.3 MΩ (±3%). +1: −30.0 MΩ (β² used) or a sign slip with correct magnitude. 0: correct method with an arithmetic error. −2 (asserted as fact): $Q_1$'s collector current added, an assumed source resistance, or a transresistance of the order of $R_L$.
