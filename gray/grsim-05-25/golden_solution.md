## Grading notes

Accept any design meeting every stated specification with correct supporting calculations: power ≤ 70 mW counting $I_Q + 21I_{\text{BIAS}} + 21I_{\text{BIASP}}$ (with $I_Q = 100I_{\text{BIAS}}$), $A \le 5$ from $g_{m11}/g_{m16}$ with the correct currents (2.5 and 7.5 × $I_{\text{BIAS}}$), gain error ≤ 1% from $1/(1 + 2Ag_mR_L)$, all four current sources ≥ 0.5 V in the stated output ranges (worst case with $M_{15}$ at $10I_{\text{BIAS}}$), complementary sizing for the bottom half, and a distortion argument (maximum quiescent current within the power budget, $A$ at or near 5, loop gain ≥ 100). A correct simulation shows a slope within 1% of unity, no crossover, and ≈ 70 mW standby. +1: valid design with one check missing (e.g. the $I_{\text{BIAS}}$ voltage) or a non-maximal $I_Q$ without justification; 0: power or gain-error budget computed incorrectly but the method recognisable; −2: a design violating a hard spec (power > 70 mW, $A > 5$, gain error > 1%) presented as compliant.
