# Detailed solution - G031

Sheet resistance is the resistance of a square piece of a uniform layer measured between opposite edges. It follows from the resistance of a rectangular bar with length equal to width.

### Step 1 — Sheet resistance of a uniform layer

A bar of resistivity $\rho$, length $L$, width $W$ and thickness $T$ has $R=\rho L/(WT)$. For a square, $L=W$, and the size cancels:

$$
R_\square=\frac{\rho}{T}.
$$

### Step 2 — Numerical value

With $T=5\ \mu\mathrm m=5\times10^{-4}$ cm:

$$
R_\square=\frac{1\ \Omega\,\mathrm{cm}}{5\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{cm}}=2000\ \Omega/\square=2\ \mathrm{k\Omega}/\square .
$$

A resistor of $n$ squares in this layer therefore has $R=n(2\ \mathrm{k\Omega})$.
