The wafer-sort yield of three IC processes A, B and C is approximated by $Y_{ws} = \exp(-A/A_0)$, where $A$ is the die area and $A_0 \approx 37{,}000$ mil² for process A, ≈ 20,000 mil² for process B and ≈ 12,000 mil² for process C. Use this expression, together with the gross-die-per-wafer curves shown in [Fig. 1], to develop an analytical expression for the cost of silicon per unit area in the finished product (cost of a good finished unit divided by its die area) as a function of die size, die-fab yield $Y_{df}$, final-test yield $Y_{ft}$, packaging-and-test cost per packaged die $C_p$, and wafer-fab cost per wafer $C_w$, for each of the three processes A, B and C.
