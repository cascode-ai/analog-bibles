The circuit in [Fig. 1] is an emitter-coupled pair $Q_1$–$Q_2$ (npn) with a pnp current-mirror load $Q_3$–$Q_4$ ($Q_3$ diode-connected), biased by an ideal 100-µA tail current source. The input $v_i$ is applied to the base of $Q_1$, the base of $Q_2$ is grounded, and the output is taken at the collectors of $Q_2$ and $Q_4$. Now 2-kΩ resistors are inserted in series with the emitters of $Q_3$ and $Q_4$ (between each emitter and $+V_{SUP}$). Determine the unloaded small-signal voltage gain $v_o/v_i$ and the output resistance.

Device data: npn $\beta_F = \beta_0 = 200$, $V_A = 130$ V; lateral pnp $\beta_F = \beta_0 = 50$, $V_A = 50$ V; $V_T = 26$ mV; $r_b = 0$, $r_\mu \to \infty$. All transistors operate in the forward-active region; neglect base currents in the bias calculation.
