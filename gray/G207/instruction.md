The ac schematic of a common-collector–common-emitter stage is shown in [Fig. 1] ($Q_1$ has its collector at ac ground and its emitter drives the base of $Q_2$; $Q_2$'s collector is the output), with $R_S = 100\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $R_L = 3\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$. Use the zero-value time-constant method to calculate the −3-dB frequency of the voltage gain $v_o/v_i$. Data: $\beta = 100$, $f_T = 500$ MHz at $I_C = 1$ mA, $C_\mu = 0.4$ pF, $C_{je} = 2$ pF, $C_{cs} = 1$ pF (collector to ac ground), $r_b = 0$, $r_o = \infty$, $I_{C1} = 10\ \mu$A, $I_{C2} = 1$ mA, $V_T = 26$ mV; $C_\mu$, $C_{cs}$ and $C_{je}$ are values at the bias point and are the same for both transistors. Use $C_\pi = \tau_Fg_m + C_{je}$ with the forward transit time $\tau_F$ obtained from $f_T$ ($\omega_T = g_m/(C_\pi + C_\mu)$).
