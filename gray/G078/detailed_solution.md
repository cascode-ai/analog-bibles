# Detailed solution - G078

Only the loads are mismatched; the transistors and their $g_m$ are identical and $r_o\to\infty$, so each drain current depends only on the gate and tail voltages, not on the load. Find the drain currents for a pure differential and a pure common-mode input with the half-circuit argument, then multiply by the unequal loads to form all four gains. Write $\bar R=(R_1+R_2)/2=10\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $\Delta R=R_1-R_2=0.2\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$.

### Step 1 — Drain currents for a differential input

Apply $v_{i1}=v_{id}/2$, $v_{i2}=-v_{id}/2$. With identical transistors and infinite $r_o$, the drain currents are $g_m(v_{i1}-v_t)$ and $g_m(v_{i2}-v_t)$, where $v_t$ is the tail voltage. KCL at the tail: their sum flows into $r_{\text{tail}}$,

$$
g_m(v_{i1}+v_{i2}-2v_t)=\frac{v_t}{r_{\text{tail}}}\quad\xrightarrow{\;v_{i1}+v_{i2}=0\;}\quad v_t=0 .
$$

So the tail stays at ac ground and

$$
i_{d1}=-i_{d2}=g_m\frac{v_{id}}2 .
$$

The load mismatch does not disturb this, because with $r_o\to\infty$ the loads do not influence the drain currents.

### Step 2 — Gains for a differential input

Each output is $v_{o}=-i_dR$ (supply at ac ground):

$$
v_{o1}=-g_m\frac{v_{id}}2R_1,\qquad v_{o2}=+g_m\frac{v_{id}}2R_2 .
$$

$$
A_{dm}=\frac{v_{o1}-v_{o2}}{v_{id}}=-\frac{g_m(R_1+R_2)}2=-g_m\bar R=-(1\ \mathrm{mA/V})(10\ \mathrm{k\Omega})=-10,
$$

$$
A_{dm-cm}=\frac{(v_{o1}+v_{o2})/2}{v_{id}}=-\frac{g_m(R_1-R_2)}4=-\frac{(1\ \mathrm{mA/V})(0.2\ \mathrm{k\Omega})}4=-0.05 .
$$

### Step 3 — Drain currents for a common-mode input

Apply $v_{i1}=v_{i2}=v_{ic}$. Both drain currents are equal, $i_d=g_m(v_{ic}-v_t)$, and together they flow into $r_{\text{tail}}$: $v_t=2i_dr_{\text{tail}}$. Thus each device is degenerated by $2r_{\text{tail}}$:

$$
i_{d1}=i_{d2}=\frac{g_mv_{ic}}{1+2g_mr_{\text{tail}}},\qquad 1+2g_mr_{\text{tail}}=1+2(1\ \mathrm{mA/V})(1\ \mathrm{M\Omega})=2001 .
$$

### Step 4 — Gains for a common-mode input

$$
A_{cm}=\frac{(v_{o1}+v_{o2})/2}{v_{ic}}=-\frac{g_m\bar R}{1+2g_mr_{\text{tail}}}=-\frac{10}{2001}=-5.0\times10^{-3},
$$

$$
A_{cm-dm}=\frac{v_{o1}-v_{o2}}{v_{ic}}=-\frac{g_m\Delta R}{1+2g_mr_{\text{tail}}}=-\frac{0.2}{2001}=-1.0\times10^{-4}.
$$

The two common-mode gains are suppressed by the tail resistance, but $A_{dm-cm}=-0.05$ is not: a differential input moves the drains by equal and opposite currents regardless of $r_{\text{tail}}$, and the unequal loads turn that into a common-mode output.
