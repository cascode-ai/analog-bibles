A resistively loaded differential pair consists of two transistors $M_1$ and $M_2$ whose sources are joined at a common tail node; the gates are driven by $v_{i1}$ and $v_{i2}$, the drain of $M_1$ is connected to the supply through load $R_1$ and the drain of $M_2$ through load $R_2$, and the outputs $v_{o1}$, $v_{o2}$ are the drain voltages. The tail current source has small-signal (Norton) resistance $r_{\text{tail}}$ from the tail node to ground. Use half-circuit analysis to determine $A_{dm}$, $A_{cm}$, $A_{cm-dm}$, and $A_{dm-cm}$ for this pair with mismatched loads: $R_1 = 10.1\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $R_2 = 9.9\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$. Assume $g_{m1} = g_{m2} = 1$ mA/V, $r_{o1} \to \infty$, $r_{o2} \to \infty$, $r_{\text{tail}} = 1\ \mathrm{M\Omega}$, and neglect the body effect.

Definitions: $v_{id} = v_{i1} - v_{i2}$, $v_{ic} = (v_{i1}+v_{i2})/2$, $v_{od} = v_{o1} - v_{o2}$, $v_{oc} = (v_{o1}+v_{o2})/2$; $v_{od} = A_{dm}v_{id} + A_{cm-dm}v_{ic}$ and $v_{oc} = A_{dm-cm}v_{id} + A_{cm}v_{ic}$.
