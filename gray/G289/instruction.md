An amplifier has a low-frequency forward gain of 200 and its transfer function has three negative real poles with magnitudes 1 MHz, 2 MHz, and 4 MHz. Calculate and sketch plots of gain (in decibels) and phase versus frequency (log scale) with no feedback applied. The amplifier is then placed in a negative feedback loop with a frequency-independent feedback factor $f$. Determine the value of $f$ that just causes instability and the value of $f$ giving a 60° phase margin.
