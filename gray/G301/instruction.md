An op amp with low-frequency gain of 108 dB has three negative real poles with magnitudes 30 kHz, 500 kHz, and 10 MHz before compensation. The small-signal resistance to ground at the input of its second stage is 1.95 MΩ. The circuit is compensated by connecting a shunt capacitance to ground at the input of the second stage; assume that this affects only the most dominant pole (the other two poles stay at 500 kHz and 10 MHz). Calculate the value of capacitance required to achieve a 60° phase margin in a unity-gain feedback connection and calculate the frequency where the resulting open-loop gain is 0 dB.
