For the common-source amplifier with depletion-mode load shown in [Fig. 1], calculate the small-signal voltage gain $v_o/v_i$, including both the body effect and channel-length modulation. $V_{DD} = 3$ V, and the dc input voltage is adjusted so that the dc output voltage is 1 V. $M_1$ has drawn dimensions $W = 100$ µm, $L = 1$ µm; $M_2$ (an n-channel depletion-mode transistor with its gate tied to its source at the output) has $W = 10$ µm, $L = 1$ µm and $V_{t0} = -1$ V. Device data: $k' = \mu_nC_{ox} = 194\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_{t0} = 0.6$ V for $M_1$, $L_d = 0.09$ µm and $X_d = 0$ (so $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.82$ µm), $\lambda = (0.02\ \mu\mathrm{m/V})/L_{\text{eff}} = 0.0244\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, body-effect coefficient $\gamma = 0.28\ \mathrm{V^{1/2}}$, $2\phi_f = 0.66$ V, with $V_t = V_{t0} + \gamma\left(\sqrt{2\phi_f + V_{SB}} - \sqrt{2\phi_f}\right)$.
