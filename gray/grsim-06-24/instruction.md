(a) [Fig. 1] shows a folded two-stage CMOS op amp: an n-channel differential input pair (tail current source $I_1$ to $-V_{SS}$), whose drains are fed from $V_{DD}$ by the current sources $I_3$; from each drain a current $I_4$ flows through a level-shifting battery $V$ (+ at the top) into an n-channel current mirror (the left mirror device diode-connected); the right mirror device's drain drives the gate of the n-channel common-source second-stage transistor, which is loaded by the current source $I_2$ from $V_{DD}$ and compensated by $C_C$. Assume that current source $I_1$ is implemented using an n-channel transistor with overdrive $V_{ovn}$, which is equal to the overdrives of the other n-channel transistors shown, and that current sources $I_2$ and $I_3$ are each implemented using one p-channel transistor with overdrive magnitude $V_{ovp}$. In the resulting op amp, only n-channel transistors conduct time-varying currents. Find the input common-mode range and the maximum output swing of the op amp in terms of $V_{DD}$, $-V_{SS}$, the level-shift voltage $V$, the threshold voltages of individual transistors, and the overdrives $V_{ovn}$ and $V_{ovp}$.

(b) [Fig. 2] shows a realization of an active floating level shift (active battery) between its + and − terminals, carrying a current $I$ into the + terminal. Design this level-shift circuit to give a battery voltage of 1.5 V with a small-signal resistance less than 1 kΩ at $I = 100\ \mu$A dc. Ignore the body effect. Use $I_B = 100\ \mu$A and $V_{DD} = V_{SS} = 1.65$ V. Use SPICE to plot the large-signal $I$–$V$ characteristic for $V = 0$ to 1.65 V. Use $\mu_nC_{ox} = 194\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0$ and $V_t = 0.6$ V. For SPICE simulations, connect the lower end of the battery to the negative supply.
