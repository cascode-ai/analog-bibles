Design a voltage-controlled oscillator based on the emitter-coupled multivibrator shown in [Fig. 1]. The center frequency is to be 10 kHz, $C = 0.01\ \mu$F, and $V_{CC} = 5$ V. For the transistors β = 100 and $I_S = 10^{-16}$ A. The frequency is to be varied by 2:1 by an input change $\Delta V_{\text{in}} = 200$ mV. Specify all resistors (the two collector resistors $R$ and the emitter resistors $R_E$ of the two current-source transistors driven by $V_{\text{in}}$) and the dc value of $V_{\text{in}}$; the current sources $I_B$ may be taken as ideal (choose their value). Use SPICE to check your design and also to produce a plot of the transfer characteristic from $V_{\text{in}}$ to frequency. Take $V_T = 26$ mV.
