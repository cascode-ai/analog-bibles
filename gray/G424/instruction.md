For the fully differential folded-cascode op amp in [Fig. 1] ($V_{DD} = 2$ V, ground = 0 V), what is the common-mode input range (CMIR) if only $M_1$ and $M_2$ are changed to low-threshold devices with $V_{t1} = V_{t2} = 0.3$ V? All other transistors keep $V_{tn} = -V_{tp} = 0.50$ V. Data: square law, no body effect; $k'_n = 170\ \mu\text{A/V}^2$, $k'_p = 45\ \mu\text{A/V}^2$. Every transistor is made of $m$ unit devices in parallel, unit $W = 40\ \mu$m, $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.3\ \mu$m. $M_1$, $M_2$: $m = 10$, $I_D = 2.0$ mA each; $M_5$, $M_6$ (tail, in parallel): $m = 10$, 2.0 mA each; $M_{1A}$, $M_{2A}$ (PMOS cascodes): $m = 24$, 2.4 mA; $M_{3}$, $M_{3A}$, $M_4$, $M_{4A}$: $m = 12$, 2.4 mA; $M_{11}$, $M_{12}$ (PMOS): $m = 44$, 4.4 mA. The gates of $M_{1A}$, $M_{2A}$ are at $V_{BiasC} = 1.0$ V. The CMIR is the range of CM input voltage over which $M_1$, $M_2$ and the tail devices stay in the active region.
