A two-stage CMOS op amp is Miller-compensated with a capacitor $C$ in series with a nulling resistor $R_Z = 1/g_{m6}$ that eliminates the right half-plane zero; its small-signal model is shown in [Fig. 1]. The first-stage transconductance is $g_{m1} = 1$ mA/V, the second-stage transconductance is $g_{m6} = 1.55$ mA/V, the total capacitance at node X is $C_1 ≈ 0.12$ pF, and the op amp drives a 5-pF load, so $C_2 ≈ 5$ pF. The op amp is connected in unity-gain feedback, and the third pole introduced by $R_Z$ (at about $-1/(R_ZC_1)$) can be assumed to lie far above the second pole. Determine the compensation capacitor $C$ that gives a 60° phase margin.
