Impact ionization in the drain depletion region of an NMOS transistor operating in the active region produces a drain-to-substrate current
$I_{DB} = K_1\,(V_{DS} - V_{DS(\text{act})})\,I_D\,\exp\!\left[-\dfrac{K_2}{V_{DS} - V_{DS(\text{act})}}\right]$,
where $V_{DS(\text{act})}$ is the minimum $V_{DS}$ for which the transistor operates in the active region, $K_1 = 5\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$ and $K_2 = 30$ V. The substrate is grounded.

Calculate the small-signal output resistance of the mirror including the effect of this substrate leakage current, for $V_{OUT} = 2$ V and for $V_{OUT} = 3$ V.
