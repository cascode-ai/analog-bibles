The large-signal behaviour of an amplifier around its quiescent point is described by $v_o = a_1v_i + a_2v_i^2 + a_3v_i^3$, and the input is $v_i = \hat v_i\sin\omega t$. For small distortion, the second- and third-harmonic distortion (amplitude of the $2\omega$ or $3\omega$ output component divided by the fundamental) are $HD_2 \approx \tfrac12(a_2/a_1)\hat v_i$ and $HD_3 \approx \tfrac14(a_3/a_1)\hat v_i^2$. Let $A_v^+$, $A_{vQ}$ and $A_v^-$ be the incremental gains $dv_o/dv_i$ when $v_i$ is maximum, zero and minimum, and define $E^+ = (A_v^+ - A_{vQ})/A_{vQ}$ and $E^- = (A_v^- - A_{vQ})/A_{vQ}$. Express $HD_2$ and $HD_3$ in terms of $E^+$ and $E^-$.
