# Golden Solution - G127

## Reference answer

$HD_2 \approx \dfrac{E^+ - E^-}{8}$ and $HD_3 \approx \dfrac{E^+ + E^-}{24}$.
- From $A_v^\pm = a_1 \pm 2a_2\hat v_i + 3a_3\hat v_i^2$: $E^+ - E^- = 4(a_2/a_1)\hat v_i = 8\,HD_2$ and $E^+ + E^- = 6(a_3/a_1)\hat v_i^2 = 24\,HD_3$.
- So the asymmetry of the gain between the two extremes measures the second harmonic, and the common change of gain at both extremes measures the third harmonic. (Signs follow the definitions: $HD_3$ is negative when the gain drops at both extremes.)

## Grading notes

+2: both relations correct ($/8$ and $/24$) with the derivation from the gain expressions. +1: one factor wrong (e.g. $/4$ or $/12$) with a sound method. 0: correct intermediate expressions for $E^\pm$ but no final relations. −2 (asserted as fact): relations interchanged ($HD_2$ from $E^+ + E^-$), or factors wrong in both.
