The fully differential op amp shown in [Fig. 1] has all transistors in the active region with $|V_{ov}| = 0.1$ V and $I_{D5} = 100\ \mu$A ($I_{D1} = I_{D2} = |I_{D3}| = |I_{D4}| = 50\ \mu$A), $V_{An} = 10$ V, $|V_{Ap}| = 20$ V ($r_o = |V_A|/|I_D|$), no body effect; its input impedance is infinite at low frequency. Common-mode feedback is provided by the scheme in [Fig. 2]: source followers buffer the outputs, two equal resistors $R_{cs}$ average them, and a CM-sense amplifier compares the result with a reference and drives the CMC input (the gate of $M_5$). The total low-frequency gain of the CM-sense path from $v_{oc}$ to $v_{cmc}$ is $a_{cms} = 1$, and the followers do not load the op-amp outputs.

The op amp is connected in the feedback circuit of [Fig. 3] with $R_1 = R_2 = R_3 = R_4 = 100$ MΩ. Compute the low-frequency closed-loop gains $A_{dm} = v_{od}/v_{sd}$ and $A_{cm} = v_{oc}/v_{sc}$ ($v_{sd} = v_{s1} - v_{s2}$, $v_{sc} = (v_{s1} + v_{s2})/2$, $v_{od} = v_{o1} - v_{o2}$, $v_{oc} = (v_{o1} + v_{o2})/2$).
