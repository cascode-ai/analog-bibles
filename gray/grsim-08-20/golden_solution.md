## Reference answer

(a) Reference: $I_{C8} = (12 - 0.7)/(10\text{k} + 1.4\text{k}) = 0.99$ mA. Equal areas and equal $V_{BE}$: $I_{C7} = I_{C9} = 0.99\times1.4/0.3 = 4.6$ mA; $I_{C10} = I_{C11} = 0.99\times1.4/0.4 = 3.5$ mA (including the $V_T\ln$ correction for the unequal currents: ≈ 4.5 mA and 3.4 mA). Hence $I_{C1} = I_{C2} \approx 2.3$ mA, $I_{C3} = I_{C4} \approx 2.3$ mA. Output followers: $V_{C3,4} = 6 - 1.1\text{k}\times2.3\text{m} = 3.46$ V, outputs at ≈ 2.76 V; the input-stage collectors (bases of $Q_3$/$Q_4$) at ≈ 1.04 V, so each 7-kΩ resistor carries ≈ 0.25 mA from the output into that node, and $I_{C5} = I_{C6} \approx 3.5 + 0.25 \approx 3.7$ mA (no dc current in $R_L$).
(b) Input stage: differential input resistance $\approx 2(\beta+1)(r_e + 640) = 2\times101\times651 \approx 132$ kΩ; its differential output current is $v_i/(2\times651\ \Omega)$. Output stage (half circuit, with $R_L/2 = 1$ kΩ): shunt-shunt transresistance ≈ $-R_F = -7$ kΩ per side, two-port loop gain $T \approx 9.5$ ($a \approx 66$ kΩ, $f = -1/7$ kΩ), so the per-side transresistance is ≈ 6.3 kΩ. Overall $v_o/v_i \approx 2\times6.3\text{k}/1.30\text{k} \approx 9.6$ (exact nodal value 9.63; ideal ≈ 14k/1.3k ≈ 10.8 for infinite loop gain). Differential output impedance ≈ 3.3 Ω: per side the follower $Q_5$ fed from 1.1 kΩ has $z_{oa} \approx (1.1\text{k} + r_{\pi5})/(\beta+1) \approx 18$ Ω, divided by $1+T \approx 10.5$ → ≈ 1.7 Ω (exact nodal value 3.34 Ω differential).
(c) With very large β a simulation reproduces these bias currents within ≈ 1–2% (the $V_{BE}$ ratio effect lowers $I_{C7}$, $I_{C9}$ and $I_{C10}$, $I_{C11}$ slightly); with β = 100 the five mirror transistors draw ≈ 0.15 mA of base current from the 0.99-mA reference, so all bias currents come out ≈ 13–16% lower ($I_{C8}$ ≈ 0.86 mA, $I_{C7} = I_{C9}$ ≈ 3.9 mA, $I_{C10} = I_{C11}$ ≈ 2.9 mA, $I_{C1..4}$ ≈ 1.9 mA, $I_{C5} = I_{C6}$ ≈ 3.1 mA); gain ≈ 9.5–10, differential input resistance ≈ 130 kΩ and differential output resistance of a few ohms.
