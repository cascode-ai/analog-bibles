The ac schematic of a wideband MOS current amplifier is shown in [Fig. 1]: the input current $i_i$ drives the diode-connected $M_1$, whose gate also drives $M_2$; the output current $i_o$ is the drain current of $M_2$ into an ac short circuit. The $W/L$ of $M_2$ is four times that of $M_1$, and the bias currents are $I_{D1} = 1$ mA and $I_{D2} = 4$ mA. Calculate the low-frequency small-signal current gain $i_o/i_i$, use the zero-value time-constant method to estimate the −3-dB frequency, and calculate the 10-to-90 percent rise time for a small step input. Data at the operating point: $M_1$: $C_{gd} = 0.05$ pF, $C_{gs} = 0.2$ pF, $C_{sb} = C_{db} = 0.09$ pF, $V_{ov} = 0.3$ V, $r_o = \infty$. $M_2$: $C_{gd} = 0.2$ pF, $C_{gs} = 0.8$ pF, $C_{sb} = C_{db} = 0.36$ pF, $V_{ov} = 0.3$ V, $r_o = \infty$. Sources and bodies are at ac ground.
