The ac schematic of a shunt-shunt feedback amplifier is shown in [Fig. 1]: three common-source stages $M_1$–$M_3$, each with a 5-kΩ drain resistor, and a 10-kΩ feedback resistor from the output (drain of $M_3$) to the gate of $M_1$; the input is a signal current $i_i$ into the gate of $M_1$. All transistors have $I_D = 1$ mA, $W/L = 100$, $k' = 60\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 1/(50\text{ V})$ ($r_o = 1/(\lambda I_D)$); ignore body effect. Use return-ratio analysis with respect to the $g_m$ generator of $M_1$ (return ratio $\mathcal{R}$, asymptotic gain $A_\infty$, direct feedthrough $d$, $A = A_\infty\frac{\mathcal{R}}{1+\mathcal{R}} + \frac{d}{1+\mathcal{R}}$, and Blackman's impedance formula). (1) Calculate the overall transresistance $v_o/i_i$, the loop transmission (here $\mathcal{R}$), the input impedance and the output impedance at low frequencies. (2) If the circuit is fed from a source resistance of 1 kΩ in parallel with $i_i$, what is the new output resistance?
