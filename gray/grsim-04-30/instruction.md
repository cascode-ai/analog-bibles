Simulate a band-gap reference of the type shown in [Fig. 1] on SPICE (pnp transistors $Q_1$, $Q_2$ with bases and collectors grounded; take $V_{OS} = 0$). Assume that the amplifier is just a voltage-controlled voltage source with an open-loop gain of 10,000 and that the resistor values are independent of temperature. Also assume that $I_{S1} = 1.25\times10^{-17}$ A and $I_{S2} = 1\times10^{-16}$ A. In SPICE, adjust the closed-loop gain of the amplifier (by choosing suitable resistor values) so that the output $TC_F$ is zero at 25 °C. What is the resulting target value of $V_{OUT}$? Now double $I_{S1}$ and $I_{S2}$. Use SPICE to adjust the gain so that $V_{OUT}$ is equal to the target at 25 °C. Find the new $dV_{OUT}/dT$ at 25 °C with SPICE. Compare this result with the case where the saturation currents double but the resistors are not readjusted (for that case, $dV_{OUT}/dT = -(k/q)\ln2 \approx -60\ \mu\mathrm{V/°C}$).

For the hand estimate use $V_{EB} = V_T\ln(I/I_S)$ with $I_S \propto T^{\gamma}\exp(-V_{G0}/V_T)$, $\gamma = 3.2$, $V_{G0} = 1.205$ V; $V_T = 25.7$ mV at 25 °C.
