# Golden Solution - G033

## Reference answer

The resistor is $L/W = 200/5 = 40$ squares (end/contact effects neglected):
- emitter-diffused: $40\times5\ \Omega = 200\ \Omega$;
- base-diffused: $40\times100\ \Omega = 4\ \mathrm{k}\Omega$;
- pinch resistor: $40\times5\ \mathrm{k}\Omega = 200\ \mathrm{k}\Omega$.

## Grading notes

+2: all three values (200 Ω, 4 kΩ, 200 kΩ); small end-effect corrections, if stated, are acceptable. +1: two of three right with an arithmetic slip in the third. 0: number of squares wrong but used consistently (e.g. 1/40). −2 (asserted as fact): $R = R_\square W/L$ or other formula errors giving values wrong by a factor of 1600, or sheet resistances assigned to the wrong resistor types.
