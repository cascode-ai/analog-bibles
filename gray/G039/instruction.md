For the substrate $pnp$ structure shown in [Fig. 1], calculate $I_S$, $C_{je}$, $C_\mu$ (zero-bias values) and $\tau_F$. The $p$-type emitter (made with the $npn$ base diffusion, 3 μm deep) is a rectangle of about 90 μm × 75 μm with a 30 μm × 55 μm hole in the middle, in which the $n^+$ base-contact diffusion ($npn$ emitter diffusion, 2.5 μm deep, base-diffusion doping at that depth ≈ 10¹⁷ cm⁻³) is placed so that it touches the $p$ emitter; the emitter area is thus 5100 μm². The isolated $n$-epi pocket (the base) is about 143 μm × 128 μm. Doping: $n$-type epi 17 μm thick, $N_D = 10^{15}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$; $p$ substrate (collector) $10^{16}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$. Neglect the depletion-layer widths when finding the base width.

Use a hole diffusivity in the base $D_p = 10$ cm²/s, $n_i = 1.5\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, built-in potentials 0.55 V (emitter-base) and 0.52 V (base-substrate), $\epsilon_{Si} = 1.04\times10^{-12}$ F/cm, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
