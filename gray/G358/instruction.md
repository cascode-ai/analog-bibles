In the differential input stage of [Fig. 1], the bipolar transistors are MOS transistors instead (gate at the base terminal, source at the emitter, drain at the collector), each with transconductance $g_m = 0.5$ mA/V and dc gate leakage current $I_G = 0.1$ fA; assume $C_{gs} = 0$. The circuit is fed from source resistances $R_S = 100$ MΩ at both gates as shown, with the signal $v_s$ in series with the first gate. Neglecting flicker noise, calculate the total equivalent input noise voltage at $v_s$ in a bandwidth of 1 kHz. Model the channel thermal noise as $\overline{i_d^2} = 4kT\frac{2}{3}g_m\Delta f$ and the gate-current shot noise as $2qI_G\Delta f$; use $4kT = 1.66\times10^{-20}$ V·C and $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
