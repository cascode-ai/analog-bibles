An amplifier has a low-frequency forward gain of 40,000 and its transfer function has three negative real poles with magnitudes 2 kHz, 200 kHz, and 4 MHz. It is to be compensated by reducing the magnitude of the most dominant pole (the 2-kHz pole), the remaining poles not moving, for use in a feedback loop (frequency-independent feedback factor) with a closed-loop gain of 20 dB and a 45° phase margin. Calculate the required dominant-pole magnitude and the corresponding bandwidth of the circuit with the feedback applied.
