The CMOS circuit of [Fig. 1] is to be used as a high-slew-rate op amp. A load capacitance of $C_L = 10$ pF is connected from $V_o$ to ground. Supply voltages are ±5 V and $I_1 = 20$ μA. Devices $M_1$–$M_4$ (NMOS) have $W = 20$ μm and $L = 1$ μm and devices $M_5$–$M_8$ (PMOS) have $W = 60$ μm and $L = 1$ μm. All other NMOS devices have $W = 60$ μm and $L = 1$ μm, and all other PMOS devices have $W = 300$ μm and $L = 1$ μm. Device data are $\mu_nC_{ox} = 60$ μA/V², $\mu_pC_{ox} = 20$ μA/V², $V_{tn} = 0.7$ V, $V_{tp} = -0.7$ V, $\gamma = 0$, and $|\lambda| = 0.05$ V⁻¹ (take $L$ as the effective length).
(a) Calculate the small-signal open-loop gain and unity-gain bandwidth of the circuit. At what value of $V_i$ does the transition occur?

What is the peak current delivered to $C_L$ during the transient?
