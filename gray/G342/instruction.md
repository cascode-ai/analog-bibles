An FM demodulator using a PLL has a center frequency of 2 kHz and is implemented as a first-order loop (no loop filter). The input signal alternates between 1.95 kHz and 2.05 kHz at a rate of 200 Hz (a 200-Hz square-wave modulation), with instantaneous transitions between the two frequency values. Sketch the demodulated output voltage waveform (the VCO control voltage $V_o$), ignoring the term related to the sum of the frequencies at the PLL input and the VCO output. For the PLL, use an amplifier gain $A = 1$, a phase-detector gain $K_D = 2.55$ V/rad and a VCO gain $K_O = 0.93\,\omega_{osc}$ (in rad/s per volt), where $\omega_{osc}$ is the free-running frequency in rad/s; the VCO runs at its free-running frequency of 2 kHz when $V_o = 0$.
