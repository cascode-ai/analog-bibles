Sketch the $I_C$–$V_{CE}$ characteristics in the forward-active region for an $npn$ transistor with $\beta_F = 100$ (measured at low $V_{CE}$), Early voltage $V_A = 50$ V, $BV_{CBO} = 120$ V, and avalanche exponent $n = 4$, for $V_{CE}$ from 0 to 10 V. Use
$$I_C = \left(1+\frac{V_{CE}}{V_A}\right)\frac{M\alpha_F}{1-M\alpha_F}\,I_B,\qquad M = \frac{1}{1-\left(V_{CB}/BV_{CBO}\right)^n},$$
with $\alpha_F = \beta_F/(1+\beta_F)$ and $V_{CB} \approx V_{CE}$. Plot the curves for $I_B$ = 1 µA, 10 µA, 30 µA and 60 µA.
