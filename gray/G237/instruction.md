In the amplifier stage shown in [Fig. 1], all bipolar transistors are replaced by $n$-channel MOS transistors (base → gate, emitter → source, collector → drain): $M_1$ and $M_4$ are source followers biased by the 10-µA current sources, and $M_2$, $M_3$ form a differential pair with the 1-mA tail current source and 5-kΩ drain resistors; the 10-kΩ resistors are in series with the gates of $M_1$ and $M_4$, and the gate of $M_1$ sits at 0 V dc. Apply the zero-value time-constant method to the differential-mode half-circuit to calculate the −3-dB frequency of the gain $v_o/v_i$.

NMOS data (all): $W = 100\ \mu$m, $L_{\text{drwn}} = 2\ \mu$m, $L_d = 0.2\ \mu$m, $X_d = 0$ ($L_{\text{eff}} = 1.6\ \mu$m), $\lambda = 0$, $k_n' = 60\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\gamma = 0$, $V_t = 1$ V, $C_{ox} = 0.7\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, overlap capacitance $C_{ol} = 0.15$ fF per µm of gate width (so $C_{gs} = \tfrac23WL_{\text{eff}}C_{ox} + C_{ol}W$ and $C_{gd} = C_{ol}W$), $C_{j0} = 0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{jsw0} = 0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$. Junction capacitances: $C_{sb} = C_{sb0}/\sqrt{1 + V_{SB}/\psi_0}$ and $C_{db} = C_{db0}/\sqrt{1 + V_{DB}/\psi_0}$ with $\psi_0 = 0.6$ V and $C_{sb0} = C_{db0} = A_DC_{j0} + P_DC_{jsw0}$, $A_D = (5\ \mu\mathrm{m})W$, $P_D = W$. Bodies are at −6 V. Square-law model; current sources ideal.
