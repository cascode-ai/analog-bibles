The ac schematic of a shunt-shunt feedback amplifier is shown in [Fig. 1], but with all three NMOS transistors replaced by $npn$ transistors $Q_1$–$Q_3$ (base for gate, emitter for source, collector for drain): three common-emitter stages with 5-kΩ collector resistors and a 10-kΩ feedback resistor from the output (collector of $Q_3$) to the base of $Q_1$; the input is a signal current $i_i$ into the base of $Q_1$. All collector currents are 1 mA, $\beta = 200$, $V_A = 50$ V, $r_b = 0$, $V_T = 26$ mV. Use return-ratio analysis with respect to the $g_m$ generator of $Q_1$ (return ratio $\mathcal{R}$, $A_\infty$, direct feedthrough $d$, $A = A_\infty\frac{\mathcal{R}}{1+\mathcal{R}} + \frac{d}{1+\mathcal{R}}$, and Blackman's impedance formula). (1) Calculate the overall transresistance $v_o/i_i$, the loop transmission (here $\mathcal{R}$), and the input and output impedances at low frequencies. (2) If the circuit is fed from a source resistance of 1 kΩ in parallel with $i_i$, what is the new output resistance?
