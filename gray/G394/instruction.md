The circuit in [Fig. 1] is a CM-sense amplifier: PMOS source-coupled pair $M_{21}$–$M_{22}$ with tail current source $M_{25}$, and diode-connected NMOS loads $M_{23}$ and $M_{24}$. Its output $V_{cms}$ is taken at the drain (= gate) of $M_{23}$. Estimate the pole associated with the RC time constant at the $V_{cms}$ output node. Assume $|I_{D25}| = 0.1$ mA, $V_{ov23} = 0.2$ V, and $V_{oc} = V_{CM}$ (so the pair is balanced). Ignore all capacitances except $C_{gs23}$ in parallel with a fixed capacitance of 90 fF, take $C_{gs} = \tfrac23 WL C_{ox}$, and take $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.8\ \mu$m. Process data: NMOS mobility $\mu_n = 550\ \text{cm}^2/\text{V·s}$, gate-oxide thickness 150 Å, so $C_{ox} = 2.30\ \text{fF}/\mu\text{m}^2$ and $k'_n = \mu_nC_{ox} = 127\ \mu\text{A/V}^2$. Assume the resistance at the node is set by the diode-connected $M_{23}$ ($r_o \gg 1/g_m$).
