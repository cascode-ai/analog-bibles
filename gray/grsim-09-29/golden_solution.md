## Grading notes

Design problem: accept any design whose hand calculations and simulation show all specifications met: infinite input resistance (MOS gate input), closed-loop gain 10 (within a few percent), $R_o$ < 30 Ω (closed loop), $f_{-3\text{dB}}$ > 2 MHz with no peaking (phase margin ≳ 65–70°, or a demonstrated monotonic response), quiescent supply current < 2 mA per supply, and ±1-V swing into 1 kΩ (sink current ≥ 1 mA plus divider current, all devices saturated at both extremes). Channel lengths must respect the 3-μm minimum and the body effect of the source follower must be accounted for in the swing check. Typical pitfalls: output sink current below 1 mA (clips at −1 V), insufficient loop gain for $R_o$, or a large-$g_m$ design whose second pole causes peaking.
