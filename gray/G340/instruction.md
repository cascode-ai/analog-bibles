A phase-locked loop has a center frequency of $10^5$ rad/s, a VCO gain $K_O = 10^3$ rad/(V·s), and a phase-detector gain $K_D = 1$ V/rad; there is no other gain in the loop. Design a passive R-C loop filter with one pole and one zero (unity gain at dc) that gives a crossover frequency for the loop gain of 100 rad/s. The loop phase shift at the loop crossover frequency should be −135°. Give the pole and zero frequencies and a set of component values.
