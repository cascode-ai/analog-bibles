A phase-locked loop has a center (free-running) frequency of $10^5$ rad/s, a VCO gain $K_O = 10^3$ rad/(V·s), and a phase-detector gain $K_D = 1$ V/rad, with no other gain in the loop. The phase detector's output is linear in phase error with slope $K_D$ and limited to a maximum of $\pm(\pi/2)K_D$. The loop filter is the passive lag-lead network $F(s) = (1 + s/\omega_2)/(1 + s/\omega_1)$ with $\omega_1 = 7.61$ rad/s and $\omega_2 = 116.5$ rad/s, which makes the loop gain cross unity at 100 rad/s with −135° of phase.

Estimate the capture range of the PLL, assuming that it is not artificially limited by the VCO frequency range. Use the following rough estimate: with the loop opened at the VCO control input and an input at frequency $\omega_i$ applied, the phase-detector output contains a difference-frequency sinusoid of amplitude $(\pi/2)K_D$ at $\omega_i - \omega_{osc}$; capture is assumed to occur if this component, after passing through the loop filter, is at least as large as the VCO control voltage $|\omega_i - \omega_{osc}|/K_O$ needed to move the VCO to the input frequency.
