In the switched-capacitor CMFB scheme in [Fig. 1], $C_1 = 0.1$ pF and $C_2 = 0.5$ pF; the switches are ideal. Ignoring all capacitors except $C_1$ and $C_2$, what are the CM and DM output load capacitances when the $\phi_2$ switches are on and the $\phi_1$ switches are off? Treat the capacitor network as a passive load on the op-amp outputs: the op amp's CMC input ($V_{cmc}$) draws no current, $V_{CM}$ and $V_{CSBIAS}$ are ideal dc sources, and the gain from $V_{cmc}$ to the outputs is not part of the load. Define each load capacitance as the capacitance from one output to ground in the DM or CM half-circuit.
