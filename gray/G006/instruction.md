The collector doping density of a bipolar transistor is $6\times10^{15}$ atoms/cm³ and is much less than the base doping. Find $BV_{CEO}$ for $\beta_F = 200$ and $n = 4$, where $n$ is the exponent of the avalanche multiplication factor $M = 1/[1-(V_{CB}/BV_{CBO})^n]$. Take $BV_{CBO}$ as the plane-junction breakdown voltage of the collector–base junction, with a critical field $\mathcal{E}_{crit} = 3\times10^5$ V/cm. Use $\epsilon_{Si} = 1.04\times10^{-12}$ F/cm and $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
