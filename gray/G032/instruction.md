Consider a hypothetical layer of silicon that has an $n$-type impurity concentration of $10^{17}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$ at the top surface, and in which the impurity concentration decreases exponentially with distance into the silicon. Assume that the concentration has decreased to $1/e$ of its surface value at a depth of 0.5 μm, and that the impurity concentration in the sample before the insertion of the $n$-type impurities was $10^{15}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$ $p$-type. Determine the depth below the surface of the $pn$ junction that results and determine the sheet resistance of the $n$-type layer. Assume a constant electron mobility of 800 cm²/V-s. Assume that the width of the depletion layer is negligible.
