$\dfrac{v_o}{i_s} = \dfrac{(g_m - Cs)R_1R_2}{1 + s[(C_2 + C)R_2 + (C_1 + C)R_1 + g_mR_1R_2C] + s^2R_1R_2(C_1C_2 + CC_1 + CC_2)}$.
