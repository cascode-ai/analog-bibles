A phase-locked loop has a center frequency of $10^5$ rad/s, a VCO gain $K_O = 10^3$ rad/(V·s), and a phase-detector gain $K_D = 1$ V/rad. There is no other gain in the loop. Determine the loop bandwidth in the first-order loop configuration (no loop filter). Then, for a single-pole loop filter $F(s) = 1/(1 + s/\omega_1)$, determine the pole location $\omega_1$ that gives closed-loop poles located on the 45° radials from the origin (at ±45° from the negative real axis).
