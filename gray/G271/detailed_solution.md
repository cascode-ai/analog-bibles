# Detailed solution - G271

Apply Blackman's formula with the op amp's voltage-controlled voltage source $a$ as the reference element:

$$
R_{out}=R_{out}(a{=}0)\,\frac{1+\mathcal R(\text{port shorted})}{1+\mathcal R(\text{port open})}.
$$

### Step 1 — Small-signal circuit

The gate of $M_1$ is held at ac ground by the $M_3$–$R$ bias branch, so $M_1$ appears only as the resistance $r_{o1}$ from node $S$ (the source of $M_2$) to ground; $g_{m1}$ does not enter. The op amp's inverting input senses $v_S$ and its non-inverting input is at the fixed $V_{BIAS}$, so its output, which drives the gate of $M_2$, is $v_G=-a\,v_S$.

### Step 2 — Resistance with $a=0$

With $a=0$ the gate of $M_2$ is at ac ground and the circuit is an ordinary cascode, $M_2$ degenerated by $r_{o1}$. A test current $i_x$ into the drain flows through $r_{o1}$ ($v_S=i_xr_{o1}$, $v_{gs2}=-v_S$) and through $r_{o2}$, which carries $i_x-g_{m2}v_{gs2}=i_x(1+g_{m2}r_{o1})$:

$$
R_{out}(a{=}0)=r_{o1}+r_{o2}+g_{m2}r_{o2}r_{o1}=(r_{o1}+r_{o2})\left[1+g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})\right].
$$

### Step 3 — Return ratio with the output port shorted

Replace the op amp's controlled source by a test voltage $v_t$ at the gate of $M_2$. With the drain grounded, $M_2$ is a source follower whose source node sees $r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2}$ to ground; KCL at $S$, $g_{m2}(v_t-v_S)=v_S/(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})$, gives

$$
v_S=v_t\,\frac{g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}{1+g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}.
$$

The returned value of the source is $-a\,v_S$, so

$$
\mathcal R(\text{short})=\frac{a\,g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}{1+g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}\approx a .
$$

### Step 4 — Return ratio with the output port open

With the drain open, $M_2$ and $r_{o2}$ are in series with an open circuit, so no current can flow into node $S$ from above; $r_{o1}$ carries no current, $v_S=0$, and

$$
\mathcal R(\text{open})=0 .
$$

### Step 5 — Output resistance

$$
R_{out}=(r_{o1}+r_{o2}+g_{m2}r_{o1}r_{o2})\left[1+\frac{a\,g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}{1+g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})}\right].
$$

Using the factored form of $R_{out}(a{=}0)$ from Step 2, the denominator cancels:

$$
R_{out}=(r_{o1}+r_{o2})\left[1+g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})+a\,g_{m2}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2})\right]=r_{o1}+r_{o2}+(1+a)\,g_{m2}r_{o1}r_{o2}\approx a\,g_{m2}r_{o1}r_{o2}.
$$

This is the same result as when $g_{m2}$ is taken as the reference: Blackman's formula gives the same impedance for any choice of reference element. Since $g_{m1}=g_{m2}$ (equal currents, equal $W/L$, $\gamma=0$), $g_{m1}$ may be written in place of $g_{m2}$.
