Design a MOS Widlar current source using the circuit shown in [Fig. 1] (choose $R_1$, $R_2$, $(W/L)_1$ and $(W/L)_2$) to meet the following constraints with $V_{DD} = 3$ V:

(i) The input current should be 100 µA, and the output current should be 10 µA.
(ii) $V_{ov1} = 0.2$ V.
(iii) Transistor $M_2$ must operate in the active region if the voltage from the drain of $M_2$ to ground is at least 0.2 V.
(iv) The output resistance should be 50 MΩ.

Ignore the body effect. Assume $L_{\text{drwn}} = 1$ µm and $X_d = L_d = 0$ (so $L = 1$ µm). Device data: $k' = \mu_nC_{ox} = 194\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $V_t = 0.6$ V, $dX_d/dV_{DS} = 0.02$ µm/V, so $\lambda = 0.02\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$. Use the square law (neglect the $1+\lambda V_{DS}$ factor in the dc design).
