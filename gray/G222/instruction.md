In the amplifier stage shown in [Fig. 1], the transistors are replaced by MOS devices: $Q_1$ by a $p$-channel transistor $M_1$ (source at $V_{\text{SUPPLY}}$, drain at the output), and $Q_2$, $Q_3$ by $n$-channel transistors $M_2$, $M_3$ (diode-connected $M_3$ biased through the 30-kΩ resistor from ground, mirrored by $M_2$). A resistor of value $1/g_{m1}$ is added from the gate to the source of $M_1$; the current source $I_B + i_i$ draws current out of the gate node of $M_1$. $V_{\text{SUPPLY}} = 2.5$ V and $I_B$ is adjusted so that $V_O = 0$ V dc. Calculate the low-frequency small-signal transresistance $v_o/i_i$ and use the zero-value time-constant method to estimate the −3-dB frequency.

Data for all transistors: $L_{\text{drwn}} = 2\ \mu$m, $L_d = 0.2\ \mu$m, $X_d = 1\ \mu$m (so $L_{\text{eff}} = L_{\text{drwn}} - 2L_d - X_d = 0.6\ \mu$m), $\gamma = 0$, $W_1 = 200\ \mu$m, $W_2 = W_3 = 100\ \mu$m. NMOS: $V_{tn} = 1$ V, $k_n' = 60\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda_n = 1/(100\ \mathrm{V})$, $C_{ox} = 0.7\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{j0} = 0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{jsw0} = 0.4\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$. PMOS: $V_{tp} = -1$ V, $k_p' = 20\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $|\lambda_p| = 1/(50\ \mathrm{V})$, $C_{ox} = 0.7\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{j0} = 0.2\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{jsw0} = 0.2\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m}$. Gate capacitances: $C_{gs} = \tfrac23WL_{\text{eff}}C_{ox} + WL_dC_{ox}$ and $C_{gd} = WL_dC_{ox}$ (overlap). Junction capacitances: $C_{db} = C_{db0}/\sqrt{1 + V_{DB}/\psi_0}$ (similarly $C_{sb}$) with $\psi_0 = 0.6$ V, $C_{db0} = A_DC_{j0} + P_DC_{jsw0}$, $A_D = (5\ \mu\mathrm{m})W$, $P_D = W$. NMOS bodies are at $-V_{\text{SUPPLY}}$, the PMOS body at $+V_{\text{SUPPLY}}$. Use the square-law model and neglect $\lambda$ in the bias calculation.
