# Golden Solution - G049

## Reference answer

Die area 22,500 mil²: cost per good finished unit ≈ USD 1.25 (process A), ≈ USD 1.70 (B), ≈ USD 3.1 (C).

- $C = \dfrac{C_w}{NY_{ws}Y_{df}Y_{ft}} + \dfrac{C_p}{Y_{ft}}$ with $Y_{df}Y_{ft} = 0.64$, $C_p/Y_{ft} = 0.50$ USD.
- 4-inch wafer: $N \approx 1.2\times10^7/22{,}500 \approx 530$ gross dice.
- Wafer-sort yields at 22,500 mil²: A ≈ 51%, B ≈ 32%, C ≈ 15%.
- $C_w/(NY_{df}Y_{ft}) = 130/(530\times0.64) = 0.38$ USD, so $C = 0.38/Y_{ws} + 0.50$: A $0.75 + 0.50 = 1.25$ USD; B $1.19 + 0.50 = 1.69$ USD; C $2.6 + 0.50 = 3.1$ USD.

## Grading notes

Three values, a third of the credit each. Accept $N$ = 480–600, yields within ±15% of the readings above, and costs within ±15% (A USD 1.1–1.4, B USD 1.5–1.9, C USD 2.7–3.5) with the correct cost model. +2: all three. +1: all three with the packaging cost not divided by the final-test yield (≈ USD 1.15, USD 1.59, USD 3.0), or two of three. 0: correct model with misread yields for two processes, or only one process done. −2 (asserted as fact): die area taken as 150 mil² or 1500 mil², yields multiplied instead of divided, or cost per wafer reported as cost per die.
