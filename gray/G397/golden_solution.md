# Golden Solution - G397

## Reference answer

Local-CMFB amplifier: $a_{dm} \approx -15.4$, $a_{cm} \approx -0.0098$ (≈ −0.01). The amplifier in [Fig. 2] has $a_{dm} = -66.7$ and $a_{cm} \approx -1.92$ (≈ −1.96 to −2 with $G_{mc} \approx 1/r_{o5h}$).

Values: $g_{m1} = g_{m3} = 1$ mA/V, $r_{o1} = 100$ kΩ, $r_{o3} = 200$ kΩ, $r_{o5h} = 2r_{o5} = 100$ kΩ.
- DM half-circuit: the resistor midpoint and the PMOS gates are ac grounds, so $M_3$ is just $r_{o3}$ and each output is loaded by 20 kΩ. $a_{dm} = -g_{m1}(r_{o1}\|r_{o3}\|20\ \text{k}) = -1\ \text{mA/V} \times 15.4\ \text{k}\Omega = -15.4$.
- CM half-circuit: no current in the resistors; the gate of $M_3$ follows $v_{oc}$, so $M_3$ is diode-connected (load $1/g_{m3} = 1$ kΩ). $M_1$ is degenerated by $r_{o5h}$: $G_m \approx 1/r_{o5h} = 10\ \mu$A/V. $a_{cm} \approx -(1/r_{o5h})(1/g_{m3}\|r_{o3}) \approx -1\ \text{k}/100\ \text{k} \approx -0.0099$ (−0.0098 exact).
- [Fig. 2]: $a_{dm} = -g_{m1}(r_{o1}\|r_{o3}) = -66.7$; $a_{cm} = -g_{m1}r_{o1}r_{o3}/[r_{o3} + r_{o1} + (1 + g_{m1}r_{o1})r_{o5h}] = -25/13 \approx -1.92$ (≈ $-(R_{o(\text{down})}\|r_{o3})/r_{o5h} \approx -196\ \text{k}/100\ \text{k} = -1.96$ with $G_{mc} \approx 1/r_{o5h}$).
- Comparison: the local CMFB lowers $|a_{cm}|$ by about 200× (to about what a separate CMFB loop with unity sense gain achieves), but the resistors load the DM half-circuit and cut $|a_{dm}|$ by about 4.3×.
