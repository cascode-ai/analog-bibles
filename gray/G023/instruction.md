Examine the effect of velocity saturation on MOSFET characteristics by plotting $I_D$–$V_{DS}$ curves for $V_{GS}$ = 1 V, 2 V, and 3 V, and $V_{DS}$ = 0 to 3 V, for an NMOS transistor with $W = 100\ \mu$m and $L = 10\ \mu$m, and by comparing the results with and without inclusion of velocity-saturation effects. Assume $V_{SB} = 0$, $V_{t0} = 0.6$ V, $k' = \mu_nC_{ox} = 194\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0$. Model velocity saturation with the drift velocity–field relation $v_d = \mu_n\mathcal{E}/(1+\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{E}_c)$ in a gradual-channel analysis, with $\mathcal{E}_c = 1.5\times10^6$ V/m.
