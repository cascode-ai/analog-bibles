The substrate $pnp$ transistor shown in [Fig. 1] is to be used as a test device to monitor epitaxial layer thickness. Its $p$-type emitter is formed by a base-type diffusion with a junction depth of 3 μm, and its emitter (bottom) area is 5100 μm²; the base is the $n$-type epitaxial layer between the bottom of the emitter and the $p$ substrate, which is the collector. Assume that the flow of minority carriers across the base is vertical, and that the width of the emitter-base and collector-base depletion layers is negligible. Assume that the epi layer resistivity is known to be 2 Ω-cm by independent measurement. The base-emitter voltage is observed to vary from 525 mV to 560 mV over several wafers at a collector current of 10 μA. What range of epitaxial layer thickness does this imply? What is the corresponding range of sheet resistance that will be observed in the epitaxial pinch resistors (the same epi layer pinched between a 3-μm-deep $p$-type base-diffusion cap and the substrate)? Assume a hole diffusivity of 10 cm²/s and an electron mobility of 800 cm²/V-s in the epi (use this mobility with the resistivity to obtain the epi donor concentration).

Use $n_i = 1.5\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{cm^{-3}}$, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C and $kT/q = 26$ mV.
