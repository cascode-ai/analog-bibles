For the CMOS operational amplifier shown in [Fig. 1] (supplies ±1.5 V, bias current 200 μA, aspect ratios as shown in μm/μm, compensation capacitor 5 pF), calculate the open-loop voltage gain, the unity-gain bandwidth, and the slew rate. Assume that the gate of $M_9$ is connected to the positive power supply and that the $W/L$ of $M_9$ has been chosen to cancel the right half-plane zero.

Device data (square-law model, $I_D = \frac{k'}{2}\frac{W}{L_{\text{eff}}}(V_{GS}-V_t)^2$, small-signal $r_o = 1/(\lambda I_D)$): $k'_n = \mu_nC_{ox} = 194$ μA/V², $k'_p = \mu_pC_{ox} = 64.7$ μA/V² ($t_{ox}$ = 8 nm, $C_{ox}$ = 4.32 fF/μm²), $V_{tn} = 0.6$ V, $V_{tp} = -0.8$ V, lateral diffusion $L_d = 0.09$ μm. For all transistors operating in the active region use $X_d = 0.1$ μm, so $L_{\text{eff}} = L_{\text{drwn}} - 2L_d - X_d$; channel-length modulation $dX_d/dV_{DS}$ = 0.02 μm/V (NMOS) and 0.04 μm/V (PMOS), with $\lambda = (dX_d/dV_{DS})/L_{\text{eff}}$.
