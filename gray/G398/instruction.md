A differential amplifier that does not use a tail current source is shown in [Fig. 1]: the sources of $M_1$ and $M_2$ connect directly to $-V_{SS}$, and the PMOS loads $M_3$, $M_4$ have their gates at a fixed bias $V_{BIAS}$. Compute the low-frequency gains $a_{dm} = v_{od}/v_{id}$ and $a_{cm} = v_{oc}/v_{ic}$. For all transistors, the drain currents are 100 μA and $|V_{ov}| = 0.2$ V. Also, $V_{An} = 10$ V and $|V_{Ap}| = 20$ V ($r_o = |V_A|/|I_D|$).
