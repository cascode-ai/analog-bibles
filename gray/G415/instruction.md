A fully differential current-mirror op amp is shown in [Fig. 1]: NMOS input pair $M_1$–$M_2$ with tail $M_5$; diode-connected PMOS loads $M_3$, $M_4$ mirrored 1:1 to $M_8$ (output $V_{o2}$) and $M_6$ (output $V_{o1}$); NMOS current sources $M_9$ and $M_7$ at the outputs. $M_1$ and $M_2$ are matched, $M_7$ and $M_9$ are matched, and $M_3$, $M_4$, $M_6$, $M_8$ are identical PMOS devices. Use $|V_{ov}| = 0.2$ V for all transistors, $I_{D5} = 200\ \mu$A, $V_{An} = 10$ V, $|V_{Ap}| = 20$ V ($r_o = |V_A|/|I_D|$), $\gamma = 0$. The CMC input is the gate of $M_5$, and the gates of $M_7$ and $M_9$ connect to a bias voltage. Compute the low-frequency CMC gain $a_{cmc} = v_{oc}/v_{cmc}$ (with $v_{ic} = 0$).
