The large-signal behaviour of an amplifier around its quiescent point is described by the power series $v_o = a_1v_i + a_2v_i^2 + a_3v_i^3$ (higher-order terms negligible). With $v_i = \hat v_i\sin\omega t$, let $A_v^+$, $A_{vQ}$ and $A_v^-$ be the incremental gains $dv_o/dv_i$ at the instants when $v_i$ is maximum, zero and minimum. Define two normalized differential gain error terms $E^+ = (A_v^+ - A_{vQ})/A_{vQ}$ and $E^- = (A_v^- - A_{vQ})/A_{vQ}$, and calculate expressions for $(E^+ + E^-)$ and $(E^+ - E^-)$ in terms of the coefficients and $\hat v_i$.
