The circuit shown in [Fig. 1] is built with NMOS transistors $M_1$, $M_2$ in place of $Q_1$, $Q_2$ (gate for base, drain for collector, source for emitter): $M_1$ is a source follower (drain at ac ground) whose source drives the gate of the common-source transistor $M_2$. $R_S = 100\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$, $R_L = 3\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$, $I_{D1} = 50\ \mu$A (supplied to $M_1$'s source by an ideal current source to ground), $I_{D2} = 1$ mA. Use the zero-value time-constant method to calculate the −3-dB frequency of $v_o/v_i$. NMOS data (both): $W = 100\ \mu$m, $L_{\text{eff}} = 1.6\ \mu$m, $k_n' = 60\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0$, $\gamma = 0$, $C_{gs} = \tfrac23WL_{\text{eff}}C_{ox}$ with $C_{ox} = 0.7\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{gd} = 14$ fF, $C_{db} = 200$ fF, $C_{sb} = 180$ fF (body at ac ground); square-law model.
