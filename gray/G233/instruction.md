The circuits shown in [Fig. 1] and [Fig. 2] are built with $npn$ transistors in place of the NMOS transistors (base for gate, emitter for source, collector for drain): a common-emitter stage and a common-emitter–common-base (cascode) stage (the cascode transistor's base at ac ground), both with $R_S = 5\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ and $R_L = 3\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$. Use the zero-value time-constant method to calculate and compare the −3-dB frequencies of the gain $v_o/v_i$ of the two circuits. Data (all transistors): $I_C = 1$ mA, $\beta = 100$, $r_b = 0$, $C_{cs} = 1$ pF, $C_\mu = 0.4$ pF, $f_T = 500$ MHz (at $I_C = 1$ mA), $r_o = \infty$, $V_T = 26$ mV.
