$$V_{OS} \simeq V_{t(1-2)} + V_{t(3-4)}\frac{g_{m3}}{g_{m1}} + \frac{V_{ov(1-2)}}{2}\left[\frac{\Delta(W/L)_{(3-4)}}{(W/L)_{(3-4)}} - \frac{\Delta(W/L)_{(1-2)}}{(W/L)_{(1-2)}}\right],$$
where $V_{t(1-2)} = V_{t1}-V_{t2}$, $V_{t(3-4)} = V_{t3}-V_{t4}$, $\Delta(W/L)_{(1-2)} = (W/L)_1-(W/L)_2$, $\Delta(W/L)_{(3-4)} = (W/L)_3-(W/L)_4$, the $(W/L)$ in the denominators are the average values of each pair, and $V_{ov(1-2)} = V_{GS}-V_t$ is the overdrive of the input transistors. $V_{OS}$ is the voltage $V_{G1}-V_{G2}$ (gate of $M_1$ minus gate of $M_2$) that must be applied to balance the first stage, i.e. to make the drain current of $M_2$ equal to the current mirrored by $M_4$. Explain the polarity of each term by assuming that the matching is perfect except for the term under consideration. Keep in mind that the overdrive is negative for p-channel transistors; explain in particular why the expression predicts that the offset stemming from $W_3 > W_4$ is negative.
