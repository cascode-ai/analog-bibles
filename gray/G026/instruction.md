A simple model of velocity saturation is shown in [Fig. 1]: an ideal square-law transistor ($I_D = \tfrac{k'}{2}\tfrac{W}{L}(V'_{GS}-V_t)^2$ in the active region) with a resistor $R_{SX} = 1/(\mathcal{E}_c\,k'W)$ in series with its source. Compare the drain current predicted by this model (solve the model circuit for $I_D$) to the drain current predicted by direct calculation from the gradual-channel equations including velocity saturation (drift velocity $v_d = \mu_n\mathcal{E}/(1+\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{E}_c)$), for $V_{GS}$ from 0 to 3 V.
