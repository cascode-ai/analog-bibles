A MOS source follower has its gate driven from a source with resistance $R_S$ and its output taken at the source terminal; the body is at ac ground, so $v_{sb} = v_o$. Consider the small-signal output impedance $z_o$ looking into the source terminal (load excluded, input source set to zero). Model the transistor by $g_m$, $g_{mb}$ (body effect present, $\gamma \ne 0$) and $C_{gs}$ only (neglect $C_{gd}$, $C_{gb}$, $C_{sb}$ and $r_o$). For $R_S \gg 1/g_m$ the output impedance is inductive and can be represented by a resistor $R_2$ in parallel with the series combination of a resistor $R_1$ and an inductor $L$ (so that $z_o(0) = R_1\|R_2$ and $z_o(\infty) = R_2$). Find expressions for $R_1$, $R_2$ and $L$.
