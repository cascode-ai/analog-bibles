Compare its output waveform with that of the continuous-time circuit shown in [Fig. 2], in which the sampling capacitor and switches are replaced by a resistor $R = 1/(fC_S)$ ($f$ = clock frequency) and the integrating capacitor is the same $C_I$, also with $V_s = 1$ V and $V_o(0) = 0$.
