## Grading notes

A correct result shows: loop gain ≈ 1900–2100 (the order of 2000); output resistance ≈ 0.04–0.06 Ω; new $V_o$ lower by ≈ 0.4–0.6 mV (10 V → ≈ 9.9995 V); a simulated load regulation of the order of −0.5 mV for a 10-mA step (≈ −0.005%); a simulated line regulation of the order of a few tenths of a mV per volt (≈ 0.002–0.01%/V). Order-of-magnitude agreement is the standard for the regulation figures because they depend on Early effects of several devices; output resistance or regulation off by more than ×3, or a loop gain below a few hundred, indicates an error.
