# Detailed solution - G430

The DM gain of the folded cascode is $a_{dm0}=-G_mR_{odh}$: the input pair's transconductance times the output resistance of the DM half circuit. At fixed drain current, doubling $W/L$ raises $g_{m1}$ by $\sqrt2$ but changes no output resistance, since every $r_o=1/(\lambda|I_D|)$ depends only on current. Compute the output resistance once, then the new $g_{m1}$.

### Step 1 — Small-signal parameters

With $W/L=133.3m$, $g_m=\sqrt{2k'(W/L)|I_D|}$ and $r_o=1/(\lambda|I_D|)$:

| Device | $m$ | $\lvert I_D\rvert$ | $g_m$ | $r_o$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $M_1$ (new) | 20 | 2.0 mA | 42.6 mA/V | 3.33 kΩ |
| $M_1$ (original) | 10 | 2.0 mA | 30.1 mA/V | 3.33 kΩ |
| $M_{1A}$ (PMOS) | 24 | 2.4 mA | 26.3 mA/V | 2.45 kΩ |
| $M_{11}$ (PMOS) | 44 | 4.4 mA | — | 1.34 kΩ |
| $M_3$, $M_{3A}$ (NMOS) | 12 | 2.4 mA | 36.1 mA/V | 2.78 kΩ |

A representative substitution:

$$
g_{m1}=\sqrt{2(170\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2})(20\times133.3)(2.0\ \mathrm{mA})}=42.6\ \mathrm{mA/V}.
$$

### Step 2 — Output resistance of the DM half circuit

In the DM half circuit the tail node is an ac ground. Looking up from the output, the PMOS cascode $M_{1A}$ is degenerated by the folding-node resistance $r_{o1}\parallel r_{o11}$; looking down, $M_{3A}$ is degenerated by $r_{o3}$. Using $R\approx g_mr_o\cdot R_S$ for a cascode:

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{up}}&\approx g_{m1A}r_{o1A}(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o11})=(26.3\ \mathrm{mA/V})(2.45\ \mathrm{k\Omega})(0.954\ \mathrm{k\Omega})=61.5\ \mathrm{k\Omega},\\
R_{\mathrm{down}}&\approx g_{m3A}r_{o3A}r_{o3}=(36.1\ \mathrm{mA/V})(2.78\ \mathrm{k\Omega})(2.78\ \mathrm{k\Omega})=279\ \mathrm{k\Omega},\\
R_{odh}&=R_{\mathrm{up}}\parallel R_{\mathrm{down}}=50.4\ \mathrm{k\Omega}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$r_{o1}$ is unchanged (same current), so $R_{odh}$ is the same as in the original design.

### Step 3 — New DM gain

$$
a_{dm0}=-g_{m1}R_{odh}=-(42.6\ \mathrm{mA/V})(50.4\ \mathrm{k\Omega})\approx-2.1\times10^{3}.
$$

The original design gives $-(30.1\ \mathrm{mA/V})(50.4\ \mathrm{k\Omega})\approx-1.5\times10^{3}$, so the gain rises by $\sqrt2$.

### Step 4 — Refinements

Using the full cascode expressions, $R_{\mathrm{up}}=(1+g_{m1A}r_{o1A})(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o11})+r_{o1A}=64.9$ kΩ and $R_{\mathrm{down}}=(1+g_{m3A}r_{o3A})r_{o3}+r_{o3A}=284$ kΩ, so $R_{odh}=52.8$ kΩ. In addition, a small part of $M_1$'s signal current flows into $r_{o1}\parallel r_{o11}$ instead of $M_{1A}$'s source, so the effective transconductance is $g_{m1}(g_{m1A}+1/r_{o1A})/(g_{m1A}+1/r_{o1A}+1/(r_{o1}\parallel r_{o11}))=41.0$ mA/V. The exact half-circuit gain is then $-(41.0\ \mathrm{mA/V})(52.8\ \mathrm{k\Omega})=-2165$ (versus $-1531$ for $m_1=10$). All estimates lie between about $-2130$ and $-2250$, i.e. $a_{dm0}\approx-2.1\times10^{3}$ to $-2.2\times10^{3}$.
