A common-base stage is driven at its emitter by a signal current source $i_i$ (source resistance $R_S = \infty$); the base is at ac ground and the collector feeds a load $R_L = 0$ (ac short circuit). Transistor data: $I_C = 0.5$ mA, $C_\pi = 10$ pF, $C_\mu = 0.3$ pF, $r_b = 200\ \Omega$, $\beta = 100$, $r_o = \infty$, $V_T = 26$ mV. Calculate an expression for the small-signal current gain $i_o/i_i$ of the stage as a function of frequency, where $i_o$ is the collector signal current delivered to the load, and thus determine the frequency where the current gain is 3 dB below its low-frequency value.
