The large-signal behaviour of an amplifier around its quiescent point is described by the power series $v_o = a_1v_i + a_2v_i^2 + a_3v_i^3$ (higher-order terms negligible), where $v_i$ and $v_o$ are the deviations of the input and output from their quiescent values. Let $v_i = \hat v_i\sin\omega t$. Derive expressions for the incremental gain $A_v = dv_o/dv_i$ at three instants: $A_v^+$ when $v_i$ is at its maximum, $A_{vQ}$ when $v_i = 0$, and $A_v^-$ when $v_i$ is at its minimum.
