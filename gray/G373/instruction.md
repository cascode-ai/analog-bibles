The ac schematic of a low-input-impedance common-base amplifier is shown in [Fig. 1]: the current source $i_s$ with shunt resistance $R_S = 5$ kΩ drives the emitter of $Q_1$ (base grounded); the collector of $Q_1$ has a 5-kΩ load and drives the base of the common-emitter transistor $Q_2$. Data: $I_{C1} = I_{C2} = 1$ mA, $r_{b1} = r_{b2} = 0$, $\beta_1 = \beta_2 = 100$; neglect capacitive effects. Each device has base-current flicker noise with a corner frequency of 1 kHz (base-current noise $2qI_B(1 + f_a/f)$, $f_a = 1$ kHz). Calculate the low frequency at which the spot noise figure is 3 dB above its value at frequencies where flicker noise is negligible. Use $4kT = 1.66\times10^{-20}$ V·C, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C and $kT/q = 26$ mV.
