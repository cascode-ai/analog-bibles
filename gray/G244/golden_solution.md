# Golden Solution - G244

## Reference answer

$A \approx 9.999$ (exactly $10^5/10{,}001 = 9.9990$); a 10% change in $a$ changes $A$ by only about 0.001% (≈ 10 ppm).

- $T = af = 10^4$; $A = a/(1+T) = 100{,}000/10{,}001 = 9.9990$.
- $\dfrac{dA}{A} = \dfrac{10\%}{10{,}001} \approx 0.001\%$.
- Exact: $a = 110{,}000$ → $A = 9.99909$ (+0.00091%); $a = 90{,}000$ → $A = 9.99889$ (−0.0011%).

## Grading notes

+2: $A \approx 9.999$ (≈ 10) and a change of about 0.001% (0.0009–0.0011%), with correct reasoning. +1: right method with a slip of a factor in the percentage that is still clearly ≪ 0.01%. 0: only one of the two quantities right. −2 (asserted as fact): a percentage change of the order of 1% or 10%, or $A$ far from 10.
