Using a translinear loop of bipolar transistors (exploiting the exponential relation between collector current and base–emitter voltage), design a circuit with a transfer characteristic $I_o = KI_i^{3/2}$ for $I_i \ge 0$. The input bias voltage must be $\ge V_{BE}$, and the output bias voltage is equal to $2V_{BE}$. The value of $I_o$ should be 100 µA for $I_i$ = 100 µA. The supply voltage available is $V_{CC} = 5$ V and device data are β = 100 and $I_S = 10^{-17}$ A. Use SPICE to verify your design and then examine the effect of finite $r_b = 200\ \Omega$ and $r_e = 2\ \Omega$. Take $V_T = 26$ mV.
