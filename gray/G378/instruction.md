A MOS transistor has two gate-current noise components: the shot noise of the dc gate leakage current, $\overline{i_g^2} = 2qI_G\Delta f$, and the noise coupled from the channel thermal noise through the gate capacitance, $\overline{i_g^2} = \frac{16}{15}kT\omega^2C_{gs}^2\Delta f$, with $C_{gs} = \frac{2}{3}C_{ox}WL$. Considering only gate-current noise, in what bandwidth (from 0 to $f_{BW}$) are the total input noise currents due to the shot noise and due to the coupled thermal noise equal? Assume $W = 50\ \mu$m, $L = 0.5\ \mu$m (no lateral diffusion), $I_G = 0.05$ fA, $t_{ox} = 80$ Å ($\epsilon_{ox} = 3.45\times10^{-11}$ F/m), $kT = 4.15\times10^{-21}$ J, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
