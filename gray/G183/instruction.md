In BiCMOS technology, MOS source followers can be used to drive a bipolar differential pair to reduce the average current flowing in the stage input leads, as shown in [Fig. 1]. Calculate the input-referred random offset voltage of this structure, i.e. the differential input voltage $V_{ID}$ (gate of $M_3$ minus gate of $M_4$) for which the differential output voltage $V_{OD}$ is zero. Assume nonzero mismatch occurs in the following parameters: the thresholds of the MOS transistors, the $W/L$ of the MOS transistors, the bias currents $I_{BIAS}$ of the two followers, the saturation currents of the bipolar transistors, and the load resistors. Ignore the body effect and base currents, and use the square-law model for the MOS transistors (overdrive $V_{ov}$ at the nominal bias) and $V_T$ for the thermal voltage. Express the result in terms of the mismatches, and give the standard deviation of the offset if the mismatches are independent random variables.
