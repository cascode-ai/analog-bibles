Consider the circuit of [Fig. 1] (a differential pair with a current-mirror load, biased by an ideal 100-µA tail current, input $v_i$ at the left input transistor, the other input grounded, output at the right-hand drains), but with $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ replaced by n-channel MOS transistors $M_1$ and $M_2$ (gates as the inputs, sources to the tail current source) and $Q_3$ and $Q_4$ replaced by p-channel MOS transistors $M_3$ ($M_3$ diode-connected) and $M_4$. $W_n = 50$ µm, $W_p = 100$ µm, $L_{\text{drwn}} = 1$ µm for all transistors, and $X_d = 0$. Determine the unloaded small-signal voltage gain $v_o/v_i$ and the output resistance. Neglect the body effect.

Process data: $t_{ox} = 150$ Å ($C_{ox} = 2.30$ fF/µm²), $\mu_n = 550$ cm²/V·s and $\mu_p = 250$ cm²/V·s, so $k'_n = 127\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$ and $k'_p = 57.6\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$; $V_{tn} = 0.7$ V, $V_{tp} = -0.7$ V; $L_d = 0.12$ µm (n) and 0.18 µm (p), so $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.76$ µm (n) and 0.64 µm (p); $dX_d/dV_{DS} = 0.08$ µm/V (n) and 0.04 µm/V (p), with $\lambda = (dX_d/dV_{DS})/L_{\text{eff}}$, i.e. $\lambda_n = 0.105\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$, $\lambda_p = 0.0625\ \mathrm{V^{-1}}$.
