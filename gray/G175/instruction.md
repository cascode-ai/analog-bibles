[Fig. 1] shows a scheme for biasing the class-AB output stage ($Q_{74}$, $Q_{75}$, driven through $Q_{64}$–$Q_{65}$ and $Q_{68}$ from nodes 25 and 26) of a bipolar op amp with $V_{EE} = 0$. The differential pair $Q_{39}$–$Q_{40}$ compares the voltage set by $I_{REF} = 7.4\ \mu$A in the diode-connected $Q_{37}$ (npn) and $Q_{38}$ (pnp) with the voltage at the base of $Q_{40}$, which is produced by $Q_{43}$ (pnp, base at Pbase, emitter resistor $R_{43}$), $R_{46}$ and $Q_{46}$ (pnp, base at Nbase). Its collectors drive nodes 25 and 26, closing a feedback loop through the output stage; assume this loop has high gain, so that it forces the base voltages of $Q_{39}$ and $Q_{40}$ to be equal. Ignoring all base currents, find the product $|I_{C74}|\,I_{C75}$ of the output-transistor collector currents. Relative emitter areas (saturation currents are proportional to emitter area within each transistor type): $Q_{37}$: 1, $Q_{38}$: 1, $Q_{43}$: 3, $Q_{46}$: 3 (identical to $Q_{43}$), $Q_{74}$: 32, $Q_{75}$: 10. $R_{43} = R_{46} = 1.3$ kΩ.
