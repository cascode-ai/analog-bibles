An NMOS common-gate stage is driven at its source by a signal current source $i_i$ (source resistance $R_S = \infty$); the gate is at ac ground and the drain feeds a load $R_L = 0$ (ac short circuit). The transistor is biased at $I_D = 0.5$ mA. NMOS data: $W = 100\ \mu$m, $L_{\text{eff}} = 1.6\ \mu$m, $k_n' = 60\ \mu\mathrm{A/V^2}$, $\lambda = 0$, $\gamma = 0$, $C_{gs} = \tfrac23WL_{\text{eff}}C_{ox}$ with $C_{ox} = 0.7\ \mathrm{fF/\mu m^2}$, $C_{gd} = 14$ fF, $C_{sb} = C_{db} = 0$; square-law model. Calculate an expression for the small-signal current gain $i_o/i_i$ ($i_o$ = drain signal current into the load) as a function of frequency and determine the frequency where the current gain is 3 dB below its low-frequency value.
