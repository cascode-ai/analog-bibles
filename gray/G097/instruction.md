In the hypothetical band-gap reference shown in [Fig. 1], the output voltage is $V_{OUT} = V_{BE(\text{on})} + MV_T$, where $V_{BE(\text{on})}$ is the base-emitter voltage of the diode-connected transistor biased by the current $I_1$, and $M$ is a temperature-independent constant. In such a reference $I_1$ is often made proportional to absolute temperature; assume instead that this current is derived from a diffused resistor, and thus has a temperature coefficient $TC_F = (1/I_1)(dI_1/dT) = -1500$ ppm/°C. Determine the value of $V_{OUT}$ required to achieve zero $TC_F$ of $V_{OUT}$ at 25 °C. Neglect base current.

Model: $V_{BE(\text{on})} = V_T\ln(I_1/I_S)$ with $I_S \propto T^{\gamma}\exp(-V_{G0}/V_T)$, $\gamma = 3.2$, $V_{G0} = 1.205$ V; $V_T = kT/q = 25.7$ mV at 25 °C ($T = 298$ K).
