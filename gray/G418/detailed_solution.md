# Detailed solution - G418

Treat the CMFB as a negative-feedback loop around the op amp's common-mode path. Compute three low-frequency gains in the CM half circuit: the open-loop CM gain $a_{cm}=v_{oc}/v_{ic}$, the CMC gain $a_{cmc}=v_{oc}/v_{cmc}$, and the given sense gain $a_{cms0}$. With the loop closed, $v_{oc}=a_{cm}v_{ic}+a_{cmc}a_{cms0}v_{oc}$, so

$$
a'_{cm}=\frac{a_{cm}}{1-a_{cmc}a_{cms0}}=\frac{a_{cm}}{1+T},\qquad T=-a_{cms0}a_{cmc}.
$$

### Step 1 — Small-signal parameters

With $r_o=|V_A|/|I_D|$, $g_m=2|I_D|/|V_{ov}|$, and the CM half circuit using half of the tail ($100\ \mu$A):

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| $r_{o2}=20.5\ \mathrm V/100\ \mu\mathrm A$ (PMOS) | 205 kΩ |
| $r_{o4}=41\ \mathrm V/100\ \mu\mathrm A$ (NMOS) | 410 kΩ |
| $r_{o5h}=2(20.5\ \mathrm V/200\ \mu\mathrm A)$ | 205 kΩ |
| $g_{m5h}=\tfrac12\cdot2(200\ \mu\mathrm A)/0.5\ \mathrm V$ | 0.4 mA/V |
| $r_{o6}=41\ \mathrm V/400\ \mu\mathrm A$, $r_{o7}=20.5\ \mathrm V/400\ \mu\mathrm A$ | 102.5 kΩ, 51.25 kΩ |

The second stage ($M_6$ common source, $M_7$ load) has gain magnitude

$$
|A_2|=g_{m6}(r_{o6}\parallel r_{o7})=(1.55\ \mathrm{mA/V})(34.2\ \mathrm{k\Omega})=53.0 .
$$

### Step 2 — First-stage load and open-loop CM gain

In the CM half circuit $M_2$ is degenerated by $r_{o5h}$. Its effective transconductance and the resistance looking into its drain are

$$
G_m=\frac{g_{m2}}{1+g_{m2}r_{o5h}}=\frac{1\ \mathrm{mA/V}}{206}=4.85\ \mu\mathrm{A/V},\qquad
R_{o(\mathrm{up})}=r_{o2}(1+g_{m2}r_{o5h})+r_{o5h}=42.4\ \mathrm{M\Omega}.
$$

The first-stage output node is loaded by $R_1=r_{o4}\parallel R_{o(\mathrm{up})}=406\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$. Raising $v_{ic}$ reduces the PMOS current into $M_4$, lowering the first-stage output; the second stage inverts again, so

$$
a_{cm}=+G_mR_1|A_2|=(4.85\ \mu\mathrm{A/V})(406\ \mathrm{k\Omega})(53.0)=1.97\times53.0=+104 .
$$

### Step 3 — CMC gain

$v_{cmc}$ drives the gate of the PMOS tail $M_5$. In the CM half circuit, $M_{5h}$ injects $-g_{m5h}v_{cmc}$ into the source of $M_2$, which acts as a common-gate stage and passes almost all of it to the first-stage output. Raising $v_{cmc}$ cuts the tail current, lowers the first-stage output and raises $v_{oc}$:

$$
a_{cmc}=+g_{m5h}R_1|A_2|=(0.4\ \mathrm{mA/V})(406\ \mathrm{k\Omega})(53.0)=+8600 .
$$

### Step 4 — Loop gain and closed-loop CM gain

The sense gain is negative (higher $v_{oc}$ raises $I_{cms}$ and lowers the gate of $M_{25}$, i.e. $v_{cmc}$), so the loop is negative feedback:

$$
T=-a_{cms0}a_{cmc}=-(-0.71)(8600)=6100,
\qquad
a'_{cm}=\frac{a_{cm}}{1+T}=\frac{104}{6108}=+0.017 .
$$

Since $a_{cm}/a_{cmc}=G_m/g_{m5h}\approx1/(g_{m5h}r_{o5h})$ for $g_{m2}r_{o5h}\gg1$, the result has the compact form

$$
a'_{cm}\approx\frac{a_{cm}}{T}=\frac{1}{g_{m5h}r_{o5h}|a_{cms0}|}=\frac1{(82)(0.71)}=0.017 .
$$

The CMFB loop reduces the CM gain by the factor $1+T\approx6100$, from about 104 to about 0.017.
