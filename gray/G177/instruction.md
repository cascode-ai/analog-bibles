- Base currents: ignore $\beta_F$ in the dc bias calculations except when computing revised estimates of $I_{C64}$, $|I_{C65}|$ and $I_{C68}$: include the base current of $Q_{74}$ in $|I_{C65}|$ (which carries $I_{R65}$ plus that base current), the base current of $Q_{65}$ in $I_{C64}$ (which carries $I_{C63}$ minus it), the base current of $Q_{75}$ in $I_{C68}$ (which carries $I_{C69}$ plus it), and the reduction of $I_{C62}$ (and hence $I_{C63}$) by the base currents of $Q_{62}$–$Q_{63}$.
