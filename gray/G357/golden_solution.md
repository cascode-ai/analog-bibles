# Golden Solution - G357

## Reference answer

$\overline{v^2}/\Delta f = 2\cdot4kT\frac{2}{3g_m} = 4kT(2.67\text{ k}\Omega) \approx 4.4\times10^{-17}$ V²/Hz (≈ 6.7 nV/√Hz); $\overline{i^2}/\Delta f = 2qI_G = 3.2\times10^{-35}$ A²/Hz (≈ 5.7 aA/√Hz).

- Per device $\overline{v_i^2} = 4kT\cdot\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{0.5\text{ mS}} = 4kT(1333\ \Omega)$; the pair doubles it.
- The gate shot noise is six orders of magnitude below the super-β bipolar pair's base-current noise ($3.2\times10^{-29}$ A²/Hz), while $\overline{v^2}$ is about 20 times lower than that pair's $8.8\times10^{-16}$ V²/Hz.

## Grading notes

+2: $\overline{v^2}$ ≈ $4.4\times10^{-17}$ V²/Hz (±10%) and $\overline{i^2}$ = $3.2\times10^{-35}$ A²/Hz. +1: single-device $\overline{v^2}$ ($2.2\times10^{-17}$) with the rest right, or $4kT/g_m$ without the 2/3 stated as a model choice. 0: one of the two generators right and the other wrong through a slip. −2 (asserted as fact): $\overline{i^2}$ taken from a drain-current shot noise, $\overline{v^2}$ with $4kT\,g_m$, or values off by orders of magnitude.
