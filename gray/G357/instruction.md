In the differential input stage of [Fig. 1], the bipolar transistors are replaced by MOS transistors (gate at the base terminal, source at the emitter, drain at the collector), each with transconductance $g_m = 0.5$ mA/V and dc gate leakage current $I_G = 0.1$ fA; assume $C_{gs} = 0$. The stage's noise is represented by a voltage generator $\overline{v^2}$ in series with the gate of the first transistor and current generators $\overline{i^2}$ from each gate to ground, as drawn. Neglecting flicker noise, calculate $\overline{v^2}$ and $\overline{i^2}$. Model the channel thermal noise as $\overline{i_d^2} = 4kT\frac{2}{3}g_m\Delta f$ and the gate-current shot noise as $2qI_G\Delta f$; use $4kT = 1.66\times10^{-20}$ V·C and $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.
