A bipolar transistor has $I_C = 10\ \mu$A, $\beta_F = 100$, $r_b = 300\ \Omega$. Its base-current noise contains flicker noise, $\overline{i_b^2} = 2qI_B\Delta f + K_1\frac{I_B}{f}\Delta f$, whose corner frequency (where the flicker and shot components are equal) is 1 kHz. At 500 Hz, calculate the source resistance giving minimum spot noise figure and the corresponding spot noise figure in decibels. Neglect capacitive effects. Use $4kT = 1.66\times10^{-20}$ V·C, $q = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ C and $kT/q = 26$ mV.
