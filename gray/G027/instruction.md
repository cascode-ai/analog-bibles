Calculate the transconductance of an $n$-channel MOSFET with $W = 10\ \mu$m, $\mu_n = 450$ cm²/(V·s), and critical field $\mathcal{E}_c = 1.5\times10^6$ V/m for channel lengths from 10 µm down to 0.4 µm. Assume that $t_{ox} = L/50$ ($\epsilon_{ox} = 3.45\times10^{-13}$ F/cm) and that the device operates in the active region with $V_{GS} - V_t = 0.1$ V. Model velocity saturation with the drift velocity–field relation $v_d = \mu_n\mathcal{E}/(1+\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{E}_c)$ in a gradual-channel analysis. Compare the result to a calculation that ignores velocity saturation. For what range of channel lengths is the model without velocity saturation accurate within 10 percent?
